# Mystic (Monk)

The Mystic school represents martial arts and psionic disciplines. It uses Fist Fighting skill and requires Tithing Points. Monks must wear a special robe — no armor or weapons.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Fist Fighting
- **Restrictions:** No weapons (pugilist gloves only), no armor, no shields
- **Equipment:** Mystic Monk Robe (mandatory)
- **Base Skills:** Focus 100+ and Meditation 100+
- **Resource:** Tithing Points

## How to Learn

Find Mystic Scrolls and inscribe them into a Mystic Spell Book. Create your Monk Robe first — it is required for all other abilities.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Quivering Palm | 20 | Convert damage to target's worst resistance type |
| Psionic Blast | 30 | Mental energy attack based on Fist Fighting and Intelligence |
| Gentle Touch | 30 | Heal; higher skill cures poison and mortal wounds |
| Leap | 35 | Leap long distances; cross obstacles and rivers |
| Wind Runner | 70 | Run as fast as a steed |
| Psychic Wall | 60 | Barrier that reflects magical attacks |
| Astral Travel | 50 | Teleport to a marked rune location |
| Astral Projection | 80 | Enter the astral plane, immune to harm |

## Playstyle Tips

- **The Monk Robe is unique** — only one can exist at a time. You can enchant it to add attribute points.
- **No armor means fragile** — rely on Wind Runner, Leap, and Psychic Wall for defense.
- **Quivering Palm** is devastating — it converts your fist damage to the target's weakest resistance.
- **Requires 100+ in Focus and Meditation** — invest early or this school is inaccessible.
- **Psychic Wall** is excellent against mages — reflects their spells back at them.

# Death Knight

Death Knights are dark warriors who draw power from trapped souls and negative Karma. They are the evil counterpart to Paladins.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Knightship
- **Karma:** Non-positive (<= 0)
- **Resource:** Trapped Souls (in a Soul Lantern) + Mana + Stamina
- **Spellbook:** Death Knight Spell Book

## How to Learn

Find Death Skulls containing Death Knight spell knowledge and inscribe them into a Death Knight Spell Book. Slay enemies with your Soul Lantern to collect trapped souls.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Strike | 10 | Deal demonic energy damage |
| Demonic Touch | 15 | Heal target with demonic forces |
| Lucifer's Bolt | 25 | Call down energy bolt, stun enemy |
| Wrath | 50 | Unleash hellish forces on nearby enemies |
| Hellfire | 70 | Scorches enemy with burning hellfire |
| Orb of Orcus | 80 | Barrier that reflects spells back at casters |
| Devil Pact | 90 | Summon the Devil to fight alongside you |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Collect souls actively** — the more souls in your Lantern, the more abilities you can use.
- **Hellfire** is one of the best single-target damage abilities in the game.
- **Orb of Orcus** is an excellent defensive tool against mages.
- **Casting reduces Karma** — you'll sink further into negative territory.
- **Power scales with negative Karma** — the worse your Karma, the stronger your abilities.

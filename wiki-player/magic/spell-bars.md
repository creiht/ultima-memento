# Spell Bars

Spell Bars are customizable on-screen toolbars that give you quick access to cast spells without opening your spellbook.

## How to Set Up a Spell Bar

1. **Open the setup gump** using the appropriate command for your magic school (e.g., `[mage_spell1` for Magery bar 1)
2. **Select spells** to include by checking the boxes next to each spell icon
3. **Choose layout:** Horizontal (compact row) or Vertical (stacked with optional names)
4. **Open the bar** with the corresponding `*tool*` command (e.g., `[mage_tool1`)
5. **Close the bar** with the corresponding `*close*` command

## Tips

- Arrange your most-used spells on spell bars for faster combat.
- Use vertical bars with names enabled while learning a new magic school.
- Switch to horizontal bars once you recognize spell icons to save screen space.
- You can have multiple spell bars open simultaneously (e.g., one for Magery, one for Necromancy).

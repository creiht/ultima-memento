# Syth

Syth powers are dark-side psychic abilities fueled by negative Karma, hatred, and rage. Like Jedi, they require Syth-themed equipment and a sword.

## Requirements

- **Skills:** Psychology 10+, Swords 10+, Tactics 10+
- **Karma:** Non-positive (<= 0)
- **Equipment:** Syth robe/cloak/helm/talisman + sword
- **Resource:** Hell Shard Crystals (stored in datacron)

## How to Learn

Find Syth Datacrons in dark dungeons throughout the world. Each datacron teaches one power.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Crystals | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Psychokinesis | 10 | 6 | Move objects at a distance; trigger traps safely |
| Death Grip | 20 | 16 | Deal 5-20 damage every few seconds for up to 75 seconds |
| Projection | 30 | 24 | Create a physical projection to distract foes |
| Throw Sword | 40 | 12 | Throw your sword for 17-53 damage |
| Speed | 50 | 80 | Run as fast as a horse |
| Syth Lightning | 60 | 32 | Strike multiple enemies with lightning |
| Absorption | 70 | 500 | Absorb and redirect damaging spells |
| Psychic Blast | 80 | 48 | Massive energy damage (80-125 points) |
| Drain Life | 90 | 52 | Drain HP from target; heals you |
| Clone | 100 | 250 | Create a cloning crystal for self-resurrection |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Drain Life** is one of the best sustained damage abilities — it heals you as it damages the enemy.
- **Psychic Blast** is your big damage nuke — save it for tough enemies.
- **Syth Lightning** is excellent for crowd control — hits multiple targets.
- **Casting reduces Karma** — you'll sink further into negative territory.
- **Must wear Syth gear** — even one piece (robe, cloak, helm, or talisman) is required.

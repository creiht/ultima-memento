# Magic Overview

Ultima Memento features **19 magic schools**, each with unique casting mechanics, resources, and playstyles. Some are traditional spellcasting, others are martial arts, psychic powers, or class abilities.

## General Mechanics

- Most spells require **mana** to cast.
- Many schools require a **spellbook** or special item equipped.
- Some schools (Knight, Death Knight, Holy Man) require **Tithing Points, Souls, or Piety** as additional resources.
- **Karma** gates access: Jedi and Knight require positive Karma; Syth and Death Knight require negative Karma.
- **Armor** can interfere with casting in some schools (notably Magery and Elementalism).
- **Spell Bars** provide quick-access toolbars — see [Spell Bars](spell-bars.md) for setup.

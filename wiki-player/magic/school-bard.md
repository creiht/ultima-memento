# Bard Songs

Bard Songs are magical performances that buff allies and debuff enemies. They require a musical instrument and scale with the combined value of Musicianship, Provocation, Discordance, and Peacemaking.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Musicianship
- **Additional Skills:** Provocation, Discordance, Peacemaking (increase power)
- **Instrument:** Must have a musical instrument equipped (lute, harp, etc.)
- **Spellbook:** Song Book

## How to Learn

Acquire a Song Book and obtain song scrolls to inscribe. Select an instrument before casting.

## Song Types

| Type | Effect | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Carols** | Group resistance buffs for allies | Fire Carol, Ice Carol, Poison Carol, Energy Carol |
| **Threnodies** | Debuff enemy resistances | Fire Threnody, Ice Threnody, Poison Threnody, Energy Threnody |
| **Etudes** | Boost ally stats | Enchanting Etude (Int), Sinewy Etude (Str) |
| **Ballads** | Mana regen for allies | Mage's Ballad |
| **Paeon** | HP regen for allies | Army's Paeon |
| **Other** | Damage, speed, magic dispel | Foe Requiem, Shepherd's Dance, Magic Finale |

## Key Songs

| Song | Effect |
|---|---|
| Army's Paeon | HP regen buff for nearby allies |
| Mage's Ballad | Mana regen for nearby allies |
| Foe Requiem | Damage to target enemy |
| Magic Finale | Dispel magical effects in an area |
| Shepherd's Dance | Speed boost for nearby allies |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Instruments break** after their last use — carry spares.
- **Cast Carols before fights** — boosting resistances before engaging is crucial.
- **Threnodies make your enemy weaker** — cast them before your allies attack.
- **Magic Finale** is a powerful crowd control tool in large battles.
- **Foe Requiem** is one of your few direct damage options — use it to finish off weakened enemies.

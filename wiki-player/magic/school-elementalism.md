# Elementalism

Elementalism is a complete magic system built around four elements: Air, Earth, Fire, and Water. Each element has different visuals and damage types. No reagents required — spells consume stamina.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Elementalism
- **Spellbook:** Elemental Spellbook (changes appearance based on element)
- **No Reagents:** Spells consume stamina instead
- **Armor:** Heavy armor increases fizzle chance; wear fabric/leather

## How to Learn

Obtain an Elemental Spellbook and elemental spell scrolls. Choose your element (Air, Earth, Fire, Water) — this changes all spell visuals and damage types.

## Element Damage Types

| Element | Damage Type | Best Against |
|---|---|---|
| Air | Energy | Energy-resistant foes |
| Earth | Physical/Poison | Low-physical-resistance foes |
| Fire | Fire | Low-fire-resistance foes |
| Water | Cold | Low-cold-resistance foes |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Choose your element based on common enemies** — Fire is great against creatures with low fire resistance, but watch out for fire-resistant foes.
- **Stamina is your reagent** — higher Dexterity means more stamina to burn on spells.
- **Elemental Steed** is incredibly useful — a fast mount that doesn't need food.
- **Lower Reagent Cost reduces stamina** instead of reagent cost.
- **Elemental Echo** (6th sphere) reflects spells back at casters — requires a gem matching your element.

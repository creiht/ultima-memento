# Knight (Chivalry)

The Knight school represents the holy combat abilities of paladins. It uses Knightship skill and requires positive Karma and Tithing Points.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Knightship
- **Karma:** Non-negative (>= 0)
- **Resource:** Tithing Points + Stamina
- **Spellbook:** Book of Chivalry

## How to Learn

Acquire a Book of Chivalry. Earn Tithing Points by tithing gold at shrines of Virtue.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Tithing | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close Wounds | 0 | 10 | Heal a target |
| Consecrate Weapon | 15 | 10 | Imbue weapon to deal damage in target's weakest resistance |
| Divine Fury | 25 | 10 | Boost attack speed and damage, lower defense |
| Dispel Evil | 35 | 10 | Damage nearby undead and dispel summons |
| Holy Light | 55 | 10 | Deal energy damage to all nearby enemies |
| Noble Sacrifice | 65 | 30 | Heal/cure/resurrect nearby allies at personal cost |
| Sacred Journey | 15 | 10 | Teleport to a marked rune (like Recall) |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Close Wounds** is a must-have — self-healing without potions.
- **Consecrate Weapon** adapts to your enemy — it deals damage in whichever resistance they're weakest at.
- **Holy Light** is great for crowd control in groups.
- **Noble Sacrifice** is a game-changer in tough group fights — but costs 30 Tithing.
- **Power scales with both Knightship and Karma** — keep your Karma non-negative.

# Druidism

Druidism is nature-based magic using herbal mixtures and druidic knowledge. It uses Druidism skill for casting and Veterinary skill for damage calculations.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Druidism
- **Damage Skill:** Veterinary
- **Equipment:** Druid Pouch (contains herbal mixtures)

## How to Learn

Acquire a Druid Pouch and herbal potions/mixtures. Brew herbal recipes using the Book of Druid Brewing.

## Key Spells

| Spell | Effect |
|---|---|
| Shield of Earth | Earthen shield protecting the caster |
| Herbal Healing | Heal wounds using herbal mixtures |
| Grasping Roots | Entangle and immobilize a target |
| Treefellow | Summon a treant ally to fight |
| Nature's Passage | Teleport through nature to a marked rune |
| Swarm of Insects | Summon a swarm to attack enemies |
| Volcanic Eruption | Call forth a volcanic eruption |
| Protective Fairy | Summon a fairy to protect and aid you |
| Blend with Forest | Camouflage into the natural environment |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Brew mixtures regularly** — they're your spell fuel. The Book of Druid Brewing has recipes for each.
- **Grasping Roots** is great crowd control — immobilize enemies while you or your pet finish them.
- **Treefellow** is a powerful tank — treants fight alongside you for an extended period.
- **Herbal Healing** is your primary sustain — use it between fights.
- **Power scales with Veterinary** — train it alongside Druidism.

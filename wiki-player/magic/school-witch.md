# Witch

Witch magic uses dark liquid concoctions — brews that produce spell effects when consumed or thrown. It relies on Necromancy skill and Forensics for damage.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Necromancy
- **Damage Skill:** Forensics
- **Karma:** Casting reduces Karma (must be above -2,459)
- **Equipment:** Witch Pouch (contains brews)

## How to Learn

Acquire a Witch Pouch and the Book of Witch Brewing. Brew elixirs and use them to trigger spell effects.

## Key Brews

| Brew | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hell's Brand | Brand target with infernal fire damage |
| Vampire Gift | Grant vampiric abilities temporarily |
| Blood Pact | Share damage and life force with target |
| Necro Poison | Apply deadly necromantic poison |
| Wall of Spikes | Create a wall of damaging spikes |
| Mana Leech | Drain mana from a target |
| Ghostly Images | Create ghostly duplicates to confuse enemies |
| Spectre Shadow | Move incorporeally through objects |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Brews are consumable** — craft and store them in your Witch Pouch.
- **Vampire Gift** is your sustain — temporary vampiric abilities let you tank and heal.
- **Blood Pact** is a risky but powerful tool — sharing damage can be devastating against high-HP targets.
- **Wall of Spikes** is great area denial — blocks paths and damages anyone who tries to cross.
- **Casting reduces Karma** — be prepared for notoriety changes.

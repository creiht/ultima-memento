# Research (Ancient Magic)

Research is a vast school of 64 ancient spells spanning 8 disciplines. It's the most extensive magic school, covering every aspect of magic.

## Requirements

- **Minimum-Skill Check:** Higher of Magery or Necromancy
- **Skill-Gain:** Inscription gains on successful casts
- **Method A:** Research Bag (requires prepared spell components and reagents)
- **Method B:** Ancient Spellbook (requires pages and quills)

## How to Learn

Research spells by discovering and studying ancient texts, runes, and scrolls. Use `[ancient` to toggle between bag and book casting modes.

## 8 Disciplines

| Discipline | Focus |
|---|---|
| Conjuration | Summoning and creation |
| Death | Necromantic offensive and debuff spells |
| Enchanting | Buff and enhancement spells |
| Sorcery | Elemental offensive spells |
| Summoning | Creature and elemental summoning |
| Thaumaturgy | Healing and protection |
| Theurgy | Travel, divination, utility |
| Wizardry | Area-effect and high-damage spells |

## Playstyle Tips

- **64 spells is a lot** — prioritize learning the ones that fit your playstyle first.
- **Ancient Spellbook mode** is simpler — just consume pages and quills per cast.
- **Research Bag mode** offers more control — prepare components for specific spells.
- **Lower Reagent Cost can bypass component consumption** — very valuable for this school.
- **Many spells have karmic consequences** — destructive spells reduce Karma.

# Jester

The Jester school is a unique set of comedic trick abilities powered by pranks. It uses Begging skill for casting and Psychology for damage.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Begging
- **Damage Skill:** Psychology
- **Resource:** Pranks (stored in Bag of Tricks)

## How to Learn

Become a Jester and acquire a Bag of Tricks. Pranks are accumulated through performing.

## Key Tricks

| Trick | Effect |
|---|---|
| Can of Snakes | Summons snakes to surprise and attack |
| Clowns | Summons comedic clown allies |
| Hilarity | Incapacitates a target with laughter |
| Flower Power | A surprising flower trick that debilitates foes |
| Jump Around | Leap to a different location |
| Popping Balloon | Balloon pops with an explosive effect |
| Surprise Gift | Delivers a surprise package with unexpected effects |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Jester is a unique playstyle** — abilities have comical sound effects and unpredictable outcomes.
- **Clowns** are fun allies that can tank for you.
- **Hilarity** is a powerful crowd control — incapacitate enemies without killing them.
- **Failed casts produce comical effects** — part of the charm!
- **Power scales with Begging + Psychology** — train both skills.

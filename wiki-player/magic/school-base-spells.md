# Base Spell System & Special Moves

The Base directory contains the core spell system infrastructure and the Special Move system used by weapon abilities.

## Special Moves

Weapon special moves are combat abilities tied to specific weapon types. They are activated through the combat system, not cast like spells. Check your weapon's properties to discover which special moves it supports.

## Forgetful Gem

The Forgetful Gem allows you to forget/unlearn spells from your spellbooks, freeing up capacity for new spells.

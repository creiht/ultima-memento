# Holy Man

The Holy Man channels divine blessings and prayers. It requires high positive Karma and Piety points earned by banishing evil.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Spiritualism
- **Damage Skill:** Healing
- **Karma:** At least 2,500 (positive)
- **Resource:** Piety (stored in a Holy Symbol)
- **Spellbook:** Holy Man Spell Book

## How to Learn

Acquire Holy Symbols and inscribe them into a Holy Man Spell Book. Earn piety by using your Holy Symbol to banish undead and evil creatures.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Touch of Life | 20 | Restore health and stamina |
| Sanctify | 30 | Boost Strength, speed, and Intelligence |
| Smite | 40 | Holy bolt — double damage to undead/demons |
| Banish Evil | 60 | Send demons and undead back to the hells |
| Enchant | 90 | Temporarily imbue a weapon with holy powers |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Banish Evil** is your main source of piety — hunt evil creatures to fuel your abilities.
- **Smite** is devastating against undead and demons — always carry Holy symbols when hunting these foes.
- **Sanctify** is a powerful buff before big fights — boosts multiple stats.
- **Power floor ensures usability** — even if Karma reduces your calculated power, you'll still get usable abilities.

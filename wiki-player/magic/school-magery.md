# Magery

Magery is the classic arcane magic of Ultima Memento — 8 circles of spells with reagents, covering healing, damage, utility, and summoning.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Magery
- **Spellbook:** Standard Spellbook
- **Reagents:** Each spell consumes specific reagents (or use an Arcane Gem as a substitute)

## How to Learn

Purchase or find spell scrolls and inscribe them into your spellbook. Scrolls can also be cast directly without a spellbook (minimum skill = circle - 2).

## Spell Circles

| Circle | Mana Cost | Key Spells |
|---|---|---|
| First | 4 | Heal, Magic Arrow, Clumsy, Weaken, Night Sight |
| Second | 6 | Cure, Agility, Strength, Cunning, Protection |
| Third | 9 | Fireball, Teleport, Bless, Poison, Unlock |
| Fourth | 11 | Greater Heal, Lightning, Recall, Curse, Mana Drain |
| Fifth | 14 | Paralyze, Summon Creature, Incognito, Blade Spirits |
| Sixth | 20 | Invisibility, Explosion, Energy Bolt, Mass Curse |
| Seventh | 40 | Chain Lightning, Meteor Swarm, Polymorph, Gate Travel |
| Eighth | 50 | Earthquake, Summon Daemons, Resurrection, Elemental summons |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Start with healing and utility** — Heal, Cure, and Recall are essential for any mage.
- **Arcane Gem is valuable** — it substitutes for reagents, making high-circle spells more sustainable.
- **Armor interferes with casting** — wear mage robes or lower-tier armor to avoid casting failures.
- **Meditation is essential** — paired with Intelligence, it lets you recover mana between fights.
- **Circle 7+ spells are game-changers** — Chain Lightning, Meteor Swarm, and Polymorph can turn difficult encounters.

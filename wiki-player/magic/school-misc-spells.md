# Miscellaneous Spells

Miscellaneous spells are special magical abilities triggered by items rather than traditional spellbooks. They use Magery or Necromancy skill.

## Key Spells

| Spell | Effect |
|---|---|
| Identify | Identify properties of items |
| Summon Snakes | Summon venomous snakes to fight |
| Summon Dragon | Summon a dragon ally |
| Thor Lightning | Call down a powerful bolt of lightning |
| Travel | Teleport to a destination |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Summon Dragon** is one of the most powerful spells in the game — a dragon ally can solo most encounters.
- **Identify** is useful when you find unidentified items and don't have high Mercantile or Arms Lore.
- **Thor Lightning** is a reliable damage spell when available.

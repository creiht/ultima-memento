# Shinobi

Shinobi abilities are advanced ninja techniques beyond standard Ninjitsu. They are learned from Shinobi Scrolls and use Tithing Points.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Ninjitsu
- **Resource:** Tithing Points
- **Learning:** Shinobi Scrolls (each scroll teaches one ability)

## How to Learn

Find Shinobi Scrolls in dungeons and from enemies. Each scroll teaches a specific ability permanently.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| Cheetah Paws | Run at increased speed (like a mount); restricted in some areas |
| Tiger Strength | Temporarily increase physical power |
| Eagle Eye | Enhanced perception to detect hidden things |
| Monkey Leap | Leap across distances to reach new locations |
| Deception | Disguise yourself to deceive enemies |
| Mystic Shuriken | Throw a magical shuriken at a target |
| Espionage | Spy on targets from a distance |
| Ferret Flee | Quick escape ability to flee from danger |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Learn Cheetah Paws early** — it's an invaluable mobility tool.
- **Eagle Eye** complements the Searching skill — great for dungeon exploration.
- **Combine with standard Ninjitsu** — Shinobi and Ninjitsu abilities work together for a powerful ninja build.
- **Tiger Strength** boosts your damage significantly — use it before tough fights.

# Bushido

Bushido is the way of the samurai — combat stances, powerful strikes, and defensive techniques. It uses Bushido skill and mana.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Bushido
- **Spellbook:** Book of Bushido

## How to Learn

Acquire a Book of Bushido. Abilities become available as your Bushido skill increases.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Mana | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Honorable Execution | 0 | 25 | Bonus damage on hit; kills grant HP regen |
| Confidence | 10 | 25 | Regenerate health, immune to spell interruption |
| Evasion | 10 | 60 | Chance to parry incoming attacks with your weapon |
| Counter Attack | 5 | 40 | Auto-counter the next enemy that strikes you |
| Lightning Strike | 5 | 50 | Next attack has greatly increased hit chance |
| Momentum Strike | 10 | 70 | Kill one foe, chain-strike a nearby enemy |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Only one stance active at a time** — choose based on the fight (Evasion for defense, Confidence for sustain, Counter Attack for aggressive play).
- **Honorable Execution is free** — always have it active before engaging.
- **Momentum Strike is devastating in groups** — chain kills cascade.
- **Evasion is powerful at high skill** — it can turn you into a near-untouchable fighter.

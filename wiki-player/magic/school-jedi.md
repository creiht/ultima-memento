# Jedi

Jedi powers are light-side psychic abilities fueled by positive Karma, Psychology and Swords skills, and Karan Crystals. You must wear Jedi-themed clothing and wield a sword.

## Requirements

- **Skills:** Psychology 10+, Swords 10+, Tactics 10+
- **Karma:** Positive (>= 0)
- **Equipment:** Jedi robe/cloak/helm/talisman + sword
- **Resource:** Karan Crystals (stored in Jedi datacron)

## How to Learn

Find Jedi Datacrons in dungeons throughout the world. Each datacron teaches one ability.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Crystals | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soothing Touch | 10 | 48 | Heal self or others; cure poison and mortal wounds at high skill |
| Force Grip | 10 | 6 | Move objects at a distance; trigger traps safely |
| Mind's Eye | 20 | 16 | Detect hidden creatures and traps |
| Mirage | 30 | 24 | Create a physical projection to distract foes |
| Throw Sabre | 40 | 12 | Throw your sword for 17-53 damage |
| Celerity | 50 | 80 | Run as fast as a horse |
| Psychic Aura | 20 | 32 | Boost physical and energy resistance |
| Deflection | 70 | 500 | Deflect damaging spells back at the caster |
| Stasis Field | 50 | 52 | Freeze a target, unable to act |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Carry Karan Crystals** — they're consumed on use and can be hard to find in large quantities.
- **Soothing Touch** makes you an excellent healer — pair with a combat school for a support build.
- **Celerity** is invaluable for exploration — but doesn't work in dungeons/caves.
- **Deflection** is your ultimate defense — 500 crystals is a big investment but worth it against mages.
- **Must wear Jedi gear** — even one piece (robe, cloak, helm, or talisman) is required.

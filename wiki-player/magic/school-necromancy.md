# Necromancy

Necromancy is the dark art of death, curses, and undead control. It draws power from souls and reduces Karma when cast.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Necromancy
- **Damage Skill:** Spiritualism
- **Spellbook:** Necromancer Spellbook
- **Reagents:** Required (or Arcane Gem charges)

## How to Learn

Find necromancy spell scrolls in crypts and undead areas, then inscribe them into a Necromancer Spellbook.

## Key Spells

| Spell | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Animate Dead | 40 | Raise a corpse as an undead servant |
| Curse Weapon | 0 | Imbue weapon with life-draining power |
| Pain Spike | 20 | Deal direct damage to target |
| Lich Form | 70 | Transform into a lich (mana regen, cold resistance) |
| Wraith Form | 20 | Transform into a wraith (mana leech on melee) |
| Summon Familiar | 30 | Summon a companion creature |
| Vampiric Embrace | 99 | Transform into a vampire |
| Vengeful Spirit | 80 | Summon a revenant to attack a target |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Animate Dead** is one of the most powerful spells — undead servants tank for you.
- **Lich Form** and **Vampiric Embrace** are transformation spells — only one can be active at a time.
- **Curse Weapon** is cheap and powerful — apply it before every fight.
- **Casting reduces Karma** — be prepared for notoriety changes.
- **Combine with Spiritualism** — higher Spiritualism means more damage from necromancy spells.

# Ninjitsu

Ninjitsu encompasses stealth, shapeshifting, deception, and lethal precision strikes. It uses Ninjitsu skill.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Ninjitsu
- **Spellbook:** Book of Ninjitsu

## How to Learn

Acquire a Book of Ninjitsu. Abilities become available as your Ninjitsu skill increases.

## Key Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Animal Form | 10 | Transform into an animal with unique bonuses |
| Backstab | 40 | Extra damage when attacking from hiding |
| Mirror Image | 20 | Create mirror copies to absorb attacks |
| Shadow Jump | 50 | Teleport while remaining hidden |
| Surprise Attack | 60 | Next attack from stealth reduces target defense |
| Focus Attack | 30 | Next attack deals bonus damage |
| Death Strike | 85 | Delayed lethal strike that triggers when target acts |
| Ki Attack | 80 | Charging attack with bonus damage based on distance |

## Playstyle Tips

- **Combine with Hiding/Stealth** — Backstab and Surprise Attack are devastating from stealth.
- **Animal Form has no mana cost** — always active when needed.
- **Shadow Jump** is the best mobility spell for ninja — reposition and stay hidden.
- **Death Strike** is a finisher — apply it to a target and let it trigger.
- **Mirror Image** is essential for survivability when fighting multiple enemies.

# Potions

Potions are consumable items that provide temporary buffs or healing effects. They are brewed primarily through the Alchemy and Cooking crafting professions.

## Common Potion Types

| Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Heal Potion | Restore hit points |
| Mana Potion | Restore mana |
| Stamina Potion | Restore stamina |
| Greater Heal Potion | Restore more hit points than regular heal potion |
| Greater Mana Potion | Restore more mana than regular mana potion |
| Explosive Potion | Deals damage to a target area |
| Clobbering Potion | Increases damage output temporarily |
| Endurance Potion | Increases stamina recovery temporarily |
| Lesser Heal Potion | Small HP restore (early game) |

## Brewing Potions

- **Alchemy skill** is required to brew most potions.
- **Reagents** are consumed during brewing — collect and stock up on common reagents.
- Higher Alchemy skill increases the chance of brewing stronger potions (Greater versions).
- Potions can be brewed in bulk and stored for later use.

## Using Potions

- Double-click a potion in your backpack to drink it.
- Some potions can be targeted at other players or creatures.
- Potions have a weight — carry only what you need for your adventures.
- Some potions can be used in combat, others require a moment to take effect.

## Tips

- **Always carry heal potions** — they're the fastest way to recover in combat.
- **Mana potions for mages** — never run out of mana mid-fight.
- **Brew in bulk** — it's more efficient to make 20 potions at once than 1.
- **Experiment** — different potion combinations can produce different effects.
- **Higher Alchemy = better potions** — investing in Alchemy skill pays off in potion quality.

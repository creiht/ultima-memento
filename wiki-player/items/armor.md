# Armor

Armor protects you from incoming damage. Each piece covers a specific body slot and provides a resistance bonus.

## Armor Slots

| Slot | Equipment |
|---|---|
| Helm | Hats, caps, helmets |
| Hair | Hair bundles |
| Beard | Facial hair |
| Necklace | Necklaces, talismans |
| Ring | Rings (2 slots) |
| Gloves | Hand coverings |
| Cloak | Cloaks, capes |
| Chest | Shirts, chest armor |
| Arms | Sleeves, arm armor |
| Legs | Skirts, leg armor |
| Boots | Foot coverings |
| Inner Torso | Chest armor (inner layer) |

## Armor Materials

Armor is crafted from different materials, each with different properties:

| Material | Max Resist Bonus | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Leather | Standard | Light armor, low weight |
| Arcane Armor | Special | Magic-focused armor |
| Varied Materials | Varies | Each material has unique resistance profiles |

Higher-quality materials provide better resistance bonuses but may weigh more.

## Weight and Carry Capacity

Armor adds weight. Your **Strength** determines your carry weight limit. If you carry too much:
- Exceeding by 4+ stones drains stamina while moving
- If stamina hits zero, you cannot move

## Identification

Armor found in the world is often unidentified. Use **Arms Lore** or **Mercantile** skill to reveal its properties before wearing or crafting.

## Tips

- **Balance resistance types** — no armor naturally maximizes all five resistances. Mix pieces to cover your weaknesses.
- **Watch your weight** — heavy armor reduces your carry capacity.
- **Craft your own** — many armor materials are craftable. Using higher-tier materials gives better stats.
- **Magery interference** — heavy armor can cause spell fizzling in some magic schools (notably Magery and Elementalism).

# Relics

Relics are collectible dungeon items worth gold. There are 28 different relic types in the world, each with unique appearances and values.

## What Are Relics?

Relics are special items found primarily through **Grave Robbing** and in dungeons throughout the world. They have no combat or crafting use — their value is purely in their gold worth when sold.

## How to Find Relics

- **Grave Robbing** — dig graves in cemeteries and burial grounds
- **Dungeon exploration** — check chests, pedestals, and containers in dungeons
- **Loot drops** — some creatures carry relics
- **Shipwrecks** — rare relics can be found near shipwreck areas

## Identifying Relics

Relics may be unidentified when first found. Use the **Mercantile**, **Arms Lore**, or **Tasting** skills to identify them and see their gold value. Unidentified relics should always be kept — you won't know if you've found a valuable one.

## Relic Value

- Different relic types have different gold values
- Rare relics are worth significantly more than common ones
- Some relic sets or collections may have special significance
- Relic value is independent of the relic type — check identification for actual value

## Tips

- **Never discard unidentified relics** — you won't know their value.
- **Grave Robbing is the best source** — it's a dedicated relic-finding skill.
- **Carry extra backpack space** — relics can be heavy, especially in bulk.
- **Sell to merchants** — identify and sell relics for gold to fund your adventures.
- **Collecting is a hobby** — some players enjoy collecting all 28 relic types.

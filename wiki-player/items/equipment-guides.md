# Equipment Guide

Quick tips on choosing weapons, armor, and building a balanced loadout.

## Weapon Selection

**General rule:** Match your weapon to your primary combat skill.

| Playstyle | Recommended Weapon | Supporting Skills |
|---|---|---|
| Melee DPS | Sword or Axe | Swordsmanship, Tactics |
| Tank/Blocker | Mace + Shield | Bludgeoning, Parrying |
| Archer | Bow or Crossbow | Marksmanship, Tactics |
| Spellcaster | No weapon needed | Magery, Meditation |
| Hybrid | Spear | Fencing, Tactics |
| Monk | Pugilist Gloves | Fist Fighting, Mystic magic |

## Armor Selection

**General rule:** Maximize resistance coverage while managing weight.

- **Mix materials** — no single material maximizes all 5 resistances. Combine pieces to cover weaknesses.
- **Watch your STR** — heavy armor reduces carry capacity. Don't exceed your Strength limit.
- **Mage armor** — if you cast spells, wear lighter armor to avoid fizzling.
- **Craft your own** — using higher-tier materials gives better stats.

## Stat Priorities by Build

| Build | Primary Stat | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Melee Fighter | Strength | Dexterity |
| Archer | Dexterity | Strength |
| Mage | Intelligence | Dexterity |
| Healer | Intelligence | Strength |
| Hybrid | Balanced | Depends on playstyle |

## Essential Items to Carry

- **Bandages** — self-healing in combat
- **Heal potions** — faster than bandages for major damage
- **Mana potions** — for spellcasters
- **Light source** — dungeons are dark
- **Food and water** — you'll need it on long adventures
- **Tool kit** — repair tools for your gear

## Tips

- **Tactics is universally useful** — boost it regardless of build.
- **Don't hoard gear** — equip what improves your current playstyle.
- **Test before committing** — try different weapon/armor combos before crafting high-tier versions.
- **Resistances matter most** — aim for 70% in each resistance category as your end goal.

# Weapons

Weapons determine your primary damage output. Each weapon type has different speed, damage range, and special abilities.

## Weapon Types

| Type | Examples | Primary Skill | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swords | Cleaver, Cutlass, Bokuto | Swordsmanship | General melee, balanced stats |
| Axes | Battle Axe, Double Axe | Swordsmanship | High damage, slower speed |
| Maces | Mace, Hammer | Bludgeoning | Armor penetration |
| Spears | Spear, Kryss, Sai | Fencing | Reach, piercing damage |
| Clubs | Club, Flail | Bludgeoning | Blunt damage, stunning |
| Bows | Short Bow, Long Bow | Marksmanship | Ranged, keep distance |
| Crossbows | Hand Crossbow, Heavy Crossbow | Marksmanship | Ranged, high damage |
| Thrown | Throwing Knives, Boots | Marksmanship | Quick ranged attacks |
| Unarmed | Pugilist Gloves | Fist Fighting | Monk builds |

## Weapon Properties

Weapons have several key properties:
- **Speed** — how fast the weapon swings (3 speed categories: Fast, Average, Slow)
- **Damage range** — minimum to maximum damage
- **STR requirement** — minimum Strength needed to use effectively
- **Special attacks** — each weapon has unique abilities accessible through the Special Attack menu
- **Durability** — weapons degrade with use and need repair

## Weapon Skills

| Skill | Weapon Types |
|---|---|
| Swordsmanship | All swords, some axes |
| Bludgeoning | Clubs, maces, blunt weapons |
| Fencing | Spears, kryss, sai, piercing weapons |
| Fist Fighting | Unarmed combat, pugilist gloves |
| Marksmanship | Bows, crossbows, thrown weapons |

## Tips

- **Match weapon to your skill** — use the weapon that matches your primary combat skill.
- **Tactics boosts everything** — higher Tactics increases special attack power and damage.
- **Special attacks matter** — each weapon has different abilities. Check the menu before picking up a new weapon.
- **Repair regularly** — damaged weapons deal less damage and can break.
- **Carry ammo** — ranged builds need arrows/bolts. They're consumed on use.

# Artifacts

Artifacts are unique, named items with fixed stats. They are among the best gear you can find or craft in Ultima Memento.

## Types of Artifacts

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Legendary Items | Player-forged, blessed, leveling items from the Hall of Legends — gain attribute points as you kill creatures |
| Unique Named Items | One-of-a-kind items with special abilities |
| Quest Rewards | Items earned from completing difficult quests |
| Champion Rewards | Drops from defeating champion bosses |

## Legendary Items

Legendary items are a special category that level up as you use them. They start with base stats and improve over time:

- **Kill creatures** while wearing a legendary item to gain experience for it.
- **Gain attribute points** — as the item levels up, you earn bonus stats.
- **Blessed** — legendary items cannot be lost on death.
- **Unique abilities** — many have special powers not found on regular gear.

## Finding Artifacts

- **Dungeon exploration** — rare artifacts spawn in powerful dungeons.
- **Quest rewards** — complete major quest chains for artifact-level gear.
- **Crafting** — some artifacts are crafted through special professions (Lapidary, Draconic).
- **Champion spawns** — defeating champion bosses can yield artifact drops.
- **Trading** — other players may trade artifacts.

## Tips

- **Legendary items take time** — don't expect instant power; they grow with you.
- **Prioritize your favorites** — you can't level all legendary items at once.
- **Wear them in combat** — you only gain experience when killing creatures while equipped.
- **Check item properties** — some artifacts have unique effects you should know about.

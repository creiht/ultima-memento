# Death

When your HP reaches zero, you die. Death has consequences, but there are ways to recover.

## What Happens on Death

1. **You drop to a ghost state** — your body remains at the location of death with all your items.
2. **Your items are left behind** — anything you were carrying or wearing stays at your corpse.
3. **Directional arrows appear** — these point you toward the nearest healer or shrine.
4. **You lose some stats** — your Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence are reduced on return. The amount lost depends on various factors.
5. **Skills may decrease** — some of your skill points can be lost.

## Resurrection

You have two options after death:

1. **Healer** — Visit the nearest town and talk to a healer NPC to resurrect with full items restored. Some stat loss occurs.
2. **Shrine** — Find a shrine and interact with it to resurrect. Shrines are found throughout the world and in certain dungeons.

## Stat Loss Recovery

Lost stats and skill points recover over time through normal gameplay. The recovery process is passive — just playing normally will restore your lost points.

## Preventing Death

- **Carry bandages** — the Healing skill with bandages can recover significant HP in combat.
- **Carry heal potions** — a quick way to restore health without using the Healing skill.
- **Know your resistances** — equip items that boost resistances to reduce incoming damage.
- **Fight smarter** — use tactics, hide, and avoid overextending in dangerous areas.
- **Use the Focus skill** — boosts stamina regen for better sustain in combat.

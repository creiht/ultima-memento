# New Player Guide

Welcome to Ultima Memento, a custom Ultima Online server with 19 magic systems, 22 crafting professions, dozens of quest types, and a vast multi-facet world to explore.

## Character Creation

When you create a new character, you will:

1. **Choose a name** — 2–16 characters, must be unique.
2. **Distribute starting stats** — 90 points across Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence (10 minimum, 60 maximum each).
3. **Pick a starter template or allocate custom skills** — choose a preset profession or hand-build your skills with 100 points.
4. **Customize your appearance** — gender, skin hue, hair style, hair color, facial hair.

Your character starts in **Sosaria** with starter equipment: a weapon, a light source, food, water, some gold, and a backpack.

## The Avatar System

The Avatar system tracks your lifetime progress across all characters. You earn coins by killing creatures and completing combat quests. Spend coins in your Avatar Book on permanent upgrades:

- **Stat cap increases** — raise your max stat sum from 250 up to 400
- **Skill cap increases** — raise your max skill total by 10 points per level
- **Skill gain rate boosts** — train skills faster
- **Coin gain rate boosts** — earn coins faster
- **Unlockable content** — monster races, temptations, and more

Each character also gets a **Rival Faction** — a random creature type that gives you bonus coins when defeated.

## First Steps

1. **Open your Avatar Book** — it's in your backpack. This is your progression hub.
2. **Explore the starting area** — it's relatively safe. Talk to NPCs and find your bearings.
3. **Eat and drink** — your character gets hungry and thirsty. You won't need it in banks, inns, or houses.
4. **Keep your light source** — dungeons are dark.
5. **Learn basic healing** — use bandages and the Healing skill to recover from combat.
6. **Visit a healer** — if you die, a directional arrow shows you where the nearest healer or shrine is.
7. **Consider joining a guild** — costs 2,000 gold base. Guild members get crafting enhancements.

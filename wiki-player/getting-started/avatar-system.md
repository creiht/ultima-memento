# Avatar System

The Avatar system tracks your lifetime progress across all characters on your account. Even if you play a new character after your old one dies, your Avatar upgrades persist.

## How It Works

You earn **coins** through:
- Killing creatures
- Completing combat-related quests

Coins are stored in your Avatar Book, which you'll find in your backpack. Open it anytime to spend coins on permanent upgrades.

## Avatar Upgrades

| Upgrade | What it Does | Max Levels |
|---|---|---|
| Stat Cap Increase | Raise your max stat sum (Str + Dex + Int) | 150 levels (1 pt each) |
| Skill Cap Increase | Raise your max total skill points | 70 levels (10 pts each) |
| Skill Gain Rate Boost | Train skills faster | Multiple levels |
| Coin Gain Rate Boost | Earn coins faster | Multiple levels |

### Starting Values

- **Stat cap:** 250
- **After activating Avatar:** stat cap resets to 100 (buy levels to restore it)
- **Skill cap:** starts at 3000, increases with purchases

## Rival Faction

Each character is assigned a random **Rival Faction** — a specific creature type. You earn 50% bonus coins when killing rivals. Your rival is shown in the Avatar Book and is tracked until fulfilled.

## Unlocks

Certain Avatar levels unlock additional content:
- **Monster races** — play as non-human character types (trolls, ogres, giants, etc.)
- **Temptations** — risk/reward modifiers that grant powerful benefits at a cost
- **Fugitive mode** — a gameplay modifier that changes how the world reacts to you

## Tips

- **Spend coins wisely** — stat and skill cap upgrades give the biggest long-term benefit.
- **Check your Avatar Book regularly** — upgrades are always available.
- **Use your rival** — hunt rival creatures whenever possible for bonus coins.
- **Plan your stat distribution** — knowing your endgame stat cap helps you build better.

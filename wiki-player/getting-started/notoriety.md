# Notoriety

Notoriety is the game's reputation and karma system. It determines how creatures and NPCs react to you. You'll see notoriety as colored names and indicators over characters in the world.

## Notoriety Colors

| Color | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| White | Innocent | Most NPCs and creatures will not attack you |
| Green | Benevolent | Positive karma; some creatures respect you |
| Blue | Just | Neutral to slightly positive |
| Grey | Criminal | You've committed crimes; guards may attack you |
| Red | Killer | You've killed other players or innocents; many will attack you |

## Karma

Karma is the underlying value that determines your notoriety color. Your karma changes based on your actions:

- **Gaining karma:** Helping NPCs, defeating creatures aligned against you, performing good deeds
- **Losing karma:** Killing innocent NPCs, attacking peaceful creatures, stealing from merchants

## How It Affects Gameplay

- **Creature behavior:** Creatures are more likely to attack you if you have red or grey notoriety. Creatures with opposing alignment will target you more aggressively.
- **NPC services:** Merchants and service NPCs may refuse to deal with you if your notoriety is too low.
- **Guard response:** Guards in towns will attack characters with criminal or killer notoriety.
- **Loot penalties:** Some systems reduce loot quality based on your karma.

## Tips

- **Keep your notoriety green or white** if you want to safely use town services and avoid unwanted guard attacks.
- **Use notoriety to your advantage** — creatures with opposing karma will engage you more readily, which can be useful for farming.
- **Watch the color indicators** over other characters to gauge whether they're likely to attack you.

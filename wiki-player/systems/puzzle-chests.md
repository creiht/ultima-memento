# Puzzle Chests

Puzzle chests are interactive treasure containers that require solving a color-coded cylinder puzzle to unlock.

## How It Works

1. **Find a puzzle chest** — they appear in dungeons and special areas throughout the world.
2. **Interact with the chest** — a puzzle gump opens showing colored cylinders.
3. **Align the colors** — rotate the cylinders until the matching colors line up.
4. **Solve it** — once all cylinders are aligned correctly, the chest opens.
5. **Collect loot** — puzzle chests contain valuable treasure.

## Tips

- **Take your time** — puzzle chests aren't time-limited, so there's no rush.
- **Look for patterns** — the solution often follows a recognizable color pattern.
- **Bring friends** — puzzle chests can be challenging for solo players.
- **Check all chests** — puzzle chests are hidden in many locations; explore thoroughly.

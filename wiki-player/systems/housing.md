# Housing

Player housing in Ultima Memento lets you buy, place, and decorate a personal home. Houses provide storage, vendor space, and a canvas for decoration.

## Getting Started

1. **Choose your house** — talk to an Architect NPC or use the Housing placement tool (item 0x14F0). You can preview the house and see costs before committing.
2. **Find a valid spot** — the land must be flat, and at least 1 yard tile around the house must be clear. Houses can't overlap existing structures, roads, or towns.
3. **Place the house** — select your house from the gump, confirm, and the build cost is withdrawn from your bank.
4. **Manage your house** — double-click the House Sign near the front door to open the management gump.

## House Management

From the House Sign, you can:

- **Set the house name**
- **Add/remove Co-Owners, Friends, and Access/Ban lists**
- **Toggle Public/Private**
- **Manage vendors** — set up player vendors in your house

## Lockdowns and Secured Containers

- **"I wish to lock this down"** — while inside your house, say this and click an item. Locked items can't be moved by non-owners.
- **"I wish to secure this"** — secure a container with role-based access (Owner, Co-Owner, Friends, Anyone, Guild).

## House Types

- **Classic houses** — pre-built structures of various sizes
- **2-story foundations** — custom multi-story homes
- **3-story foundations** — largest custom homes
- **Tents** — simple temporary dwellings
- **Magic Carpets** — flying carpet vehicles

## Additional Features

- **Lawn system** — decorate the exterior with plants and outdoor items
- **Shanty remodeling** — add walls, doors, and tiles to your house
- **Basements** — public basements for shared housing (when enabled)
- **Teleporters** — set up home-to-home travel
- **Deeds** — transfer, resize, or advertise your house

## Ownership

- **Max 2 houses per account** (by default)
- **Houses do not decay** on this server — once placed, your house is permanent.
- **Co-Owners** — share full management access with trusted players.

## Tips

- **Lock down your valuables** — always lock down containers with rare items.
- **Set up a vendor** — houses can hold player vendors for earning gold.
- **Decorate lawns** — outdoor decoration adds personality to your home.
- **Place in safe areas** — houses in dangerous areas may be harder to defend.

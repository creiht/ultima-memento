# Achievements

Achievements (also called **Feats**) track your progress across many activities. Completing them awards achievement points and sometimes item rewards.

## Viewing Achievements

- Type **`[feats`** or **`[achievements`** in chat to open the Achievements gump.
- You can view your own achievements or target another player to see theirs.

## Achievement Categories

### Exploration
Discover new lands, towns, dungeons, and points of interest across all facets.

### Hunting
Kill specific creature types. Tracked automatically — just land the killing blow.

### Resource Gathering
Mine ore, chop wood, fish, and dig graves. Milestones at 1, 100, 1,000, 10,000, and 100,000.

### Crafting
Craft items through any of the trade skills (Alchemy, Blacksmithy, Tailoring, Cooking, etc.).

### Character Development
Reach skill milestones as you train your character.

### Feats of Strength
Special one-time accomplishments like defeating named bosses (Exodus, Jormungandr, etc.).

## Tips

- **Track your progress** — check Achievements periodically to see what milestones you're close to.
- **Hunting achievements** — focus on specific creature types to complete these faster.
- **Explore all facets** — discovery achievements reward thorough world exploration.
- **Crafting achievements** — any crafted item counts, so focus on the skill you enjoy most.

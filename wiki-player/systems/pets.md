# Pets & Companions

Ultima Memento has multiple companion systems — tamed pets, leveled pets, henchmen, and familiars.

## Taming Pets

Use the **Taming** skill on wild creatures to add them to your stable. Each pet requires food and can be healed with the **Veterinary** skill.

### Pet Management

- **Stables** — you get extra stabled pet slots (default 5). Stabled pets don't need food.
- **Bonding** — tamed pets take time to bond (default 7 days). Until bonded, they fight less effectively.
- **Feeding** — different creatures eat different things. Check what your pet needs.
- **Follower slots** — your followers limit (based on skills) determines how many active pets you can have.

## Pet Leveling (Jako System)

Tamed creatures earn **trait points** through combat. Spend them to permanently improve your pet:

| Upgrade | Trait Points | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Hit Points | 1 | +10 HP (cap: 550) |
| Stamina | 1 | +15 Stamina (cap: 750) |
| Mana | 1 | +10 Mana (cap: 550) |
| Each Resistance | 2 | +1 to that resistance (cap: 65) |

### Tips

- **HP upgrades are most efficient** — 1 point for +10 HP.
- **Stronger base pets benefit more** — the scaling system means high-base creatures get more out of leveling.
- **Resistances cost more** — 2 trait points per +1 resistance.

## Henchmen

Henchmen are hired adventuring companions paid with gold. Unlike pets, they are not tamed — they are purchased as deed items.

| Type | Buy Price |
|---|---|
| Fighter | 5,000 gp |
| Archer | 6,000 gp |
| Wizard | 7,000 gp |

- **Max 2 active henchmen** at a time.
- **Hire from inns/taverns** or recruit from Prisoner quests.
- **Pay them treasure** to keep their morale up.
- **Deed can be transferred** — give the deed to another player.

## Familiars

Three distinct familiar systems:

1. **Necromancy Summon Familiar** — cast the Summon Familiar spell to bind a creature for up to 24 hours.
2. **Crystal Ball of Summoning** — persistent pack-animal familiar (survives world restarts).
3. **Hoard Minion Familiar** — consumable item that grants a temporary pack-animal.

## Tips

- **Balance your companions** — a tank pet + healer pet + henchman is a solid group.
- **Level your pets** — trait points make a huge difference in tough dungeons.
- **Keep extra deed items** — henchmen deeds can be sold or traded.
- **Use stables wisely** — stabled pets don't consume food but take up stable slots.

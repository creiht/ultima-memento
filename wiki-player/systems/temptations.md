# Temptations

Temptations are **permanent, irreversible gameplay modifiers** that grant powerful benefits at the cost of significant drawbacks. Once you accept one, it can never be removed.

## How to Access

- **Command:** `[temptations`
- **Location:** Must be in the Gypsy Forest (outside, the gump is read-only)
- **Requirement:** Must unlock "Power Overwhelming" in the Avatar Shop first

## Available Temptations

| Temptation | Benefit | Drawback |
|---|---|---|
| **Puzzle Master** | Learn how to solve puzzle boxes | Reduced skill cap bonuses for other unlocks |
| **I Can Take It** | Deal 10% more damage | Take 20% more damage |
| **Strongest Avenger** | Learn how to wear pants | Enemies cast spells faster |
| **Deathwish** | Faster stat/skill gain; receive an old sword | **Permanent death** — no resurrection ever |

## Important Notes

- **Permanent** — once accepted, temptations cannot be reversed. Choose carefully.
- **Multiple selections** — you can select more than one temptation.
- **Deathwish** is the most extreme — it enables permadeath. Your character cannot be resurrected by any means.
- **Viewing others** — target another player and type `[temptations` to see their choices (read-only).

## Tips

- **Think long-term** — these are permanent changes to how you play.
- **I Can Take It** is great for aggressive players who want more damage.
- **Deathwish** is only for players who want a true challenge — no coming back from death.
- **Stack temptations carefully** — some drawbacks may compound in unexpected ways.

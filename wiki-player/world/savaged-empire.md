# Savaged Empire

A primal jungle land with tribal villages and monster-filled ruins. The Savaged Empire is a dangerous wilderness area with unique resources.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Map Size | 1280 x 4096 tiles |
| Description | Primal jungle with tribal content |

## Features

- **Tribal villages** — settlements with unique NPCs and quests
- **Monster-filled ruins** — ancient ruins overrun by creatures
- **Steel ore** — a premium crafting material available only here
- **Jungle environment** — unique terrain and creature types

## Tips

- **Bring steel ore** — mine it here and bring it back for crafting high-tier weapons and armor.
- **Jungle terrain** — watch for ambushes and environmental hazards.
- **Tribal quests** — unique quest line not available on other facets.

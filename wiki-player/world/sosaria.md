# Sosaria

Sosaria is the primary land and starting area of Ultima Memento. It's the largest continent and home to most of the game's towns, dungeons, and resources.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting Location | Gypsy Forest |
| Map Size | 7168 x 4096 tiles |
| Description | Largest continent, most settlements and dungeons |

## Towns & Settlements

Sosaria contains the most towns of any facet, including major cities like Britain and Montor, as well as smaller villages and settlements. These towns provide:

- **Merchants** — buy and sell goods
- **Healers** — resurrect after death
- **Quest givers** — NPC quests with rewards
- **Guild halls** — join crafting or adventure guilds
- **Inns** — safe rest spots

## Dungeons

Sosaria has the highest concentration of dungeons, ranging from easy to deadly. Dungeons contain:
- **Loot and relics** — valuable items to find
- **Quest items** — journals, cubes, schematics
- **Special creatures** — unique spawns and champion bosses
- **Rare materials** — premium ores and resources

## Sub-Regions

Sosaria contains several special sub-regions:
- **Umber Veil** — dark island chain with Brass ore
- **Kuldar** — bottle world with sci-fi content
- **Ambrosia** — lost continent with unique dungeons

## Tips

- **Explore all the towns** — each has unique NPCs and quests.
- **Map the dungeons** —Sosaria has the most dungeons but also the best organization for navigation.
- **Visit Ambrosia** when you're ready for harder content.
- **Check Kuldar** for sci-fi items and alien-themed loot.

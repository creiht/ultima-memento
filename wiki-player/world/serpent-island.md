# Serpent Island

A dangerous volcanic island chain with themed dungeons of vice and virtue. The island is known for its obsidian ore and unique serpent-themed quests.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Map Size | 2560 x 2048 tiles |
| Description | Volcanic island chain |

## Features

- **Obsidian ore** — a premium crafting material available only on Serpent Island
- **Vice and virtue dungeons** — themed dungeon pairs with contrasting environments
- **Serpent quests** — unique quest line involving balance between Order and Chaos
- **Volcanic terrain** — fire-themed hazards and creatures

## Tips

- **Mine obsidian ore** — bring it back for crafting high-tier weapons and armor.
- **Resist fire damage** — volcanic terrain means lots of fire-based enemies.
- **Serpent quests** — balance is key; completing the quest line is rewarding.
- **Pair dungeons** — vice and virtue dungeons are thematically linked; explore both.

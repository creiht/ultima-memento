# Ledoria

A vast classic UO-style continent featuring traditional dungeons and many settlements. Ledoria is the go-to facet for exploration and classic Ultima Online gameplay.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Map Size | 7168 x 4096 tiles |
| Description | Classic UO-style continent |

## Features

- **Many settlements** — towns with merchants, healers, and quest givers
- **Classic dungeons** — traditional UO-style dungeon design
- **Skara Brae** — a quest-driven area with special entry requirements
- **Temple of Osirus** — accessible via Lodoria, leads to Isles of Dread dungeons

## Tips

- **Explore the towns** — Ledoria has a classic UO feel with many settlements.
- **Skara Brae** — check for quest requirements before entering.
- **Good for medium-level content** — dungeons are well-balanced for players who've outgrown Sosaria but aren't ready for the Underworld.

# Underworld

A dark subterranean realm of infernos, abysses, and ancient sky ships. The Underworld is the most challenging facet, reserved for experienced players.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Map Size | 2304 x 1600 tiles |
| Description | Dark underground realm |

## Features

- **Mithril ore** — available on the main Underworld map (Sosaria/Lodoria/Isles/Serpent sub-regions)
- **Xormite ore** — available on the Underworld Savaged Empire map
- **Petrified wood** — premium wood type found on the Underworld map
- **Ancient sky ships** — floating vessels with sci-fi loot
- **Infernos and abysses** — fire and void-themed environments
- **High-level dungeons** — the toughest content in the game

## Tips

- **Only visit when prepared** — the Underworld has the highest-level creatures and toughest dungeons.
- **Bring cold resistance** — infernos mean lots of fire damage.
- **Mithril ore is valuable** — one of the best crafting materials in the game.
- **Sky ships** — check the ancient sky ships for sci-fi loot and unique items.
- **Go with a group** — Underworld content is best tackled with other players.

# Isles of Dread

Remote and perilous islands featuring icy fortresses, blood temples, and pirate themes. The Isles offer a unique bonus: boosted harvest yields for all resources.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Map Size | 1448 x 1448 tiles |
| Season | Spring/Summer |

## Towns

- **Cimmeran Hold** — a fortified settlement and the main hub

## Dungeons

- **Blood Temple** — dark ritual site
- **Glacial Scar** — ice-themed dungeon
- **Ice Queen Fortress** — frozen fortress
- **Scurvy Reef** — pirate-themed dungeon
- **Sanctum of Saltmarsh** — accessible via Lodoria
- **Temple of Osirus** — accessible via Lodoria

## Harvest Bonus

The Isles of Dread have a **boosted harvest yield** — roughly 50%+ more resources per action compared to other lands. This applies to:
- Mining (ore)
- Lumberjacking (wood)
- Fishing
- Sand digging

## Tips

- **Bring mining/lumber tools** — the yield bonus makes this the best place to gather resources in bulk.
- **Ice-themed dungeons** — resist cold damage for easier clearing.
- **Pirate dungeons** — expect high-level humanoids and demons.

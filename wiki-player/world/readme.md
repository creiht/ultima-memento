# World Overview

The world of Ultima Memento spans multiple continents (facets) connected by moongates, ships, and magical portals. Each facet has its own settlements, dungeons, creatures, and resources.

## The Six Facets

| Facet | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| [Sosaria](sosaria.md) | Primary homeland and starting area, largest continent | Everything — towns, dungeons, resources |
| [Isles of Dread](isles-of-dread.md) | Remote icy islands with pirate themes | Boosted harvest yields, unique dungeons |
| [Ledoria](ledoria.md) | Vast classic UO-style continent | Exploration, classic dungeons |
| [Savaged Empire](savaged-empire.md) | Primal jungle land with tribal villages | Steel ore, tribal quests |
| [Serpent Island](serpent-island.md) | Volcanic island chain with vice/virtue dungeons | Obsidian ore, serpent quests |
| [Underworld](underworld.md) | Dark subterranean realm | Mithril ore, high-level challenges |

## Sub-Regions (within Sosaria)

| Sub-Region | Description | Premium Material |
|---|---|---|
| Ambrosia | Lost continent on Sosaria map | — |
| Umber Veil | Dark island chain | Brass ore |
| Kuldar | Bottle world with unique dungeons | Sci-fi metals, leathers, bones |

## Travel

- **Moongates** — `[magicgate` to find the nearest gate
- **Ships** — travel between major ports on different facets
- **Magical portals** — found in dungeons and special areas
- **Recall/Portation** — standard magery spells for personal travel

## Dungeon Difficulty

Dungeons scale by tier, affecting creature stats and loot:

| Tier | Difficulty Increase |
|---|---|
| Normal | +0% |
| Difficult | +30% |
| Challenging | +60% |
| Hard | +90% |
| Deadly | +120% |

Higher tiers mean tougher enemies but better rewards.

## Tips

- **Start in Sosaria** — it's the largest continent with the most towns and resources.
- **Visit the Isles of Dread** for boosted harvest yields (~50%+ more resources).
- **Explore the Underworld** when you're ready for high-level challenges and premium ores.
- **Check your map** — use a sextant or map gump to navigate between areas.

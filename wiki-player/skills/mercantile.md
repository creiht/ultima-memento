# Mercantile

Mercantile is the merchant skill that governs NPC trading prices, item identification, and cargo shipping economics. It is the default identification skill for most unidentified items.

## How to Use

- **Identify items** — Target unidentified items to reveal their true name, properties, and estimated gold value.
- **Vendor trading** — Mercantile directly affects how much gold you receive when selling items and how much vendors have available to buy from you.
- **Passive appraisal** — With Mercantile below 50, unidentified items in your vicinity may be automatically appraised.

## How to Train

- **Best method:** Buy and sell items at vendors. Each 200 gold purchased and 500 gold received triggers a skill check.
- **Identification:** Identify unidentified items from monsters.
- **Merchants Guild** membership provides a flat 50% bonus to vendor gold availability (same as 100 Mercantile).

## Key Synergies

- **Arms Lore** — Identifies weapons and armor. Shares the same identification system.
- **Tasting** — Identifies potions, food, and drink. Also shares the identification system.
- **Begging** — Can temporarily replace Mercantile when selling to vendors (while performing the begging pose).

## Tips

- Join the Merchants Guild for the 50% vendor gold bonus and better training conditions.
- At 125 Mercantile, all unidentified items are guaranteed to survive auto-delete checks.
- Mercantile is useful for traders and merchants — it pays to have high Mercantile when running a shop.

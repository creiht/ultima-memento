# Necromancy

Necromancy is the casting skill for dark death magic. It governs success when casting spells from a Necromancer Spellbook, including the duration and strength of debuffs and transformations.

**Note:** Spell damage is driven by Spiritualism, not Necromancy itself. This makes Necromancy primarily a cast-reliability and access skill.

## How to Use

Cast Necromancy spells from your Necromancer Spellbook. The skill governs success chance, spell duration, and the power of certain effects like Strangle, Corpse Skin, and Blood Oath.

**Warning:** Casting Necromancy spells awards negative Karma, which affects your alignment and NPC reactions. At 50+ Necromancy with negative Karma, NPCs flag you as "evil."

## How to Train

Cast Necromancy spells from your spellbook. Each cast rolls a skill gain check. Cast at or near the minimum skill of each spell for best gain rates.

## Key Spells

- **Animate Dead** — Raise undead minions; power scales with average of Necromancy and Spiritualism
- **Corpse Skin** — Bark-like protection, modifies resistances
- **Blood Oath** — Curses a target; reflected damage taken by the caster
- **Strangle** — Damage over time from spiritual force
- **Wraith Form / Lich Form** — Transformative spells with unique effects

## Key Synergies

- **Spiritualism** — Damage skill for all Necromancy spells. Averaged with Necromancy for Animate Dead power.
- **Meditation** — Mana regeneration for sustained casting.
- **Magery / Elementalism** — Alternative magic skills that share region access (Moon facet at 80+).

## Tips

- Pair Necromancy with Spiritualism for maximum spell power.
- Be aware of the Karma penalty — casting too many spells may make NPCs hostile.
- At 80+ Necromancy, you gain access to the Moon facet and can prevent accidental Moon teleports from house teleporters.

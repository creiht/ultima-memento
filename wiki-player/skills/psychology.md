# Psychology

Psychology lets you assess a target's intelligence and mana percentage. Beyond evaluation, it is the most universally important support skill — enhancing spell duration, damage, and power across nearly all magic systems.

## How to Use

Target a creature or player with the Psychology skill. On success, you receive a message describing their intelligence level. At 76+ skill, you also see their current mana percentage.

## How to Train

Target creatures and players. The skill check ranges from 0-125, so any target can provide gains. At 100+ Psychology, your reading is perfectly accurate.

## Why It Matters

Psychology enhances nearly every magic system:
- **Spell duration** — All spells last longer with higher Psychology
- **Stat drain spells** — More effective with higher Psychology
- **Jedi magic** — Psychology is the primary casting skill for all Jedi spells
- **Syth magic** — Psychology is the primary casting skill for all Syth spells
- **Magery** — Combined with Magery for Magic Reflect absorption

## Key Synergies

- **Magery** — Enhances all Magery spell duration and damage. Combined for Magic Reflect absorption.
- **Jedi / Syth** — Primary casting skill for both magic systems. Requires 25+ for Jedi path, 50+ for Syth class.
- **Meditation** — Pairs with Psychology for sustained spellcasting.

## Tips

- Psychology is valuable even for non-mage builds since it enhances nearly every magic system's effectiveness.
- Consider Psychology as a supporting skill alongside your primary magic school.
- At 50+ Psychology with high Swords and negative Karma, you qualify for the Syth class.

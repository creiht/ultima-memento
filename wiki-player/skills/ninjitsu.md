# Ninjitsu

Ninjitsu is a shadow-art skill covering stealth strikes, shapeshifting, and misdirection. It provides a set of abilities cast from a Book of Ninjitsu, including Animal Form transformations, Backstab, and Death Strike.

## How to Use

Acquire a Book of Ninjitsu (purchased from a Monk merchant or found as loot). Activate abilities in combat for offensive and stealth bonuses.

Ninjitsu also provides a passive melee damage bonus to all attacks, identical to Bushido, Tactics, and Necromancy.

## How to Train

Activate Ninjitsu abilities from your book. Each use rolls a skill gain check. Animal Form has no mana cost and is an easy low-skill ability to train early. Ninjitsu has a 2x gain rate, making it one of the faster skills.

## Ninjitsu Abilities

| Ability | Mana | Min Skill | Requires Stealth | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Animal Form | 0 | 10 | No | Transform into an animal (16 forms available) |
| Backstab | 30 | 20 | Yes | Ignores armor; applies Paralyzing Blow |
| Surprise Attack | 20 | 30 | Yes | Debuffs defender's defense for 30 seconds |
| Mirror Image | 10 | 40 | No | Creates a decoy clone |
| Shadow Jump | 15 | 50 | Yes | Teleport while remaining hidden |
| Focus Attack | 20 | 60 | No | Large damage scalar |
| Ki Attack | 25 | 80 | No | Melee damage based on distance traveled |
| Death Strike | 30 | 85 | No | Delayed lethal strike with Mortal Strike |

## Key Synergies

- **Hiding / Stealth** — Required for Backstab, Surprise Attack, and Shadow Jump. Combined Hiding + Stealth boosts Death Strike damage.
- **Tracking** — Adds a stalking bonus that enhances Backstab damage and Surprise Attack malus.
- **Bushido** — Feint weapon ability uses whichever is higher between Ninjitsu and Bushido. Damage bonus formulas are identical.
- **Shinobi** — All Shinobi spells use Ninjitsu as their casting and damage skill.

## Tips

- Several Ninjitsu abilities require being hidden — pair with Stealth and Hiding for maximum effectiveness.
- Animal Form at 10 skill gives access to Rat and Rabbit forms (+20 Stealth each), making early stealth training easier.
- Ki Attack cannot be used while hidden or with ranged weapons.

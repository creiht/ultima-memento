# Fencing

Fencing is the hit skill for piercing weapons — spears, kryss, sai, daggers, and other thrusting weapons.

## How to Use

Fencing is a passive weapon skill. Whenever you attack with a fencing weapon, your skill determines accuracy and defense. Dexterity affects swing speed; Tactics and Strength contribute damage.

At Grandmaster level, your character title is "Fencer."

## How to Train

Attack with any fencing weapon. Fencing gains passively on every swing. It has no anti-macro restrictions, so you can train anywhere.

## Key Synergies

- **Swordsmanship / Bludgeoning / Fist Fighting** — Some weapons use whichever of these four is highest (UseBestSkill).
- **Tactics** — Contributes to weapon ability mana reduction. Paired with Fencing on runic tool bonuses.
- **Poisoning** — Many fencing weapons deal piercing damage that applies poison coatings effectively.

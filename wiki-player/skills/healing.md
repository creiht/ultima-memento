# Healing

Healing allows you to mend your own wounds, cure poison, and stop bleeding without bandages or reagents.

## How to Use

Use the Healing skill on yourself (or others) to heal hit points. The priority is: cure poison and stop bleeding first, then heal HP if neither condition is present.

Heal amount scales with your skill level — roughly 12 at 50 skill, 22 at 100, and 42 at 125.

## How to Train

Use the skill whenever you are injured. You can gain at any skill level as long as you have damage to heal.

## Key Synergies

- **Anatomy** — Required alongside Healing (80+ each) to resurrect dead henchmen with bandages.
- **Spiritualism** — Paired in Holy Man spell formulas for healing, durations, and benefits.
- **Bandages** — Healing skill determines bandage quality when crafting.

## Tips

- Healing is one of the most frequently used skills — nearly every build benefits from it.
- Poison curing requires both Healing >= 60 AND Anatomy >= 60.
- Even without Healing, you can use bandages on yourself, but the skill makes it more effective.

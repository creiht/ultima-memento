# Provocation

Provocation uses music to incite one creature to attack another creature (or yourself). It is one of three bard combat skills and a key tool for controlling battlefield flow.

## How to Use

Select an instrument, then target a creature to anger, and a second creature (or yourself) as the target. The first creature will attack the second. Both must be within bard range.

You can target yourself to pull aggro — useful for tanking in group situations.

## How to Train

Provoke creatures against each other, starting with weaker ones. Provocation has a 95% gain chance, making it one of the fastest bard skills to train.

## Key Synergies

- **Musicianship** — Required before every Provocation attempt. Musicianship above 100 reduces difficulty.
- **Discordance / Peacemaking** — All three bard skills feed into the combined MusicSkill total that powers every bard song.
- **Taming / Herding** — Useful for controlling multiple pets in combat situations.

## Tips

- Provocation has a 95% gain chance — the second-highest among all skills.
- Success gives a 6-second cooldown; failure only 3 seconds, so failed attempts still allow quick retries.
- Use it to pull dangerous creatures toward safer allies or away from vulnerable targets.

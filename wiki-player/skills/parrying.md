# Parrying

Parrying lets you raise your shield to absorb incoming melee and magic damage. It also passively improves your dodge chance and increases the effective armor rating of your shield.

## How to Use

Use the skill with a shield equipped to gain temporary damage absorption. The absorbed amount scales with your Parrying skill and Dexterity. The buff lasts 5 seconds — time it to absorb heavy hits.

**Note:** Parrying is a revealing action that breaks hiding.

## How to Train

Use the skill with a shield equipped. The passive gain check uses a 50% success threshold, so gains are relatively steady.

## Key Synergies

- **Evasion (Bushido)** — Bushido's Evasion stance checks Parry. If both are active and the check succeeds, the attacker completely misses.
- **Tactics / Anatomy** — Compete equally for Block and Defense Mastery weapon ability bonuses (whichever is higher determines the value).
- **Dexterity** — Primary stat. Low Dex (<80) significantly reduces absorption values.

## Tips

- The 5-second absorption window means timing matters — activate before taking heavy hits.
- Parrying breaks hiding, so plan accordingly when sneaking.
- Warriors Guild members receive faster skill gain for Parrying.

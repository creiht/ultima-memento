# Druidism

Druidism is the nature skill — it lets you examine creatures to learn their stats and weaknesses, governs a herbalism crafting profession, and enables 16 nature-themed druidic spells. It also reduces taming difficulty.

## How to Use

Target a creature to open a detailed information gump showing its resistances, stats, damage types, and taming requirements.

Druidism passively reduces taming difficulty by `Druidism / 5` points. When Druidism exceeds Taming, the lore value is used instead of Taming for taming calculations.

## How to Train

Target creatures to open the lore gump. Druidism also gains passively when taming creatures for the first time.

## Thresholds

| Skill | What You Unlock |
|---|---|
| 100+ | Can lore any creature (yours or others') |
| 110+ | Can lore untameable creatures too |
| 50+ | Can set up an Herbalist shop |

## Druid Spells

Druidism governs 16 nature-themed spells cast from potions. Key spells include:

- **Herbal Healing Elixir** (45) — Heals target of all ailments
- **Woodland Protection Oil** (25) — Bark-like skin, increases protection
- **Forest Blending Oil** (50) — Blends with the forest, foes lose sight
- **Nature Passage Mixture** (15) — Turns caster to petals, transports to a rune
- **Jar of Magical Mud** (85) — Resurrects after death

## Herbalism Crafting

Druidism is the primary skill for druidic herbalism, brewed at a Druid Cauldron. Recipes require both Druidism and Veterinary. Notable potions include healing elixirs, protection oils, and summons like Treant Fertilizer and Fairy in a Jar.

## Pet Follower Capacity

Druidism is one of four skills (with Taming, Veterinary, Herding) that determine follower capacity:
- All four at 60+: 6 followers
- All four at 90+: 7 followers
- All four at 120+: 8 followers

## Key Synergies

- **Veterinary** — Required sub-skill for all herbalism recipes. Damage skill for druidic spells. Both contribute to follower capacity.
- **Taming** — Druidism reduces taming difficulty. Passively trained during taming.
- **Herding** — Paired with Druidism, Veterinary, and Taming for max follower count and stable slots.

## Tips

- Druidism is valuable even for non-druid builds — the creature lore helps you prepare for dangerous encounters.
- The 100+ threshold lets you lore any creature's taming requirements, which is useful for planning your pet collection.

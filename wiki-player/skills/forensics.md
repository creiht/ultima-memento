# Forensics

Forensic Evaluation lets you investigate crime scenes, examine corpses for clues, and detect Thieves' Guild members. It also enhances corpse skinning and is used by several crafting systems.

## How to Use

- **Examine corpses** — Learn who killed them and who looted them
- **Examine NPCs** — Detect if they are Thieves' Guild members
- **Examine coffers and locks** — Discover who robbed them

## How to Train

Examine corpses and locked containers. Corpse skinning also performs a Forensics check, contributing to passive training.

## Key Benefits

- **Corpse carving** — Increases the quantity of harvested resources (hides, meat, bones, etc.) when skinning. Scales with both Forensics and Anatomy.
- **Grave Robbing** — Higher Forensics increases treasure chest find rates.
- **Crafting** — Primary skill for Witchery (undead-themed brewing) and required for certain Masonry/Shelves recipes.
- **Shoppe access** — At 50+ Forensics, you can set up a shop at Undertaker and Necromancer vendors.

## Key Synergies

- **Anatomy** — Both contribute equally to corpse carving bonuses.
- **Necromancy** — Averaged with Forensics for Mortician Shoppe skill.
- **Grave Robbing** — Uses Forensics as its primary skill.

## Tips

- Forensics trains passively during skinning — no active use required.
- Combine with Anatomy for maximum harvesting bonuses.
- High Forensics with negative karma grants the "Undertaker" classification.

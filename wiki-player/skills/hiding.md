# Hiding

Hiding lets you conceal yourself from the sight of other players and creatures, enabling sneak attacks and evasion. It is a foundational rogue skill.

## How to Use

Use the skill to conceal yourself. Out of combat, it is guaranteed at 100 skill. In combat, it is extremely difficult below 100 — you need 100+ for any chance, reaching 40% at 125 skill.

## How to Train

Simply use the skill repeatedly out of combat. The check ranges from 0-100, so you can gain on any attempt until reaching GM.

## Auto-Stealth

At 100+ Hiding, successfully hiding will automatically attempt to activate Stealth. If successful, you begin moving silently immediately.

## Key Synergies

- **Stealth** — Paired for advanced mobility. Combined skill determines sneak attack damage bonus (up to 125% for melee). Auto-stealth at 100+ Hiding.
- **Ninjitsu** — Death Strike damage scales with combined Hiding + Stealth.
- **Searching** — Detection contests: Searchers try to reveal you while you try to stay hidden.

## Tips

- Hiding cancels active targets and sets you out of war mode.
- Your pets become hidden when you hide, providing a team-wide stealth benefit.
- Invisibility potions reduce your Hiding skill — avoid drinking them before hiding.

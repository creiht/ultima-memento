# Stealing

Stealing lets you pilfer items from containers, coffers, dungeon chests, and the backpacks of other creatures and players.

## How to Use

Use the skill and target an item, container, or creature. You must be **empty-handed** (no weapons wielded).

- **From creatures/players** — Steal a random item from their backpack (or target a specific item). Difficulty scales with item weight.
- **From coffers** — Steal all gold from a merchant coffer.
- **Dungeon chests** — Steal the entire container with its contents.

Stolen items last 2 minutes. If you die while carrying stolen goods, they return to the victim.

## Getting Caught

At 100 skill, you have roughly a 33% chance of being noticed. Getting caught removes your disguise and may flag you as criminal.

## How to Train

Steal from dungeon chests and coffers. Lighter items are easier to steal. Pickpocket training dummies can help at lower skill levels.

## Key Synergies

- **Snooping** — Directly paired. Snooping enables stealing from containers and creatures.
- **Hiding / Stealth** — Stay hidden while stealing. Failed attempts may reveal you.
- **Thieves Guild** — Required to steal from innocent players and access coffer quests. Guild members receive faster gains.

## Tips

- You must be empty-handed to steal (pugilist gloves are allowed).
- Stolen items disappear after 2 minutes if not used.
- Getting caught stealing from innocent players will criminalize you.

# Remove Trap

Remove Trap allows you to disarm traps on containers and hidden floor traps. It is essential for dungeon exploration and provides passive protection against deadly traps.

## How to Use

Target a trapped object to disarm it. Failed attempts do not trigger the trap, so it is safe to try at any skill level.

## How to Train

Find trapped containers in dungeons and disarm them. Hidden floor traps (found by Searching) provide skill checks at any level.

## Key Benefits

- **Container traps** — Disarm trapped chests, coffers, and dungeon containers
- **Floor traps** — Detect and disarm hidden floor traps in dungeons
- **Passive protection** — At 90+ Remove Trap, you have a chance to survive killer tiles (25% at 90, increasing to ~35% at 125)
- **Trapping** — Higher Remove Trap makes your own traps more effective

## Key Synergies

- **Searching** — Detects hidden traps. Use Searching first, then Remove Trap.
- **Stealing** — Both are used together in thief quests for stealing from pedestals.
- **Lockpicking** — Chests are often both locked and trapped — disarm traps before picking.
- **Magery** — The Remove Trap spell (2nd circle) provides a magical alternative using Magery skill.

## Tips

- Failed Remove Trap checks do not trigger the trap — always safe to try.
- At 90+ Remove Trap, you gain meaningful protection against killer tiles.
- The Torch of Trap Burning artifact provides an enormous +100 bonus.

# Poisoning

Poisoning lets you apply poison from potions onto weapons, food, and beverages. Higher Poisoning allows stronger poisons and increases the effectiveness of poison-related abilities.

## How to Use

Select a poison potion from your inventory, then target the item you want to poison. Valid targets include weapons, food, and drinks.

**Note:** Successfully poisoning costs 20 Karma. At 80+ Poisoning, failed attempts have no penalty. Below 80, there is a 5% chance you poison yourself.

## How to Train

Apply poison potions to weapons, food, or drink. Use progressively stronger poisons as your skill increases.

### Poison Levels

| Poison Potion | Min Skill | Charges on Weapon |
|---|---|---|
| Lesser | 0 | 18 |
| Regular | 20 | 16 |
| Greater | 40 | 14 |
| Deadly | 60 | 12 |
| Lethal | 80 | 10 |

## Key Synergies

- **Tasting** — Detects poison in food and drink. Essential for using poisoned consumables safely.
- **Stealth** — Shadow Infectious Strike requires Stealth >= 80.
- **Magery / Necromancy** — Poison and Poison Field spells scale with Poisoning. Necromancy's Poison Strike benefits from Poisoning.
- **Weapon Abilities** — Infectious Strike and Shadow Infectious Strike apply weapon poison on hit.

## Tips

- Poisoning is a core Assassins Guild skill — members receive faster gains.
- At 80+ Poisoning, you can consistently apply Deadly poison. Lethal requires higher skill or weapon abilities.
- The 20 Karma cost means frequent poisoning may affect your alignment over time.

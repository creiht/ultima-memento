# Lockpicking

Lockpicking lets you open locked chests, coffers, and dungeon doors using lockpicks as a consumable tool. Difficulty scales with the lock level.

## How to Use

Double-click a Lockpick, then target a locked container or door. Doors require 30+ Lockpicking before any attempt is allowed.

**Warning:** Every failed attempt has a 25% chance to break the lockpick. Keep a stack on hand.

**Treasure Map Chests:** Failing on these destroys a random item from the chest's contents. Raise your skill before attempting these.

## How to Train

Attempt to pick locks repeatedly. Lockpicking gains passively on each attempt, whether you succeed or fail. If your base is 0, the first attempts fire 10 rapid checks to help you get started.

## Key Synergies

- **Remove Trap** — Chests are often both locked and trapped. Disarm traps before picking.
- **Tinkering** — Crafts lockpicks and determines lock levels on crafted containers.
- **Alchemy** — Elixir of Lockpicking provides a temporary boost.

## Tips

- Always disarm traps before attempting to pick a lock.
- Treasure Map Chests are dangerous — get your skill to at least 60+ before attempting.
- Acid bottles can bypass many locks as an alternative to lockpicking.

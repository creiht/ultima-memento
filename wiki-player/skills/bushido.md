# Bushido

Bushido is the samurai combat discipline. It provides a set of powerful abilities activated from a Book of Bushido, including healing stances, counter-attacks, and devastating strikes.

## How to Use

Acquire a Book of Bushido (purchased from a Monk merchant or found as loot). Activate abilities in combat to gain offensive and defensive bonuses. Only one stance (Confidence, Evasion, or Counter Attack) can be active at a time.

Bushido also passively adds damage to Axe, Slashing, and Polearm weapons, and improves parry chance when both Parrying and Bushido are at 100+.

## How to Train

Activate Bushido abilities in combat. Start with Confidence/Honorable Execution at 25, then progress to Counter Attack at 40, Lightning Strike at 50, and Momentum Strike at 70.

## Bushido Abilities

| Ability | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Confidence | 25 | Regenerates HP each tick; prevents spells from being interrupted by hits |
| Counter Attack | 40 | After blocking, immediately counter-attack with your weapon |
| Honorable Execution | 25 | Next hit deals extra damage. Kill = heal and swing bonus. Miss = debuffs on target |
| Lightning Strike | 50 | Next attack gains accuracy and armor-piercing chance |
| Evasion | 60 | Defensive stance that lets you parry with a weapon |
| Momentum Strike | 70 | After killing a target, strikes a second nearby enemy |

## Key Synergies

- **Ninjitsu** — Feint ability uses whichever skill is higher between Bushido and Ninjitsu. Both contribute to weapon ability mana reduction.
- **Tactics / Anatomy** — GM Tactics + GM Anatomy extends Evasion duration and parry effectiveness.
- **Parrying** — Synergizes with Evasion's weapon-parry chance.

## Tips

- Use Confidence when fighting spellcasters — it prevents hit-interrupts.
- Honorable Execution is risk/reward: kill = big heal, miss = heavy debuff on you.
- Evasion requires 50+ in a weapon skill to activate.

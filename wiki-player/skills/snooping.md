# Snooping

Snooping lets you peek into the backpacks of other creatures and players to see what they are carrying. It is a passive skill that triggers automatically when you attempt to open a container belonging to another mobile.

## How It Works

Snooping is not an active skill — it triggers when you open a container that belongs to another creature or player.

- **Success** — You see the container's contents.
- **Failure** — "You failed to peek into the container." If your Hiding is low, you may be revealed from hiding.
- **Detection** — Even on success, nearby players may see "You notice [Name] attempting to peek into [Target]'s belongings."

**Cost:** Snooping costs 4 Karma when successful with positive karma.

## How to Train

Snooping gains come from attempting to open containers on creatures and NPCs. The skill check is 0-125.

## Key Synergies

- **Stealing** — Directly paired. Snooping enables stealing from containers and creatures. Thieves Guild auto-restricts both.
- **Hiding** — Failed snooping may reveal you based on Hiding. Protects against being exposed.
- **Lockpicking** — Both used on containers. Thieves Guild auto-restricts both.
- **Remove Trap** — Trapped containers trigger on snooping; disarm first.

## Tips

- Snooping citizens is always free of restrictions.
- Be aware of nearby players — even successful snooping may be detected.
- Disguise Kit requires 50+ Snooping to use.

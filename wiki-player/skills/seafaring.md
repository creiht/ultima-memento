# Seafaring

Seafaring governs fishing, sailing, and maritime combat. It is the primary skill for all ocean-based activities in Ultima Memento.

## How to Use

- **Fishing** — The sole skill checked on every fishing attempt. Below 50 skill you may fish from land; at 50+ you must be aboard a boat.
- **Boat speed** — Higher Seafaring unlocks faster sailing speeds.
- **Harpoon combat** — The Harpoon weapon gains a damage bonus from Seafaring.

## How to Train

Seafaring gains passively on each fishing action and each sailing movement. Fish regularly and sail frequently to raise it.

## Skill Thresholds

| Seafaring | What You Unlock |
|---|---|
| 30+ | Basic fishing net |
| 50+ | Must fish from a boat (land fishing blocked); faster sailing |
| 60+ | Special fishing net |
| 90+ | Fabled fishing net; boat door becomes visible from outside |
| 100+ | Neptune's fishing net; dock anywhere |
| 125 | Fastest sailing speed |

## Key Synergies

- **Marksmanship** — The Harpoon combines Seafaring (damage bonus) with Marksmanship (hit chance).
- **Mercantile** — Increases gold received when selling fish and cargo to merchants.
- **Carpentry** — Affects quality of boards salvaged from driftboats and sunken ships.
- **Tracking** — Can detect hidden sea creatures that Seafaring alone cannot reveal.

## Tips

- At 50+ Seafaring, you must fish from a boat — plan your trips accordingly.
- Grandmaster Seafaring (100+) lets you dock anywhere, bypassing official docks entirely.
- The Sword of Sinbad artifact provides a +30 Seafaring bonus.

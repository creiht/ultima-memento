# Inscribe

Inscribe allows you to copy the contents of one book to another, and is the primary skill for the Research system's scribing mechanics. It also provides passive weapon damage and spell resistance bonuses.

## How to Use

Use the skill to target a source book, then a destination book. On success, the destination book receives the title, author, and all page contents of the source book.

Requirements: the source book must not be empty, the destination must be writable, and neither book can be actively inscribed by someone else.

## How to Train

Copy books repeatedly. The skill check range for book copying is 0-50, so gaining slows significantly after 50 skill.

## Key Benefits

- **Book copying** — Duplicate spellbooks and other written materials
- **Weapon damage** — Small passive bonus at very high skill levels
- **Spell resistances** — Increases resistances for Reactive Armor, Protection, and Psychic Aura spells
- **Runebook charges** — Higher Inscribe means more charges on crafted runebooks
- **Research system** — Required (30+) to open Research Bags and scribe spells from scrolls

## Key Synergies

- **Research** — Core skill for scribing. Requires 30+ Inscribe to open Research Bags.
- **Magery** — Reactive Armor and Protection spell resistances scale with Inscribe.
- **Mercantile** — Complementary utility skill for identifying items.

## Tips

- Inscribe is essential for players interested in the Research magic system.
- At 50+ Inscribe, you can set up a Scribe shop at appropriate vendor NPCs.
- High Inscribe fully negates the Physical resist penalty of the Protection spell.

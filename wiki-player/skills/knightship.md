# Knightship

Knightship is the combat-faith skill shared by holy Paladins and dark Death Knights. It governs the casting and power of both the Knight (Chivalry) and Death Knight magic systems.

## How to Use

Your **Karma** determines which spellbook you access:
- **Positive Karma** → Paladin (Chivalry) spells — healing, buffs, and protective abilities
- **Negative Karma** → Death Knight spells — curses, drains, and dark summons

Activate Knightship spells from your applicable spellbook. Death Knight abilities additionally require trapped souls from a Soul Lantern.

## How to Train

Cast Knightship spells from your spellbook. Skill gains occur on successful spell casts.

## Key Paladin Spells

- **Cleanse By Fire** (5) — Cure poison
- **Consecrate Weapon** (15) — Weapon deals damage in target's weakest resistance
- **Divine Fury** (25) — Increased attack speed and damage
- **Noble Sacrifice** (65) — Heals, cures, and resurrects allies at personal cost

## Key Death Knight Spells

- **Demonic Touch** (15) — Heal using karma power
- **Grim Reaper** (30) — Mark target for increased damage
- **Banish** (40) — Dispel summoned creatures
- **Hellfire** (70) — Damage and burn effect
- **Devil Pact** (90) — Summon a Devil

## Mounts

- **Paladin Warhorse** — Requires 100+ Knightship and positive Karma
- **Death Knight Warhorse** — Requires 100+ Knightship and negative Karma

## Key Synergies

- **Tactics** — Damage amplifier for melee combat between spell casts.
- **Parrying** — Defensive complement for shielded Paladin combat.
- **Karma** — Your alignment determines which spells are available.

## Tips

- Knightship is unique in that the same skill provides two entirely different spellbooks based on Karma.
- Death Knight spells consume souls from a Soul Lantern in addition to mana.
- At 100+ Knightship, you can summon a dedicated warhorse mount.

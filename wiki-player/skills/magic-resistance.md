# Magic Resistance

Magic Resistance passively gives you a chance to reduce or completely ignore the debuff component of hostile spells from multiple magical systems.

## How It Works

When hit by a hostile spell from Magery, Elementalism, Jedi, Syth, or other magic systems, Magic Resistance determines whether you resist the spell's effects. A successful resist cancels the debuff but does not reduce direct damage.

Magic Resistance also protects against bardic debuffs like Discordance and Peacemaking.

## How to Train

Magic Resistance trains passively whenever you are hit by a hostile spell. Each incoming spell whose difficulty exceeds your current skill fires a gain check automatically — no action required.

## What It Resists

- **Magery** — Poison, Mana Drain, Fire Field, and other spell effects
- **Elementalism** — Elemental Protection's resistance reduction, immobilization effects
- **Jedi / Syth** — Psychic debuffs and effects
- **Necromancy** — Poison Strike (though return value is sometimes ignored)
- **Bardic skills** — Discordance strength debuff, Peacemaking pacify effects
- **Various magic items** — Fire Horn, Hidden Magic Traps

## Key Synergies

- **Meditation** — Mana regeneration pairs well for magic-focused characters.
- **Psychology** — Evaluating caster skill levels helps predict your resist chance.

## Tips

- Magic Resistance is one of the best defensive skills in the game since it trains automatically.
- Some magic systems (Shinobi, Jester) completely bypass Magic Resistance.
- The skill also reduces reflected damage from effects like Blood Oath.

# Begging

Begging has three uses: requesting gold from NPCs, pacifying hostile creatures in combat, and gaining merchant discounts by toggling a begging demeanor.

## How to Use

- **Request gold** — Target a human NPC while within 2 tiles. They may give you gold, but this costs 40 karma.
- **Pacify enemies** — Target a hostile creature attacking you. On success, it stops fighting for a period of time. This costs 40 karma and 40 fame.
- **Vendor discounts** — Target yourself to toggle begging demeanor. While active, you receive a discount at most vendors (0.5% per skill point).

## How to Train

Use the skill on hostile creatures. Stronger enemies are harder to beg. Training is faster against more difficult targets.

## Key Synergies

- **Mercantile** — Competing skill for vendor discounts. Begging replaces Mercantile when begging demeanor is active, so choose your preferred barter skill.
- **Psychology** — Used together in Jester spell calculations.
- **Hiding** — Alternative way to disengage from combat without the karma/fame cost of begging in combat.

## Tips

- Toggle begging demeanor before visiting vendors for the best prices.
- Combat begging is a useful emergency escape but costs karma and fame — don't overuse it.
- Players cannot be paralyzed by begging; only creatures.

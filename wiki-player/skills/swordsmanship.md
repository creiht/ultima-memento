# Swordsmanship

Swordsmanship is the hit skill for most bladed melee weapons — long swords, katanas, broadswords, scimitars, and more. It is the default weapon skill for anything that doesn't declare its own.

## How to Use

Swordsmanship is a passive weapon skill. Whenever you attack with a sword-type weapon, your skill determines accuracy and defense. Tactics and Strength add damage; Dexterity affects swing speed.

Swordsmanship also serves as the damage skill for the Jedi and Syth magic systems, and is one of the three prerequisite skills for casting those spells (alongside Tactics and Psychology).

## How to Train

Attack with any sword-type weapon. Swordsmanship gains passively on every swing. Unlike most skills, it has no anti-macro restrictions — you can train anywhere.

## Key Synergies

- **Jedi / Syth** — Swordsmanship is the damage skill for both magic systems. Requires 10+ in Swordsmanship, Tactics, and Psychology to cast any spell.
- **Tactics** — Adds damage and gates weapon ability tiers. Both contribute to aggro generation in Jedi/Syth.
- **Fencing / Bludgeoning / Fist Fighting** — Some weapons use whichever of these four is highest (UseBestSkill).

## Tips

- Swordsmanship can be trained anywhere since it has no anti-macro restrictions.
- Even if you mainly use another weapon skill, a moderate Swordsmanship level can help since many weapons use the best skill among all four weapon skills.

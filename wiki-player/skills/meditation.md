# Meditation

Meditation lets you enter a trance to regenerate mana at an increased rate. It is the primary skill governing mana regeneration for all spellcasters.

## How to Use

Use the Meditation skill whenever your mana is not full. On success, you enter a meditative trance and your mana regenerates much faster. You cannot meditate when mana is already full.

**Armor interferes with meditation.** Heavy armor prevents it entirely. Wear mage robes or armor marked as MeditationAllowance.All to meditate without penalty.

## How to Train

Use the skill after casting spells whenever mana is below max. The skill check is against 0-100, so you can gain at any level. Meditation is a secondary skill that ignores the total skill cap.

## Key Synergies

- **Focus** — Adds bonus mana regen on top of Meditation. Both work together for maximum recovery.
- **Intelligence** — Your Int stat directly factors into Meditation's mana regeneration formula.
- **Mystic magic** — Requires 100+ base Meditation to cast any Mystic spell.

## Tips

- Keep Meditation active whenever your mana is below max for the fastest recovery.
- If you wear armor, ensure it has MeditationAllowance.All or MeditationAllowance.Half to avoid penalties.
- Meditation is essential for any spellcasting build — it's one of the most valuable skills in the game.

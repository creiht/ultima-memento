# Lumberjacking

Lumberjacking is a harvest skill used to cut down trees and collect wood for crafting and construction.

## How to Use

Use an axe or chainsaw on trees in forested areas. Higher skill yields more and better quality wood.

## How to Train

Use an axe on common trees. Specialized tools like chainsaws on high-value trees increase efficiency and experience gains.

## Key Synergies

- **Carpentry** — Uses harvested wood for furniture, structures, and wooden items.
- **Bowfletching** — Uses wood for crafting bows and other ranged weapons.
- **Weapon Ability Mana** — Contributes to the combined weapon skill pool for mana cost reduction.

## Tips

- Higher-quality trees yield better wood — explore different regions for variety.
- Chainsaws can significantly improve harvesting speed and efficiency.

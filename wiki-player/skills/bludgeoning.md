# Bludgeoning

Bludgeoning is the hit skill for all blunt weapons — clubs, maces, war hammers, and mauls. It also receives a unique damage bonus from Mining.

## How to Use

Bludgeoning is a passive weapon skill. Whenever you attack with a blunt weapon, your Bludgeoning skill determines accuracy and defense. It also affects your unarmed defense.

At Grandmaster level, your character title is "Bludgeoner."

## How to Train

Attack with any bashing weapon (club, mace, war hammer, maul). Bludgeoning gains passively on every swing.

## Mining Bonus

Bludgeoning weapons receive extra damage from the Mining skill — up to +10 bonus damage at 100+ Mining. This bonus applies to all bashing weapons, even if you don't use Bludgeoning as your best weapon skill.

## Key Synergies

- **Mining** — The only weapon skill with a direct damage bonus from a gathering skill. Higher Mining = more damage with blunt weapons.
- **Tactics** — Both contribute to weapon ability mana cost reduction. When combined with other weapon skills to reach 300+ total, special abilities cost 10 less mana.
- **Swordsmanship / Fencing / Fist Fighting** — Some weapons can use whichever skill is highest among these four.

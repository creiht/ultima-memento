# Tasting

Tasting serves multiple roles: detecting poison in food and drinks, identifying certain items and relics, and enhancing the potency and duration of consumed foods, elixirs, and mixtures.

## How to Use

- **Detect poison** — Target food or drink to learn its poison level (if any). A failed check against poisoned food means you accidentally consume it!
- **Identify items** — Functions as an identification skill for Tasting-marked items and relics.

## How to Train

Test poisoned and unpoisoned food/beverages. You can also identify Tasting-marked items to gain skill. Be careful with strong poisons at low skill — failure means consuming them.

## Key Benefits

- **Poison detection** — Critical for eating food safely, especially in dungeons
- **Food healing** — Higher Tasting means more HP recovered from eating food
- **Elixir/mixture enhancement** — Increases duration and strength of consumed elixirs and mixtures
- **Potion upgrades** — At 30+, loot packs can upgrade LesserHealPotion to HealPotion to GreaterHealPotion

## Key Synergies

- **Poisoning** — Apply poison to items that Tasting detects.
- **Cooking** — Works alongside Tasting for elixir/mixture duration and strength.
- **Healing** — Higher Tasting means more HP recovered from food consumption.
- **Arms Lore / Mercantile** — The three identification skills cover different item categories.

## Tips

- Always check food and drink with Tasting before consuming in dangerous areas.
- Failed poison detection checks consume the poisoned item — be cautious with Deadly and Lethal poisons at low skill.
- Tasting is valuable for traders, cooks, and anyone who carries potions or elixirs.

# Anatomy

Anatomy lets you evaluate creatures and players to learn their physical stats, stamina levels, and weaknesses. It also adds damage to your attacks and is essential for healing and unarmed combat.

## How to Use

Target a living creature or player with the Anatomy skill. You will receive a message describing their relative strength and dexterity. At 65+ skill, you also see their current stamina percentage.

You can also use Anatomy during combat — your weapon deals more damage the higher your Anatomy skill is. Two-handed weapons get a bonus crushing blow at higher skill levels.

## How to Train

Target creatures or other players with the skill. It can be trained on any target at any skill level. Training is faster against stronger creatures.

## Key Synergies

- **Healing** — Anatomy scales healing output and is required alongside Healing (80+ each) to resurrect henchmen with bandages.
- **Tactics** — Both add damage to your attacks. For certain weapon abilities, whichever skill is higher determines the bonus.
- **Fist Fighting** — At 80+ in both, you can use the Stun move to freeze opponents for 4 seconds. Anatomy also boosts your unarmed defend value.
- **Forensics** — Together they increase the amount of hides, meat, and materials you harvest from creatures.

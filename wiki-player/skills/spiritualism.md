# Spiritualism

Spiritualism channels spiritual energy to heal HP, restore stamina, and partially recover mana. It is also the damage skill for Necromancy spells.

## How to Use

Use the skill to channel energy. If an unchanneled corpse is nearby (within 3 tiles), you draw from it for free. Without a corpse, it costs 10 mana.

On success, you heal HP, restore stamina, and gain 25% of the heal as mana. Your character speaks a mantra — positive karma players say "Anh Mi Sah Ko" (golden particles), negative karma players say "Xtee Mee Glau" (dark particles).

## How to Train

Use the skill regularly, ideally near corpses to avoid the mana cost. The skill check is 0-120.

## Heal Formula

Heal amount scales with Spiritualism and Fist Fighting:
- Min heal = 1 + (Spiritualism × 0.25) + (Fist Fighting × 0.15)
- Max heal = Min + 4
- Success rate = Spiritualism / 100 (always succeeds at 100)

## Key Synergies

- **Fist Fighting** — Contributes to the heal formula. Both required at 100+ for Mystic magic.
- **Necromancy** — Damage skill for all Necromancy spells. Averaged with Necromancy for Animate Dead power.
- **Meditation** — Mana regeneration synergizes with Spiritualism's mana restoration.

## Tips

- Keep an eye out for corpses to channel for free healing between fights.
- Channeling a corpse turns it grey — it cannot be used again.
- Spiritualism is essential for Necromancy builds since it determines spell damage.

# Tactics

Tactics amplifies the damage of every melee and ranged attack, and is the gating skill for all weapon special ability tiers.

## How to Use

Tactics is a passive skill — it adds damage to every swing automatically. It also determines which weapon special abilities are available to you. Higher Tactics unlocks more powerful abilities.

## How to Train

Tactics gains passively on every weapon swing. Fight regularly and it will rise alongside your weapon skill.

## Key Synergies

- **Anatomy** — Both contribute to damage. For certain weapon abilities, whichever skill is higher determines the bonus.
- **All weapon skills** — Tactics gates all special ability tiers across every weapon type.

## Tips

- Tactics is essential for every combat build — it boosts damage and unlocks special attacks.
- No active use required; it trains passively during combat.

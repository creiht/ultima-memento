# Marksmanship

Marksmanship is the hit skill for all ranged weapons — bows, crossbows, throwing gloves, harpoons, and firearms.

## How to Use

Marksmanship is a passive weapon skill. Whenever you attack with a ranged weapon, your skill determines accuracy and defense. Tactics and Strength contribute to damage; Dexterity affects swing speed and stamina cost.

## How to Train

Attack with any ranged weapon. Marksmanship gains passively on every shot. It has no anti-macro restrictions, so you can train anywhere.

Ranged weapons also have a 40% chance to recover ammo on hit and another 40% on miss, making Marksmanship efficient for extended combat.

## Key Synergies

- **Tactics** — Amplifies ranged damage and gates weapon ability tiers. Combined with Marksmanship for special move mana reduction.
- **Seafaring** — Provides a Harpoon damage bonus on top of Marksmanship hit chance.
- **Weapon Abilities** — Marksmanship contributes to mana cost reduction when combined weapon skills reach 300+.

## Tips

- Marksmanship can be trained anywhere since it has no anti-macro restrictions.
- Ammo recovery makes ranged combat very efficient for sustained use.

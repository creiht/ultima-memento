# Tracking

Tracking lets you locate creatures and players within a wide area by selecting from a category list, then following them with a quest arrow.

## How to Use

Use the skill to open the Track What gump. Select a creature category, view matching targets within range, and lock onto one with a quest arrow.

Tracking range = 25 + (Tracking skill / 2). Higher skill means a wider search area.

## How to Train

Track different creature categories. The skill gains passively on each use.

## Stalking Bonus

When tracking a creature and attacking it, you gain a stalking bonus that enhances your attack accuracy. This bonus combines with Hiding and Ninjitsu for Ninjitsu ability checks.

## Key Synergies

- **Hiding / Stealth** — Tracking difficulty factors in a target's combined Hiding + Stealth.
- **Ninjitsu** — Tracking's stalking bonus adds to Surprise Attack malus and Backstab damage.
- **Camping** — Both provide access to Ranger Outpost housing (either 90+ works).

## Tips

- Track rare or valuable creatures to find them quickly across the continent.
- Use tracking to locate specific creature types for farming, taming, or quest objectives.

# Mining

Mining is a harvest skill used to extract ore and stone from the environment. It is a fundamental skill for crafters, providing raw materials for blacksmithing and other metal-based trades.

## How to Use

Use a pickaxe on ore veins found in mountains, caves, and sandy areas. Higher skill levels increase success rate and allow access to more valuable ores.

### Ore Types

- **Common Ores:** Iron, Dull Copper, Copper, Bronze
- **Rare Ores:** Gold, Agapite, Verite, Valorite
- **Stone:** Granite and other geological materials

At 100+ skill, you can mine sand in coastal or desert regions.

## How to Train

Use a pickaxe on ore veins. Target higher-tier veins (Verite or Valorite) for better experience gains at higher skill levels.

## Key Synergies

- **Blacksmithy** — Uses mined ores to create weapons and armor.
- **Bludgeoning** — Receives a unique damage bonus from Mining on blunt weapons.
- **Glassblowing** — Sand harvested via mining is a key component for glass production.

## Tips

- Mining cannot be performed while mounted or polymorphed.
- A Gargoyle Pickaxe can help mutate veins to higher-tier ores.

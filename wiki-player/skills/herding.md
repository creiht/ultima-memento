# Herding

Herding lets you direct tamed animals with a shepherd's crook, assists with pet loyalty control, and amplifies pet experience gain.

## How to Use

Double-click a Shepherd's Crook, target a tamed creature, then target a destination tile. The skill moves the creature toward that location.

## How to Train

Use the crook to herd various creatures. Herding also gains passively during pet loyalty checks and when pets gain experience.

## Pet Synergy

Herding assists pet loyalty checks — on failed control attempts, a Herding check can prevent the loyalty drop. It also boosts pet XP gain.

## Beastmaster Tiers

Your follower capacity and stable slots are determined by the combined levels of four skills: Taming, Druidism, Veterinary, and Herding.

| All Four Skills ≥ | FollowersMax |
|---|---|
| 60 | 6 |
| 90 | 7 |
| 120 | 8 |

## Key Synergies

- **Taming / Druidism / Veterinary** — All four skills together determine follower capacity and stable slots.
- **Shepherd's Crook** — Required tool for moving tamed animals. Crafted via Carpentry.

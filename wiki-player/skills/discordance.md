# Discordance

Discordance lets you play jarring music to weaken a target, reducing their resistances and skills. It is one of three bard combat skills and a key component of the Bard (Song) magic system.

## How to Use

Select an instrument from your pack, then target a creature or player. You must first pass a Musicianship check before the Discordance check is attempted.

On success, the target takes a multi-layered debuff — reduced resistances and skills, slower swing speed, and lower damage output. Each successful use extends the duration by 10 seconds. On failure, a reduced daze effect applies for 4 seconds.

## How to Train

Target progressively harder creatures. The difficulty depends on the instrument's difficulty for the target. Higher Musicianship makes it easier.

## Key Synergies

- **Musicianship** — Required before every Discordance attempt. Musicianship above 100 reduces Discordance difficulty by 0.5 per point above 100.
- **Peacemaking / Provocation** — All three bard skills feed into the combined `MusicSkill()` total that powers every bard song's effects.
- **Magic Resistance** — Players can resist Discordance with Magic Resistance.

## Tips

- Discordance is most effective against groups — weaken multiple targets before engaging.
- Against very difficult creatures, the effect is halved. Consider combining with bard songs for maximum debuff.
- Elixirs of Discordance can provide a temporary boost for boss fights.

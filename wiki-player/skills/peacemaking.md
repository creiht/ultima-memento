# Peacemaking

Peacemaking uses soothing music to calm hostile creatures, forcing them to stop attacking. It is one of three bard combat skills and a component of the Bard (Song) magic system.

## How to Use

Select an instrument from your pack, then use Peacemaking. You have two modes:

- **Area Peacemaking** (target yourself) — Calms all hostile creatures in range for `Musicianship / 10` seconds
- **Single-target** (target a creature) — Longer pacification, up to 120 seconds

Against players, Peacemaking causes paralysis instead of pacification.

## How to Train

Target progressively harder creatures. Area peacemaking is good for training on groups but carries a 50% risk of stopping the song if a creature's check fails.

## Key Synergies

- **Musicianship** — Required before every Peacemaking attempt. Area pacification duration = Musicianship / 10 seconds. Musicianship above 100 reduces difficulty.
- **Discordance / Provocation** — All three bard skills feed into the combined MusicSkill total that powers every bard song.
- **Magic Resistance** — Players can resist Peacemaking with Magic Resistance.

## Tips

- Single-target mode gives significantly longer pacification than area mode — use it on the most dangerous creature in a group.
- Area Peacemaking carries a 50% risk of stopping the song entirely if a creature's check fails.

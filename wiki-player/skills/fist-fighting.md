# Fist Fighting

Fist Fighting governs unarmed combat and is the core skill of the Mystic (Monk) magic system. It is used when no weapon is equipped or when wearing pugilist gloves.

## How to Use

Fist Fighting is a passive weapon skill. When unarmed, your skill determines accuracy and defense. Non-weapon abilities like Disarm and Paralyzing Blow can be used without a Tactics requirement when fighting with fists.

## How to Train

Attack unarmed or with pugilist gloves. Fist Fighting gains passively on every strike.

## Mystic (Monk) Magic

Fist Fighting is both the casting and damage skill for all Mystic spells. To use any Mystic spell, you must simultaneously meet:

- **100 base Fist Fighting**
- **100 base Meditation**
- **100 base Focus**
- Wearing a **Mystic Monk Robe** with no other armor

Key Mystic spells include Psionic Blast (damage), Gentle Touch (healing), Quivering Palm (paralyze), and Purity of Body (poison cure).

## Key Synergies

- **Meditation / Focus** — Both must be at 100+ alongside Fist Fighting to cast Mystic spells.
- **Anatomy** — Contributes to unarmed defend value. At 80+ in both, you can use the Stun move.
- **Spiritualism** — Spiritualism at 50+ allows Mystic spellcasting without monk robes.

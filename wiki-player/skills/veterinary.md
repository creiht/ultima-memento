# Veterinary

Veterinary is the healing and resurrection skill for animals and monsters. It mirrors the Healing-Anatomy relationship but applies to creatures instead of players.

## How to Use

Apply bandages to injured animals or monsters. Veterinary is the primary healing skill for creatures, with Druidism as the secondary skill. Healing completes in 2 seconds regardless of Dexterity.

## How to Train

Apply bandages to injured animals and monsters. Gain fires passively on each application.

## Healing and Resurrection

- **Normal healing** — Heal amount scales with average of Veterinary and Druidism, plus the monster's max hits.
- **Poison cure** — Requires Veterinary >= 60 AND Druidism >= 60. Cure chance decreases with poison level.
- **Resurrection** — Requires Veterinary >= 80 AND Druidism >= 80. Dead pets can be brought back with bandages.

## Key Synergies

- **Druidism** — Primary synergy. Both skills scale healing, poison cure, and resurrection. Required together for herbalism crafting.
- **Taming / Herding** — All four skills (Taming, Druidism, Veterinary, Herding) determine follower capacity and stable slots.

## Tips

- Keep bandages on hand when out taming — your pets will take damage in combat.
- Get Veterinary and Druidism to 80+ if you want to be able to resurrect dead pets.
- Higher Veterinary also increases egg hatch refunds and Herbalist Shoppe stock levels.

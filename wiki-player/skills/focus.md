# Focus

Focus passively boosts both stamina and mana regeneration. Unlike Meditation, its mana bonus works even while wearing heavy armor. It is also one of the three prerequisites for the Mystic (Monk) magic specialization.

## How It Works

Focus provides passive bonuses to your resource recovery:

| Focus Skill | Stam Regen Bonus | Mana Regen Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | +5.0 per tick | +2.5 per tick |
| 100 | +10.0 per tick | +5.0 per tick |

These bonuses are applied automatically during each regen tick — no skill use required.

## How to Train

Focus trains completely passively. Each time your stamina or mana regenerates, there is a chance to gain Focus skill. Simply existing and recovering resources will raise Focus over time.

## Mystic (Monk) Magic

Focus is one of three skills required (alongside Meditation and Fist Fighting, each at 100+) to qualify for the Mystic (Monk) magic specialization.

## Key Synergies

- **Meditation** — Both boost mana regen. Meditation provides a larger active bonus while meditating (but is blocked by armor). Focus works as a passive always-on bonus regardless of equipment.
- **Fist Fighting / Meditation** — Required alongside Focus for Mystic magic.

## Tips

- Focus is one of the easiest skills to train since it gains passively with no action required.
- Its mana regen bonus stacks with Meditation's active bonus, making both highly valuable for spellcasters.

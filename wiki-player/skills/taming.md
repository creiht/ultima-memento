# Taming

Taming lets you domesticate wild creatures so they serve you as loyal pets and combat companions.

## How to Use

Target a tameable creature with the skill. The creature will be tamed after a brief taming period (9-12 seconds). You must remain within 6 tiles and maintain line of sight.

Each creature has a minimum taming skill requirement. The difficulty increases by 6 per previous owner.

**Warning:** 95% of untamed creatures will become hostile when you attempt to tame them.

## How to Train

Tame progressively harder creatures. Druidism gains passively during the first tame of each creature type.

## Pet Control and Loyalty

Your Taming skill determines your pet's obedience chance. Higher Taming and lower loyalty requirements mean better control. Loyalty naturally decays over time — feed your pet regularly to maintain it.

## Bonding

Bonding lets your pet survive death and return to you. Requires Taming >= the pet's MinTameSkill. Bonded pets have slower loyalty decay and can return after death.

## Follower Capacity

Your maximum follower count depends on the combined levels of four skills:
- **All four at 60+:** 6 followers
- **All four at 90+:** 7 followers
- **All four at 120+:** 8 followers

The four skills are: Taming, Druidism, Veterinary, and Herding.

## Key Synergies

- **Druidism** — Reduces taming difficulty by Druidism/5. Gains passively during first-time tames.
- **Veterinary** — Heals and resurrects your pets. Both contribute to follower capacity and stable slots.
- **Herding** — Leads pets to new locations. Contributes to follower capacity and stable slots.

## Tips

- Bring food for your pets to maintain loyalty.
- Bonding your pet (at level 3 or by feeding) ensures they survive death.
- Some creatures must be subdued (reduced below 10% health) before they can be tamed.

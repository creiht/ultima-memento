# Arms Lore

Arms Lore lets you identify unidentified weapons and armor, and passively reduces durability loss on your equipped gear during combat.

## How to Use

Target an unidentified item with the skill to reveal its properties, enchantments, and quality. You can identify weapons, armor, and most equipment types.

Arms Lore also passively protects your equipped gear from wearing out. During combat, when your weapon or armor would lose durability, there is a chance the skill will prevent the loss. The chance increases with your skill level.

## How to Train

Target unidentified or identified weapons, armor, and other equipment to gain skill. The difficulty scales with the item being examined.

## Key Synergies

- **Tactics** — Both add damage to your attacks. Arms Lore provides a bonus to base weapon damage.
- **Mercantile** — Both identify items but cover different categories. Mercantile identifies containers, decorative items, and misc. goods, while Arms Lore handles weapons and armor.
- **Exceptional Crafting** — Higher Arms Lore gives exceptional crafted weapons more Damage Increase and exceptional armor more resistance points.

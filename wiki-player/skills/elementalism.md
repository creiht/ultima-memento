# Elementalism

Elementalism is a reagent-free magic system organized into 8 Spheres themed to Air, Earth, Fire, or Water. It uses an Elemental Spellbook trinket instead of a standard spellbook.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** Elementalism
- **Spellbook:** Elemental Spellbook (trinket slot, 4 element variants)
- **No reagents needed** — spells consume mana and stamina instead

## How to Use

Cast Elementalism spells from your Elemental Spellbook. The skill governs cast success, spell power, resist checks, and effect duration. Each sphere has minimum skill thresholds.

**Important:** Elementalism is mutually exclusive with Magery and Necromancy. Having 1 point in any of these locks out the others' spellcasting.

## How to Train

Cast Elementalism spells from your Elemental Spellbook. Each cast rolls a skill gain check.

**Stamina management is critical** — Elementalism spells consume both mana AND stamina. Train Focus and Meditation, and avoid heavy encumbrance.

## Key Features

- **No reagents** — only stamina and mana costs
- **Armor fizzle penalty** — heavier armor increases spell failure chance. Mage armor and Spell Channeling bypass this entirely.
- **Staff damage bonus** — staves and scepters gain a damage bonus from Elementalism (up to +6.25 at GM)
- **90+ threshold** — Elemental Sanctuary spell can be cast in dungeons

## Key Synergies

- **Focus** — Stamina regeneration is critical for Elementalism's dual mana+stamina cost.
- **Meditation** — Mana regeneration for sustained casting.
- **Magic Resistance** — All Elementalism spells train Magic Resist when resisted.

## Tips

- Elementalism is a strong alternative to Magery for players who want powerful magic without reagent management.
- The stamina cost means you need to invest in Focus or carry Elixir of Stamina for prolonged casting sessions.
- Invest in Mage Armor to avoid the fizzle penalty from regular armor.

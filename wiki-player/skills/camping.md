# Camping

Camping is a survival skill for setting up temporary shelters in the wilderness. It enables lighting campfires, sleeping in bedrolls, and using tents that let you restore HP and stamina while bypassing hunger and thirst decay.

## How to Use

- **Campfire** — Use Kindling to light a fire. Nearby players who aren't hungry or thirsty restore 2 HP and 2 stamina per second.
- **Bedroll** — Use a bedroll near a campfire to rest. Successful rest bypasses hunger and thirst decay.
- **Camping Tent** — Teleports you to a private instanced tent. Requires 40+ Camping outdoors, 90+ in dungeons.

Camping at 90+ also gates access to Ranger Outpost housing.

## How to Train

Use Kindling, bedrolls, or camping tents repeatedly. Each use fires multiple skill checks at once, making Camping one of the faster skills to train.

## Key Synergies

- **Tracking** — Both skills provide access to Ranger Outpost housing (either 90+ works).
- **Cooking** — Campfires enable food cooking. The Barbaric Talisman bonds Camping + Cooking together for a massive bonus.
- **Stealth / Hiding** — Safe camping requires no enemies nearby. Assassin's Guild members have these skills bonded together.

## Tips

- Bring Kindling and a bedroll for long dungeon sessions — you can rest and bypass hunger without returning to town.
- Camping at 90+ opens up Ranger Outpost housing, a valuable option for players who want outdoor placement.

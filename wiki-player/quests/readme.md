# Quest Overview

Ultima Memento features over 20 quest types — from simple bounties to epic multi-stage artifact hunts. Quests are a primary source of gold, items, fame, and companions.

## How to Check Quests

- Type **`[quests`** in chat to open your Quest Log.
- Active quests are listed with titles and progress.
- Click a quest to see detailed objectives.

## Starting Quests

- **Talk to NPCs** — double-click quest givers in towns for offered quests.
- **Quest bulletin boards** — find them in taverns and docks for bounty hunts.
- **Find quest items** — journals, cubes, and schematics in dungeons often start exploration quests.

## Quest Objectives

| Objective Type | How It Works |
|---|---|
| Kill creatures | Automatic progress tracking |
| Collect items | Items must be marked as Quest Items in your backpack |
| Deliver items | Carry an item to a destination NPC |
| Craft items | Craft specific items and mark as Quest Items |

## Completing Quests

1. Complete all objectives.
2. Return to the quest giver NPC and double-click them.
3. Accept the reward — items go to your backpack.
4. You can have up to **10 active quests** at once.

## Tips

- **Keep quest items in your backpack** — many quest triggers check your pack.
- **Bring a sextant** on epic/courier quests for map navigation.
- **Higher Luck** improves rewards from treasure-related quests.
- **Check cooldowns** — some quest givers won't offer the same quest again until the cooldown expires.

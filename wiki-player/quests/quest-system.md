# How Quests Work

Quests are one of the best ways to earn gold, items, and experience in Ultima Memento. This page explains the quest system in detail.

## Quest Log

Type **`[quests`** in chat to open the Quest Log gump. It shows all active quests with progress. Click the arrow next to a quest to see detailed objectives.

## Accepting Quests

1. **Double-click an NPC** who offers quests — a Quest Offer gump appears with objectives and rewards.
2. Click **Accept** to take the quest.
3. You can have up to **10 active quests** at once.

## Quest Objectives

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Kill | Slay a specific number of creatures — tracked automatically |
| Collect | Gather items and mark them as Quest Items in your backpack |
| Deliver | Carry an item to a destination NPC |
| Craft | Craft specific items and mark as Quest Items |
| Gain Skill | Increase a specific skill |

For collect/craft objectives, items must remain at the **top level of your backpack** and be toggled as Quest Items (use the crosshair button in the Quest Log).

## Completing Quests

When all objectives are met, you hear a completion sound. Return to the quest giver and double-click them to receive your reward. If your backpack is full, you cannot claim the reward until you make space.

## Quest Types

Ultima Memento has two quest systems running in parallel:

**NPC Quests (ML-style):** Given by quest-giving NPCs with gumps showing objectives and rewards. These include chains (completing one quest unlocks the next).

**Classic Quests (boards and items):** Bulletin boards in towns offer bounties. Special items found in dungeons (journals, cubes, schematics) start exploration quests. Progress is tracked through the item itself.

## Rewards

Rewards vary by quest:
- **Gold** — most common
- **Items** — weapons, armor, unique quest rewards
- **Fame & Karma** — earned or lost depending on quest alignment
- **Companions** — henchmen, golems, robots, flesh golems
- **Stat/Skill bonuses** — the Codex grants +25 Intelligence and +100 in two skills

## Tips

- **Keep quest items accessible** — store them at the top of your backpack, not in containers.
- **Talk to citizens** for rumors about Major quest items.
- **Check cooldowns** — some quests have timers before they're offered again.
- **Higher Luck** improves rewards from treasure quests.

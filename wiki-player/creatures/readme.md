## Taming Overview

To tame creatures, use the **Taming** skill on a wild creature. Successful taming adds the creature to your stable. Higher Taming skill lets you tame tougher creatures.

- **Animals** are the easiest tameables and great for new players.
- **Dragons** are powerful but require high Taming skill.
- **Reptilian** creatures offer a good variety of tameable options.
- **Mystical** creatures include some of the most unique and powerful tameables.
- **Insects** and **Hellish** creatures offer mid-tier tameable options.

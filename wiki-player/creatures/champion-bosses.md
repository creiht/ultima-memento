# Champion Bosses

Champion spawns are large-scale group encounters with escalating waves of enemies culminating in a powerful champion boss.

## How Champion Spawns Work

1. **Activate a champion altar** — find an altar in the world and interact with it to start the spawn.
2. **Wave 1** — a group of weaker enemies spawns. Kill them all.
3. **Wave 2** — tougher enemies appear.
4. **Wave 3+** — continue until the champion boss arrives.
5. **The Champion** — a unique boss with high HP and powerful abilities.
6. **Rewards** — defeating the champion gives special loot drops.

## Rewards

Defeating champion bosses grants special loot including:
- **High-quality gear** — weapons and armor with good stats
- **Unique items** — champion reward items not found elsewhere
- **Champion skulls** — collectible items that can be traded or used
- **Gold** — significant gold payouts

## Tips

- **Bring a group** — champions are designed for multiple players. Soloing is very difficult.
- **Heal and buff** — make sure your group has healing and resist buffs ready.
- **Assign roles** — have tanks, healers, and DPS in your group.
- **Stock up on reagents** — champion fights drain mana and stamina fast.
- **Check the altar before starting** — some altars have conditions or requirements.
- **Champion skulls are valuable** — keep them or trade them for rewards.

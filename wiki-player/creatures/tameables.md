# Tameable Creatures

Notable creatures you can tame using the Taming skill. These are organized by family and difficulty.

## Easy Tameables (Good for Beginners)

| Creature | Family | Why Tame It |
|---|---|---|
| Wolf | Animals | Reliable fighter, easy to tame |
| Bear | Animals | High HP, good tank |
| Cat | Animals | Low upkeep, quick |
| Rabbit | Animals | Mount option, easy to tame |
| Bird | Animals | Flying mount, very easy |

## Medium Tameables

| Creature | Family | Why Tame It |
|---|---|---|
| Lizardman | Reptilian | Good fighter, decent resistance |
| Scorpion | Insects | Poison attack, decent tank |
| Nightmare | Hellish | Flying mount, good damage |
| Unicorn | Mystical | Healing aura, beautiful mount |
| Hydra | Dragons | Multiple heads = multiple attacks |

## Hard Tameables (High Skill Required)

| Creature | Family | Why Tame It |
|---|---|---|
| Dragon | Dragons | One of the strongest mounts |
| Fire Elemental | Elementals | Fire damage, tanky |
| Daemon | Demons | Powerful fighter |
| Phoenix | Mystical | Revives once, fire resistance |
| Wyrm | Dragons | Massive HP and damage |

## Taming Tips

- **Train your pet** — tameable creatures improve with use, just like player skills.
- **Feed your pet** — tamed creatures need food. Different creatures eat different things.
- **Use Veterinary** — heal injured pets with the Veterinary skill.
- **Stables** — you get extra stabled pet slots (default 5). Use them wisely.
- **Bonding** — pets take time to bond with you (default 7 days). Until then, they may not fight as effectively.

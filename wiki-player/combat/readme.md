# Combat Overview

Ultima Memento uses a real-time combat system with weapon-based attacks, special abilities, and magic support.

## Combat Basics

Combat is real-time: you select a target and your character attacks automatically. Your attack speed depends on your weapon and Dexterity. Damage is modified by your weapon type, Strength, Tactics skill, and the target's resistances.

## Weapon Types

| Type | Examples | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Slashing | Swords, Axes, Maces | General melee combat |
| Piercing | Spears, Kryss, Sais | Reaching behind shields |
| Blunt | Clubs, Maces | Armor penetration |
| Fisticuffs | Unarmed | Specialized builds |
| Ranged | Bows, Crossbows, Thrown | Distance combat |

Your primary hit skill determines how accurately you strike:
- **Swordsmanship** — bladed melee weapons
- **Bludgeoning** — clubs, maces, blunt weapons
- **Fencing** — spears, kryss, sai, piercing weapons
- **Fist Fighting** — unarmed combat
- **Marksmanship** — all ranged weapons

## Special Attacks

Most weapons have a **Special Attack** button that opens a menu of abilities based on your weapon type and Tactics skill. Higher Tactics unlocks more powerful special attacks. Abilities include:

- **Crushing Blow** — a heavy hit that can stun
- **Disarm** — temporarily remove the opponent's weapon
- **Bleed Attack** — deals damage over time
- **Mortal Strike** — a devastating finishing move
- **And many more** — each weapon type has its own set

See [Weapon Abilities](weapon-abilities.md) for the full list.

## Combat Tips

- **Manage your stamina** — every attack and special ability costs stamina. Don't spam abilities.
- **Use Tactics** — higher Tactics increases special attack options and damage.
- **Know when to back off** — if you're low on HP or stamina, retreat and use potions.
- **Respect notoriety** — attacking certain creatures or characters will draw unwanted attention.
- **Team up** — many creatures are too tough alone, especially in dungeons.

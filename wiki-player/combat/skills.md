# Essential Skills

This page covers the most important skills for combat, gathering, and day-to-day play. For a complete skill list and detailed breakdowns, see [Skills](./).

## Combat Skills

| Skill | What It Does |
|---|---|
| [Tactics](../skills/tactics.md) | Increases damage and unlocks more powerful weapon special attacks |
| [Healing](../skills/healing.md) | Use bandages to restore HP, cure poison, and stop bleeding |
| [Parrying](../skills/parrying.md) | Block incoming melee and magical damage with a shield |
| [Anatomy](../skills/anatomy.md) | Evaluate a target's stats to find weaknesses |

## Magic Skills

| Skill | What It Does |
|---|---|
| [Magery](../skills/magery.md) | Cast arcane spells from the Magery magic school |
| [Meditation](../skills/meditation.md) | Recover mana faster (essential for spellcasters) |
| [Necromancy](../skills/necromancy.md) | Cast dark spells of death and curses |
| [Knightship](../skills/knightship.md) | Cast Paladin and Death Knight abilities |
| [Druidism](../skills/druidism.md) | Examine creatures; governs druidic herbalism and spells |
| [Psychology](../skills/psychology.md) | Evaluates intelligence; enhances nearly all magic systems |
| [Magic Resistance](../skills/magic-resistance.md) | Passively resist hostile spell effects |

## Tips

- **Prioritize healing and meditation** — nearly every build benefits from self-recovery.
- **Don't ignore Tactics** — it boosts damage and unlocks special attacks for all weapon types.
- **Carry bandages** — even without the Healing skill, you can use bandages on yourself.
- **Learn Stealth + Hiding** — even melee fighters benefit from being able to avoid unwanted encounters.

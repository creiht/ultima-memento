# Weapon Abilities

Weapon abilities (also called special attacks) are powerful attacks that cost stamina and mana, activated through a menu on your weapon. Your Tactics skill determines which abilities you can use and how effective they are.

## How Abilities Work

- Open the abilities menu by clicking the **Special Attack** button on your weapon or using the relevant command.
- Each ability has a **stamina cost** and a **mana cost**.
- Higher Tactics unlocks additional ability tiers.
- Abilities are weapon-type specific — not all weapons have the same abilities.

## Basic Abilities

These are available to most weapon types at low Tactics levels:

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Crushing Blow** | A heavy blow that deals increased damage and may stun the target |
| **Disarm** | Temporarily remove the opponent's weapon or shield |
| **Bleed Attack** | Inflict a wound that deals damage over several seconds |
| **Concussion Blow** | A powerful hit that stuns and deals significant damage |
| **Reflect Blade** | Deflect the next incoming attack back at the attacker |
| **Whirlwind Attack** | Strike all nearby enemies in a sweeping attack |

## Advanced Abilities

These require higher Tactics and are often weapon-type specific:

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Mortal Strike** | A devastating strike that deals massive damage |
| **Armor Ignore** | Your next attack bypasses part of the target's armor |
| **Dual Wield** | Attack with both weapons simultaneously for doubled strikes |
| **Shadow Strike** | A sneak attack from hiding that deals bonus damage |
| **Power Strike** | Channel your strength into a single devastating blow |
| **Snipe** | (Ranged) A precise shot that ignores armor |
| **Lightning Strike** | (Blades) A lightning-fast series of slashes |
| **Nerve Strike** | (Blades) Attack that reduces the target's stats temporarily |
| **Paralyzing Blow** | (Blunt) Stuns the target and reduces their effectiveness |

## Weapon Type Notes

- **Swords** — generally have the best variety of abilities
- **Maces/Clubs** — excel at stunning and armor-piercing abilities
- **Spears** — have reach-based abilities and anti-magic options
- **Bows/Crossbows** — ranged abilities like snipe, multi-shot, and piercing shots
- **Unarmed** — unique abilities focused on grappling, disarming, and stat reduction

## Tips

- **Always invest in Tactics** — it directly increases your special attack power and unlocks more abilities.
- **Watch your stamina** — abilities are expensive. Don't burn through stamina on weak targets.
- **Save big abilities for tough fights** — Crushing Blow and Mortal Strike can turn the tide against powerful enemies.
- **Combine abilities with magic** — buff yourself before engaging, then unleash abilities.
- **Study your weapon** — each weapon type has different abilities. Check the abilities menu before picking up a new weapon.

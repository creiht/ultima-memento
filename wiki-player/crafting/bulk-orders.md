# Bulk Orders

Bulk orders are an automated mass-crafting system that rewards you for turning in large quantities of crafted items.

## How It Works

1. **Receive a bulk order** from a vendor (usually a town blacksmith, baker, or crafter).
2. **Craft the required items** — the order specifies the item type, quantity, and material tier.
3. **Turn in the items** — return them to the vendor for rewards.
4. **Complete sets** — finishing a full set of bulk orders unlocks bonus rewards.

## Rewards

- **Gold** — basic payment for each set turned in
- **Special items** — completing sets gives access to unique, high-quality gear
- **Reputation** — increases your standing with crafting vendors

## Tips

- **High-tier materials give better rewards** — use the highest tier you can craft with.
- **Plan your crafting** — check the order before crafting so you make the right item.
- **Bulk craft with friends** — split orders and help each other complete sets faster.
- **Keep a supply of materials** — bulk orders often require large quantities (10-20+ items).

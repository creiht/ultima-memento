# Gathering Skills

Gathering skills are used to harvest raw resources from the world. These resources feed into crafting professions.

## Mining

Extract ore, granite, sand, and gems from rock formations and veins in the world.

- **Skill required:** Mining
- **What you get:** Iron ore (and higher-tier ores as you find them), granite, sand, gems
- **Where to find:** Caves, mountains, dungeons, mines throughout all facets
- **Tips:** Higher Mining skill gives better yield and chance to find rare ores. Rich veins are rare high-yield nodes.

## Lumberjacking

Harvest wood from trees found in forests and wilderness areas.

- **Skill required:** Lumberjacking
- **What you get:** Logs, boards, bark fragments
- **Where to find:** Forests, groves, and tree clusters on all facets
- **Tips:** Higher skill yields more wood and grants chances for rare wood types (Elven, Ebony, Ghostwood). Rich sparkle nodes are rare high-yield trees.

## Fishing

Catch fish and special items from bodies of water.

- **Skill required:** Fishing (improved by Seafaring)
- **What you get:** Fish for Cooking, treasure, materials
- **Where to find:** Lakes, rivers, oceans, ponds
- **Tips:** Seafaring skill improves fishing yield and lets you fish from boats. Higher skill catches rarer fish and more treasure.

## Grave Robbing

Dig bones, reagents, and relics from graves and burial sites.

- **Skill required:** Grave Robbing
- **What you get:** Bones, reagents, relics, sometimes gold
- **Where to find:** Graveyards, burial grounds, catacombs, cemeteries
- **Tips:** Some graves are more fruitful than others. Relics found here are valuable — always check what you dig up.

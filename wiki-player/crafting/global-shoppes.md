# Global Shoppes

Global Shoppes lets you run your own craft business — take walk-in customer contracts and fulfill bulk craft orders for Gold, Reputation, and Points redeemable for unique rewards.

## How It Works

1. **Set up a shop** in your house or a designated area.
2. **Serve walk-in customers** — they'll request specific crafted items.
3. **Accept bulk craft orders** — larger orders with bigger rewards.
4. **Get paid** in Gold, Reputation, and Points.
5. **Redeem Points** for unique rewards from the shoppes reward pool.

## Rewards

- **Gold** — per-order payment
- **Reputation** — increases with consistent service
- **Points** — accumulated across all orders, redeemable for unique gear and items
- **Higher reputation = better orders** — high-rep shops get more lucrative contracts

## Tips

- **Stock your shop** with common items so you're ready for walk-in customers.
- **Accept bulk orders** — they pay much better per item than walk-ins.
- **Maintain high reputation** — the better your reputation, the more profitable your shop becomes.
- **Check Points rewards regularly** — unique items can be worth hoarding Points for.

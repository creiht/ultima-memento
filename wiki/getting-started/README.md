# Getting Started

Welcome to Ultima Memento! This guide covers everything a new player needs to know to create a character and begin adventuring.

## What is Ultima Memento?

Ultima Memento is a custom Ultima Online server emulator featuring a massive world with multiple continents (facets), 19 magic systems, 22 crafting professions, over 24 quest types, and hundreds of creatures to battle. The server uses an Avatar progression system that tracks your lifetime achievements across characters.

## Character Creation

When you create a new character, you will:

1. **Choose a name** — must be 2–16 characters, unique across all players.
2. **Distribute starting stats** — you have 90 points to spread across Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence (minimum 10, maximum 60 each). See [Attributes](attributes.md) for details on what each stat does.
3. **Pick a starter template or custom skills** — choose a preset profession or allocate 100 skill points yourself across any available skills.
4. **Customize appearance** — select gender, skin hue, hair style, hair color, and facial hair.

Your character will begin in the **Gypsy Forest** in Sosaria with starter equipment, a light source, some food, a water pitcher, a dagger or knife, and starting gold (500–600 gold).

## Starter Templates

Templates give you a pre-built set of 4 skills at 30 each and optimized starting stats. You also receive skill-appropriate items in your backpack.

| Template | Starting Stats (Str/Dex/Int) | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | 50 / 30 / 10 | Swords 30, Tactics 30, Parry 30, Healing 30 |
| **Mage** | 35 / 10 / 45 | Magery 30, Psychology 30, Mercantile 30, Fist Fighting 30 |
| **Archer** | 35 / 40 / 15 | Marksmanship 30, Tactics 30, Bowcraft 30, Lumberjacking 30 |
| **Knight** | 50 / 25 / 15 | Knightship 30, Tactics 30, Healing 30, Bludgeoning 30 |
| **Ninja** | 40 / 30 / 20 | Ninjitsu 30, Hiding 30, Stealth 30, Fencing 30 |
| **Bard** | 40 / 30 / 20 | Musicianship 30, Peacemaking 30, Discordance 30, Provocation 30 |
| **Druid** | 30 / 20 / 40 | Druidism 30, Taming 30, Veterinary 30, Herding 30 |
| **Custom** | Player's choice (90 total) | Player's choice (100 total across any skills) |

When selecting Custom, you can distribute up to 100 skill points across any combination of skills (max 50 per skill).

### Starter Equipment by Skill

Each skill you start with comes with relevant equipment in a labeled bag. For example:
- **Magery** — Spellbook, Heal scroll, Magic Arrow scroll, a bag of reagents
- **Swords** — A random fast sword (Bokuto, Cleaver, or Cutlass)
- **Healing** — Scissors and 200 bandages
- **Taming** — A random caged animal companion
- **Mining** — Two spades
- **Blacksmithy** — Smith hammer and 50 iron ingots

## The Avatar System

Ultima Memento features an **Avatar progression system** that persists across your character's lives. As you play, you earn coins from killing creatures and completing combat quests. These coins can be spent on upgrades such as:

- **Stat cap increases** — raise your stat cap beyond the starting 250 (1 point per level, up to 150 levels)
- **Skill cap increases** — increase your maximum skill points (10 points per level, up to 70 levels)
- **Skill gain rate boosts** — gain skills faster
- **Coin gain rate boosts** — earn coins faster
- **Unlocks** — monster races, fugitive mode, temptations, and more

Each character also receives a **Rival Faction** — a randomly assigned enemy creature type. Killing rivals provides bonus coins (50% extra) until your rivalry is fulfilled.

## First Steps & Tips

1. **Open your Avatar Book** — it's in your backpack. This tracks your progression and lets you spend coins on upgrades.
2. **Explore the Gypsy Forest** — this is a relatively safe starting area. Talk to NPCs and explore.
3. **Eat and drink** — your character gets hungry and thirsty. Carry food and water. You won't get hungry inside banks, inns, or houses.
4. **Get a light source** — you start with a torch, lantern, or candle. Dungeons are dark!
5. **Visit healers and shrines** — if you die, a directional arrow guides you to the nearest healer or shrine. See [Death & Resurrection](death-and-resurrection.md).
6. **Join a guild** — guild membership costs 2,000 gold base, increasing if you've been in guilds before. Guild members get access to special crafting enhancements.
7. **Check your stats** — Stat cap starts at 250. Stats gain passively through use, with gains checked every 7.5 minutes.

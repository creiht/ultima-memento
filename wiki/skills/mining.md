# Mining

Mining is a harvest skill used to extract ore and stone from the environment.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Primary Stat** | Strength |
| **Usage** | Active |
| **Skill Type** | Trade / Harvest |
| **Skill Check** | 0 - 125 |

## Description

Mining allows players to excavate various types of ore and stone from resource veins found in mountains, caves, and sandy areas. It is a fundamental skill for crafters, providing the raw materials needed for blacksmithing and other metal-based trades.

## How It Works

Players use a pickaxe or shovel on suitable terrain to trigger a harvest action. Higher skill levels increase the success rate and allow access to more valuable ores.

### Ore and Stone

Mining in rocky or mountainous terrain yields different ores based on the vein quality and player skill.

- **Common Ores**: Iron, Dull Copper, Copper, Bronze.
- **Rare Ores**: Gold, Agapite, Verite, Valorite.
- **Stone**: Granite and other geological materials can also be extracted.

### Sand Mining

At high skill levels (100.0+), players can mine sand in specific coastal or desert regions. This provides a unique way to gather materials.

## How to Train

- **Standard Training**: Use a pickaxe on ore veins in mountainous or cave regions.
- **High-Level Training**: Target higher-tier veins (e.g., Verite or Valorite) for better experience gains.
- **Sand Mining**: Once Mining reaches 100.0, practice mining sand in coastal areas.

## What It Affects

### Crafting & Trades

- **Blacksmithing**: Provides the primary source of metal ores required for forging weapons and armor.
- **Glassblowing**: Sand harvested via mining is a key component for glass production.

## Related Systems & Skills

### Synergies
- [Blacksmithy](../crafting/blacksmithy.md): Uses mined ores to create equipment.
- [Glassblowing](../crafting/glassblowing.md): Uses sand for crafting.

### Prerequisites / Co-requisites
- None.

## Notes
- Mining cannot be performed while mounted.
- Mining cannot be performed while polymorphed.
- Using a `Gargoyle Pickaxe` can help in mutating veins to higher tiers.

# Bushido

Bushido is the samurai's martial discipline. It governs the activation and power of Bushido combat techniques cast from a Book of Bushido — offensive strikes, defensive stances, and evasive parries.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary Stat | Strength |
| Usage | Active (activate Bushido abilities) |
| Type | Combat / Magic |

## What It Affects

- Success chance and duration of Bushido stances (Confidence, Evasion, Counter Attack).
- Bonus damage on Honorable Execution, Lightning Strike, and Momentum Strike.
- Chance to parry with a weapon while Evasion is active.
- Minimum-skill gating per ability (see [Bushido magic system](../magic/bushido.md)).

## How to Train

- Acquire a Book of Bushido and activate abilities in combat — each use rolls a skill gain check.
- Train at or near the minimum skill of each ability; moving to higher-tier techniques as you cap lower ones keeps gain rates steady.
- Only one Bushido stance (Evasion / Confidence / Counter Attack) can be active at a time.

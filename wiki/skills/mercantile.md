# Mercantile

Mercantile (Item Identification) lets you appraise and identify items, revealing their properties, enchantments, and value.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary Stat | Intelligence |
| Usage | Active (targeted) |
| Range | 8 tiles |

## How It Works

Use the skill and target any item. The item is identified via the `RelicFunctions.IDItem` system, which reveals the item's properties, magical attributes, and other details based on your skill level.

This is functionally similar to [Arms Lore](arms-lore.md) and [Tasting](tasting.md) identification but uses the Mercantile skill for the check.

## How to Train

Target items to identify them. Higher-quality and more complex items provide better skill gains at higher levels. Vendor items can also be targeted (AllowNonlocal is enabled).

## Related Skills

- [Arms Lore](arms-lore.md) - Also identifies items, specializing in weapons and armor with a passive durability bonus.
- [Tasting](tasting.md) - Detects poison in food/drink and can also identify items.

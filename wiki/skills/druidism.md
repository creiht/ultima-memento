# Druidism

Druidism (Animal Lore) lets you examine creatures to learn detailed information about their stats, skills, resistances, damage types, and taming requirements.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary Stat | Intelligence |
| Usage | Active (targeted) |
| Range | 8 tiles |
| Skill Check | 0 - 125 |

## How It Works

Use the skill and target a creature. On success, a detailed information gump opens showing:

- **Information:** Power Level, Barding Difficulty, Taming Skill Needed, Follower Slots, Sex
- **Damage Types:** Physical, Fire, Cold, Poison, Energy percentages, and Base Damage range
- **Combat Ratings:** Anatomy, Magic Resist, Poisoning, Tactics, Combat Skill
- **Lore & Knowledge:** Magery, Meditation, Psychology
- **Resistances:** Physical, Fire, Cold, Poison, Energy (with upgrade buttons for your own pets)
- **Stats:** Hits, Stamina, Mana, Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence

### For Your Own Pets

When targeting a creature you own, you get additional information:
- Pet Level and Max Level
- Available Traits
- Experience and Experience needed for next level
- Pack Instinct type
- Mood/Loyalty level
- Favorite Foods
- **Upgrade buttons** to spend traits on Hit Points, Stamina, Mana, and Resistances

### Skill Requirements

| Druidism Skill | What You Can Lore |
|---|---|
| Below 100 | Only your own tamed creatures |
| 100+ | Tamed or tameable creatures (any owner) |
| 110+ | Any creature, including untameable ones |

### Creature Type Restrictions

Druidism works only on "animal-type" creatures. It does not work on:
- Slimes, Elementals, Humanoids (Repond), Undead, Giants, Golems
- Dead pets
- Henchmen

Special mounts (Riding Dragons, Daemonic, Drakkhen, etc.) can be lored only if they are your controlled pet.

### Passive Gains During Taming

Druidism is passively checked during the [Taming](taming.md) process, providing skill gains even when not actively loring.

## How to Train

Target creatures with the skill. The difficulty check is 0-125, so any creature can provide gains. Taming wild creatures also passively trains Druidism.

## Related Skills

- [Taming](taming.md) - Tame creatures; Druidism gains passively during taming.
- [Anatomy](anatomy.md) - Evaluates humanoid physical stats.

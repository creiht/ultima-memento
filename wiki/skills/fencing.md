# Fencing

Fencing is the hit skill for piercing weapons — spears, forks, kryss, sai, lajatang, tek-agi, kama, war cleaver, and lances.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary Stat | Dexterity |
| Usage | Passive (every fencing-weapon swing) |
| Cooldown | None |

## How It Works

### Weapons That Use Fencing

Weapons that explicitly declare `DefSkill = Fencing` include:

- Spears and forks (`BaseSpear`)
- Kryss, Sai, Lance
- Tek-agi, Kama, Lajatang
- War Cleaver
- Other Oriental piercing weapons

### Hit Chance

Hit chance is calculated identically to Swordsmanship — attacker's Fencing versus the defender's active defence skill. Tactics and Strength contribute damage; Dexterity affects swing speed.

### Best-Skill Pool

Fencing is included in the `UseBestSkill` pool alongside Swordsmanship, Bludgeoning, Marksmanship, and Parrying when resolving weapon ability tiers on adaptive or multi-skill weapons.

### Poison Synergy

Many fencing weapons (kryss, lance, spears) are commonly used with [Poisoning](poisoning.md) because they deal piercing damage that applies poison coatings effectively.

## How to Train

Attack with any fencing weapon. Fencing gains passively on every swing.

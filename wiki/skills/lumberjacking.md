# Lumberjacking

Lumberjacking is a harvest skill used to fell trees and collect wood for crafting and construction.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Primary Stat** | Strength |
| **Usage** | Active |
| **Skill Type** | Trade / Harvest |
| **Skill Check** | 0 - 125 |

## Description

Lumberjacking allows players to cut down trees and harvest wood from forests and wooded areas. It is an essential skill for many woodworking and construction-based professions.

## How It Works

Players use an axe or a chainsaw to harvest wood from trees. The quality and quantity of wood obtained depend on the player's Lumberjacking skill and the type of tree being harvested.

## How to Train

- **Standard Training**: Use an axe on common trees in forested regions.
- **Advanced Training**: Use specialized tools like a chainsaw on high-value trees to increase efficiency and experience gains.

## What It Affects

### Crafting & Trades

- **Carpentry**: Provides the primary source of wood for furniture, structures, and wooden items.
- **Bowfletching**: Provides wood used in the crafting of bows and other ranged weapons.

## Related Systems & Skills

### Synergies
- [Carpentry](../crafting/carpentry.md): Uses harvested wood for crafting.
- [Bowfletching](../crafting/bowfletching.md): Uses wood for crafting bows.

### Prerequisites / Co-requisites
- None.

## Notes
- Using a chainsaw can significantly improve the speed and efficiency of wood harvesting.
- High-quality wood can sometimes be obtained through "Rich Lumberjacking" mechanics.

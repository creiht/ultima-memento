# Lord Oaks 

Lord Oaks is a Champion Boss who leads the Forest Lord champion spawn. A powerful fey creature aligned with nature, he fights alongside his queen and summons waves of pixies to defend himself. Unlike most champions, Lord Oaks fights evil creatures (FightMode.Evil).

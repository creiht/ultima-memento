# Champion Bosses

Champion Bosses are the ultimate enemies in Champion Spawn encounters. When players defeat enough creatures in a champion spawn area, the champion boss appears as the final challenge.

[Back to Creature Index](../README.md)

## Overview

All champion bosses inherit from `BaseChampion`, which provides:

- **Can Move Over Obstacles** — Champions can walk through blocking objects
- **Can Destroy Obstacles** — Champions can break through barriers
- **Gold Percent** — Controls gold drop percentage (default: 100%)
- **Power Scroll Rewards** — Champions drop Power Scrolls (default: 1)
- **Boss Item Reward Chance** — Chance to drop boss-specific items (default: 100%)
- **Artifact Reward Chance** — Chance to drop artifact items
- **Treasure Chest Reward Chance** — Chance to spawn a treasure chest

Champions can drop decorative items (monster statuettes, tiles) and Power Scrolls as special rewards.

# Barracoon the piper

Barracoon the Piper is a Champion Boss who leads the Vermin Horde champion spawn. A sinister human figure dressed in colorful jester-like garb, he commands rats and slimes. He is known for his ability to polymorph attacking players into rats, disarming them in the process.

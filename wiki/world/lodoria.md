# Lodoria

Lodoria is a vast continent featuring many classic Ultima Online dungeons alongside original content. It is the second major land mass and offers a wide variety of environments from frozen tundras to volcanic regions.

## Sub-Region: Skara Brae

Skara Brae is a quest-driven area located on the Lodoria map. It has special entry requirements and quest objectives that must be completed before characters can leave. A warning is displayed before entering.

## Unique Features

- **Classic UO Dungeons**: Many of the original Ultima Online dungeons (Covetous, Deceit, Despise, Destard, Hythloth, Shame, Wrong) are located here
- **Safari Animals**: Disabled by default
- **Diverse Environments**: Ranges from frozen wastes to volcanic caves
- **Door Generation**: Doors are auto-generated for Lodoria dungeons

# Serpent Island

## Special Materials & Harvests

| Resource | Details |
|---|---|
| Standard Ore (Iron–Valorite) | Available at any mountain or cave wall |
| **Obsidian ore** | Mining Agapite/Verite/Valorite veins anywhere on this map has a **50% chance** to yield Obsidian ore instead. Obsidian requires 105.0+ Blacksmithing to craft with and offers +5 Phy resist plus Fire+20/Energy+10 weapon bonuses. |
| Standard Wood (Regular–Walnut) | Available at any tree |
| **Shipwreck Fishing** | 4 wreck sites at: (504, 1931), (1472, 1776), (1560, 579), (1328, 144) |

See [Mining](../crafting/mining.md) for full Obsidian stats. See [Fishing](../crafting/fishing.md) for shipwreck loot details.

## Unique Features

- **Land-Specific Resources**: Obsidian and other rare materials can only be found here; merchants on Serpent Island may stock obsidian items
- **Safari Animals**: 50% chance to spawn safari animals (elephants, giraffes, cheetahs, zebras) by default
- **Dynamic Weather**: Rain storms sweep across the island
- **Vice & Virtue Dungeons**: Eight dungeons named after virtues of evil, each with themed challenges

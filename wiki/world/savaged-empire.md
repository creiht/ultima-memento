# Savaged Empire

The Savaged Empire is a primal jungle land filled with tribal villages, ancient ruins, and monster-infested dungeons. It features a variety of unique environments from dense jungles to frozen peaks.

## Unique Features

- **Safari Animals**: 50% chance to spawn safari animals (elephants, giraffes, cheetahs, zebras) by default
- **Tribal Culture**: Two tribal villages (Barako and Kurak) with unique NPC merchants
- **Dynamic Weather**: Snow regions in the north, rain in various areas
- **Large Dungeon Count**: 15 dungeons make it one of the most dungeon-dense lands
- **Diverse Environments**: Ranges from snowy peaks to jungle temples to undersea castles

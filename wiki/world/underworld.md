# Underworld

The Underworld is a dark subterranean realm filled with infernos, abysses, ancient technology, and forbidden tombs. It is one of the most dangerous lands in the game.

## Special Materials & Harvests

The Underworld has special ore and wood mutations. Which ore you get depends on which map file you entered the Underworld from.

| Resource | Details |
|---|---|
| Standard Ore (Iron–Valorite) | Available at any rock/cave wall |
| **Mithril ore** | Mining Agapite/Verite/Valorite veins in Underworld on **Sosaria/Lodoria/IslesDread/Serpent** maps → 50% mutation to Mithril. Mithril requires 115.0+ Blacksmithing; grants Durability +100, Luck +100, Lower Req 50, Energy +30. |
| **Xormite ore** | Mining Agapite/Verite/Valorite veins in Underworld on the **Savaged Empire** map → 50% mutation to Xormite. Xormite requires 115.0+ Blacksmithing; grants Energy +30, Lower Req 25. |
| **Petrified wood** | Chopping any non-Regular wood vein in the Underworld (any map) → 100% mutation to Petrified. Petrified requires 115.0+ Lumberjacking; grants Fire +25 weapon bonus, Durability +70. |
| **Sci-Fi metals/materials** | The **Ancient Sky Ship** dungeon houses sci-fi droids and alien creatures — all sci-fi material families (metals, leathers, scales, bones, woods) drop here. |
| **Underworld wreck fishing** | Wreck fishing in the Underworld is available within a specific area at approximately (578, 1370) — this is a hardcoded coordinate check in the fishing engine (`Fishing.cs`), not a named region. The "Waters of Ktulu" is a region on the Sosaria map, not the Underworld. |

See [Mining](../crafting/mining.md) for full Mithril and Xormite stats. See [Lumberjacking](../crafting/lumberjacking.md) for Petrified stats. See [Fishing](../crafting/fishing.md) for Underworld fishing coordinate (578, 1370) and wreck loot details.

## Unique Features

- **Subterranean Setting**: The entire land takes place underground with dungeon-level lighting in many areas
- **High Danger**: Home to some of the most challenging content in the game
- **Mixed Themes**: Combines fantasy elements (demon crags, abysses) with sci-fi elements (the Ancient Sky Ship)
- **Fort of Tenebrae**: The only settlement, serving as a refuge in the darkness
- **Door Generation**: Doors are auto-generated for Underworld regions

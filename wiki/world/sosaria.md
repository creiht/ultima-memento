# Sosaria

Sosaria is the primary land and starting area of Ultima Memento. It is the largest and most populated continent, serving as the home base for new characters who begin their journey in the Gypsy Forest.

## Sub-Regions on the Sosaria Map

Sosaria's map file also contains the following distinct geographical areas:

### Ambrosia
A lost continent in the south-east corner of the Sosaria map (approx. 5160–6163 x, 3036–4095 y). Dungeons: the Cave of the Zuluu, the City of the Dead, the Dragon's Maw.

### Umber Veil
Dark islands in the south-west (approx. 737–2310 x, 3130–4095 y). Dungeons: the Mausoleum, the Tower of Brass.

### Kuldar (Bottle World)
A bottle world in the east of the map (approx. 6166–7204 x, 829–2741 y). Features the City of Kuldara, Vordo's Castle, and several dungeons. A warning is shown before entering. Dungeons: the Crypts of Kuldar, the Kuldara Sewers, the Valley of Dark Druids, Vordo's Castle, Vordo's Dungeon.

### Luna
A small area (approx. 5856–6164 x, 2740–3018 y).

## Unique Features

- **Starting Area**: All new characters begin in the Gypsy Forest on Sosaria
- **Safari Animals**: Can be configured to spawn (default: disabled for Sosaria)
- **Random Powerful Creatures**: When enabled, powerful daemons, dragons, wyrms, and angels roam the land
- **Weather**: Rain and snow weather systems are active across various regions
- **Merchant Gold Scaling**: Vendors on other facets may carry more gold than Sosaria vendors when merchant gold scaling is enabled

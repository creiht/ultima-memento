# Serpents Quest

The Serpents quest line is inspired by Ultima VII Part Two: Serpent Isle. It involves restoring balance between the forces of Order and Chaos by collecting special artifacts and capturing serpent essences.

## Key Items

### Blackrock Serpents
Three serpent figurines carved from blackrock:

| Serpent | Alignment |
|---------|-----------|
| Blackrock Serpent of Order | Order |
| Blackrock Serpent of Chaos | Chaos |
| Blackrock Serpent of Balance | Balance |

### Captured Serpent Stones
When serpents are subdued, they are contained in stones:

| Stone | Contents |
|-------|----------|
| Order Serpent Stone | Contains the Subdued Serpent of Order |
| Chaos Serpent Stone | Contains the Subdued Serpent of Chaos |
| Balance Serpent Stone | Contains the Power of the Serpent of Balance |

### Quest Artifacts

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Lantern of Discipline | Related to Order |
| Orb of Logic | Related to Order |
| Scales of Ethicality | Related to Balance |

## Bane Bases

**Bane Bases** are pedestals found in dungeons on the Serpent Island that hold serpent-related artifacts. Interact with them to collect items needed for the quest.

## How It Works

1. Explore the **Serpent Island** and related dungeons.
2. Find and collect the Blackrock Serpents and quest artifacts from Bane Base pedestals.
3. Use the serpent figurines and artifacts to capture/subdue the great serpents.
4. Restore the Balance by bringing all captured serpents and items together.

## Rewards

- Unique serpent-themed items and artifacts.
- Significant karma and fame adjustments.
- Access to endgame Serpent Island content.

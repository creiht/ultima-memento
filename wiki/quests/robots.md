# Robot Quest

The Robot quest lets you build a combat robot companion by gathering high-tech materials and having a tinker assemble it. These are found primarily in the sci-fi themed areas.

## How to Start

Find **Robot Schematics** (a data pad) as loot.

## Required Materials

All materials are robot-specific parts found in science-fiction themed areas:

| Material | Amount Needed |
|----------|---------------|
| Sheet Metal | 1,000 |
| Engine Parts | 9 |
| Circuit Boards | 9 |
| Transistors | 9 |
| Gears | 45 |
| Oil (Robot Oil) | 27 |
| Bolts | 45 |
| Xormite | 10,000 (tinker's fee) |

Drag and drop collected parts onto the schematics data pad to add them.

## Completing the Quest

1. Gather all required materials and add them to the schematics.
2. The schematics tell you which **tinker** (by town) can build your robot.
3. Give the completed schematics to that tinker.
4. If you have Tinkering skill, the tinker refunds some xormite (up to 50% at GM).

## The Robot

- Robots are **combat companions** that use 3 follower slots.
- They are controlled like tamed creatures (follow, stay, stop, dismiss).
- Robots run on **charges** -- each summon uses one.
- Recharge with **batteries**: each battery adds 1 charge (max 100).
- Robots are **not transferable** once built.

## Tips

- Robot parts are primarily found in the Ancient Crash Site, Sky Ship, and other sci-fi locations.
- The schematics track all progress -- double-click to check.
- Xormite (the currency of sci-fi areas) is used instead of gold for the tinker's fee.

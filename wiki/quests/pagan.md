# Pagan Quest

The Pagan quest line involves collecting artifacts related to the Pagan world, including obelisk tips and obsidian items. It is inspired by Ultima VIII: Pagan.

## Key Items

- **Obelisk Tips** -- found on corpses or in special locations.
- **Pagan Artifacts** -- unique items from the Pagan realm.
- **Obsidian Gate** -- a portal related to the Pagan dimension.

## How It Works

1. Explore dungeons and the world to find **Obelisk Tips** on special corpses.
2. Collect Pagan-themed artifacts from various locations.
3. Approach the **Obsidian Gate** locations to progress.
4. Pedestals (PaganBase) in dungeons hold Pagan artifacts that can be collected.

## Pagan Bases

Pagan Bases are pedestals found in dungeons that hold Pagan artifacts. When you take an artifact, the pedestal becomes empty and eventually respawns.

## Rewards

- Unique Pagan-themed items and artifacts.
- Titan Riches (large gold rewards).
- Progress toward endgame content.

# Prisoner Quests

Caged creatures are found in dungeons. You can free them for a gold reward or recruit them as henchman companions.

## How to Start

Find a **caged creature** in a dungeon and double-click it.

## Options

| Choice | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Free for Gold** | Receive a gold reward (1,000-3,000 based on creature type) |
| **Recruit as Henchman** | Pay gold (5,000-15,000) to gain them as a companion |
| **Leave Them** | Walk away with no effect |

## Prisoner Types

Prisoners are randomly generated from many creature races:

| Race Category | Examples |
|---------------|----------|
| Humanoids | Bugbears, morlocks, hobgoblins, satyrs, centaurs, minotaurs, mind flayers, gnolls, pixies |
| Reptilians | Lizardmen, sakkhra, sleestax, grathek, kobolds |
| Orcs/Goblins | Orcs, orx, urcs, goblins |
| Aquatic | Tritun, neptar |
| Ratkin | Various ratman types |
| Serpentine | Serpyn, ophidians |
| Human Henchmen | Fighters, archers, wizards (higher cost) |

## Prisoner Classes

| Class | Cost Modifier | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Warrior (melee) | 1x | Most common |
| Archer (ranged) | 1.25x | Less common |
| Mage (caster) | 1.5x | Least common |
| Human Henchmen | 4,100-7,000 | Special, come as standard henchman items (cost depends on type: fighter/wizard 4,100–5,000; archer 5,100–6,000; elite type 6,100–7,000) |

## Henchman System

Recruited prisoners become **henchmen** -- see the prisoner gump for full details:

- Henchmen use follower slots and follow/fight like tamed creatures.
- Pay them gold/treasure to keep them traveling with you (300 gold/hour rate).
- If you stop paying, they leave (henchman item returns to your pack).
- Henchmen can be healed with Healing skill.
- They cannot be stabled, transferred (while active), or bonded.

## Tips

- Begging skill can reduce the recruitment cost (up to 3,000 gold discount at GM).
- Freeing prisoners earns fame and karma (or negative karma if karma is locked).
- Human henchman prisoners are the most expensive but function as full henchmen.

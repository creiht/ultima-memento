# Epic Quests (Courier Mail)

Epic quests are courier-style delivery and retrieval quests. An NPC gives you a message to deliver or an item to find in a specific dungeon.

## How to Start

An NPC sends you a **Courier Mail** message. The message contains a story, a target dungeon, and a quest objective.

## Quest Details

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Quest Item | Courier Mail (a sealed message) |
| Objective | Travel to the named dungeon, find the item in a Search Base chest |
| Reward | Gold (shown in the message) |
| Sextant | If you carry a sextant, the message gump shows map buttons for navigation |

## How It Works

1. You receive a **Courier Mail** item in your backpack, addressed to you.
2. Double-click it to read the quest details: who sent it, what to find, and where.
3. Travel to the specified dungeon and interact with a **Search Base chest** on a pedestal.
4. If the correct dungeon matches, the message updates to "Complete."
5. Return the completed message to the quest-giving NPC to receive your gold reward.

## Tips

- Keep the Courier Mail in your backpack at all times.
- Bring a sextant to use the built-in map navigation feature.
- The message updates its tooltip to show "Complete" when the objective is done.

# Underworld Quests

The Underworld is a separate realm accessible through special portals. Quests in the Underworld involve exploring this dark domain and confronting its unique challenges.

## Key Items

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Runic Doorway** | Large stone doors covered in strange runes that serve as passage points in the Underworld |
| **Skull of Baron Almric** | A quest artifact found in the Underworld |
| **Underworld Teleporter** | Teleportation points for navigating the Underworld |

## How It Works

1. Find and use **Runic Doorways** to navigate the Underworld's passages.
2. Runic doorways display "This large stone door is covered in strange runes" when examined.
3. Explore the Underworld to find quest items, artifacts, and special encounters.
4. The Underworld Teleporter network connects different areas of this realm.

## Relationship to Other Quests

The Underworld serves as a location for:
- **Runes of Virtue** -- The Chamber of Corruption is in the Underworld (for evil-aligned characters).
- **Search Quests** -- Search Base chests exist in the Underworld.
- **Standard/Fishing Quests** -- Some bounty targets are located in the Underworld.
- **Codex Quest** -- Keys and crystals may be found in Underworld dungeons.

## Currency

The Underworld uses **Jewels** instead of standard gold for many loot drops (hoard piles, etc.).

## Tips

- The Underworld is a dangerous area -- prepare accordingly before entering.
- Many quests reference the Underworld as a possible quest location for higher-difficulty objectives.
- The Runic Doorways are the primary means of navigation between areas.

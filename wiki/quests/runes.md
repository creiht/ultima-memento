# Runes of Virtue Quest

The Runes of Virtue quest challenges you to collect all 8 Runes representing the Virtues and bring them to a special chamber for purification (or corruption).

## How to Start

Find a **Chest of Virtue** in a dungeon. The chest is bound to you.

## The 8 Runes

| Rune | Virtue | Corruption |
|------|--------|------------|
| Compassion | Good | Cruelty |
| Honesty | Good | Deceit |
| Honor | Good | Scorn |
| Humility | Good | Arrogance |
| Justice | Good | Oppression |
| Sacrifice | Good | Neglect |
| Spirituality | Good | Sacrilege |
| Valor | Good | Fear |

## How It Works

1. Carry the Chest of Virtue and explore dungeons.
2. Find **Runes Bases** (pedestals) in dungeons. Opening one spawns a guardian creature.
3. Defeat the guardian to claim the Rune and add it to your chest.
4. Double-click the chest to see which Runes you've collected.
5. Once all 8 Runes are collected, the chest shows the location of the Chamber.

## Choosing Your Path

Your path depends on your **karma lock** status:

| Path | Chamber | Location |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Virtue** (karma unlocked) | Chamber of Virtue | Land of Sosaria |
| **Corruption** (karma locked) | Chamber of Corruption | The Underworld |

## Completing the Quest

Open the chest inside the appropriate Chamber. You receive:

### Rewards

| Reward | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **8 Rune Souvenirs** | Decorative rune items (tinted for corruption path) |
| **8 Virtue Tapestries** | Valuable decorative wall hangings |
| **Rune of Virtue** | A personal rune that grows in power as you adventure |
| **Chest of Virtue/Corruption** | A container with all souvenirs inside |
| **Wealth** | Crystals, jewels, gold, silver, copper, gemstones, gold nuggets |
| **Fame** | Set to 15,000 |
| **Karma** | Set to +15,000 (virtue) or -15,000 (corruption) |

### Additional Effects

- Virtue path: All kills and criminal status are cleared; fugitive status removed.
- Corruption path: Chest is named "chest of corruption" with a dark hue.

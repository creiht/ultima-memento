# Quests

Ultima Memento features a rich quest system with over 20 distinct quest types ranging from simple bounty hunts to epic multi-stage artifact hunts. Quests are a primary source of gold, fame, karma, unique items, and companions.

## How to Start

- **Talk to NPCs** -- double-click quest givers, bulletin boards, or special items found in the world.
- **Find quest items** -- many quests begin when you pick up a special item (journal, cube, schematics, etc.) in a dungeon.
- **Use the `[quests` command** to open your quest log at any time.

See [Quest System Overview](quest-system.md) for full details on how quests work.

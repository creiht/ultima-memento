# Fishing Quests

Fishing quests are sea-themed bounty hunts and item recovery missions obtained from sailor quest bulletin boards.

## How to Start

Visit a **Sailor Quest Bulletin Board** found near docks and coastal towns.

## Quest Details

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Quest Giver | Sailor Quest Bulletin Board |
| Objective | Kill a sea creature/pirate or recover a lost item from sea monsters |
| Reward | Gold (based on target Fame or item value) |
| Cooldown | Time-based cooldown after completion |

## Quest Types

### Monster Bounty (2/3 chance)
- Kill a specific sea creature on the high seas in a particular world.
- Targets include sea monsters (creatures with `WhisperHue == 999`) and pirate captains.
- Pirate captain hunts are rarer (1 in 8 chance, requires higher fame bracket).

### Item Recovery (1/3 chance)
- Recover a named item from sea creatures.
- Items drop from sea monsters with a **10% chance** per kill in the correct world.
- The quest story describes a lost item from a shipwreck.

## Completing the Quest

- For bounties: kill the named creature on the high seas in the correct world.
- For items: kill sea creatures in the correct world until the item drops (10% per kill).
- Once done, return to any **Sailor Quest Bulletin Board** to collect your gold payment.

## Rewards

- Gold paid directly to your backpack.
- Fame and Karma (positive karma if karma is not locked, negative if locked).

## Tips

- Targets are selected from lands you've already discovered.
- Higher-fame characters get harder (and more rewarding) targets.
- Pirate captain bounties pay significantly more than regular sea creature bounties.

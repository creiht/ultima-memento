# Codex of Ultimate Wisdom

The Codex quest is a long, multi-stage collection quest to obtain the legendary Codex of Ultimate Wisdom, granting +25 Intelligence and Grandmaster status (+100) in two skills of your choice.

## How to Start

Find the **Vortex Cube** on a special corpse in the world. Double-click it or pick it up to begin the quest. The cube is bound to you -- only you can use it.

## Quest Stages

### 1. Collect the Keys of Infinity (3 keys)

You need three keys to enter the Chamber of the Codex:

| Key | Info |
|-----|------|
| Key of Law | Location is hinted on the Vortex Cube gump |
| Key of Chaos | Location is hinted on the Vortex Cube gump |
| Key of Balance | Location is hinted on the Vortex Cube gump |

Keys are found inside **Search Base chests** (pedestals) in dungeons. The cube tells you which dungeon to visit.

### 2. Collect the Lenses (2 lenses)

To read the Codex, you need two lenses obtained from gargoyle NPCs:

| Lens | NPC |
|------|-----|
| Concave Lens | **Naxatilor** the gargoyle (location shown on cube) |
| Convex Lens | **Lor-wis-lem** the gargoyle (location shown on cube) |

### 3. Collect the Void Crystals (6 crystals)

Six colored crystals power the cube to draw items from the Void:

- Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, White, Purple

Crystals are found one at a time in dungeon chests. After finding each crystal, the cube updates with the next crystal's rumored location.

### 4. Enter the Chamber of the Codex

With all 11 items collected (3 keys + 2 lenses + 6 crystals), travel to the **Chamber of the Codex** and approach the Void. The Vortex Cube is consumed and the **Codex of Ultimate Wisdom** is placed in your backpack.

### 5. Use the Codex

- The Codex is a **talisman** (worn in the trinket slot).
- To study it, you must be inside the **Chamber of the Codex** and have both lenses.
- Open the Codex to choose **two skills** to set to 100 (Grandmaster).
- You can change your skill selections by returning to the Chamber with new lenses.
- The Codex also grants **+25 Intelligence**.
- Restriction: You cannot study both Magery and Elementalism, or both Necromancy and Elementalism simultaneously.

## Important Notes

- The Codex is **Blessed** (cannot be lost on death).
- The Codex is bound to you -- if another player touches it, it returns to its owner.
- Only one Vortex Cube or Codex can exist per character at a time.
- Lenses vanish if you leave the Chamber after changing skills and haven't returned them to the Codex.

# Assassin Quests

Assassin quests send you to kill a specific target in a dungeon for a gold bounty. Targets can be monsters or innocent NPCs.

## How to Start

Talk to **Xardok**, the assassin guild leader. He assigns you a target with a location, a description, and a gold reward.

## Quest Details

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Quest Giver | Xardok (assassin guild) |
| Objective | Travel to the target's dungeon region and kill them |
| Reward | Gold (based on target's Fame) |
| Cooldown | Time-based cooldown after completion |
| Karma Effect | Lose karma (double loss for killing innocents) |

## How It Works

1. Xardok randomly selects a target from the world -- either a **monster** in a dungeon or an **innocent vendor** in a village.
2. Target locations are drawn from lands you have discovered (Sosaria, Lodoria, Serpent Island, etc.).
3. Travel to the specified dungeon region and slay the named creature.
4. You will see "Your victim has been assassinated!" when you kill the correct target.
5. Return to Xardok to collect your gold payment.

## Target Categories

- **Monster** -- an evil creature in a dungeon. Reward is based on Fame / 5.
- **Innocent** -- a vendor NPC in a village. Reward is a flat 1,000 gold (modified by server settings). Killing innocents gives double karma loss but removes the murder count for that kill.

## Failing a Quest

If you wish to abandon an assassin quest, you can use the **Box of Atonement**. Drop gold equal to the quest's penalty amount into the box to be forgiven.

## Tips

- The quest story text tells you the target's name, region, and world.
- Your quest status updates to "Return to Xardok for your payment" once the target is dead.
- Criminal status is cleared when you collect payment.

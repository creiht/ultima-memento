# Standard Quests

Standard quests are the most common quest type -- bounty hunts and item recovery missions available from bulletin boards in taverns and towns.

## How to Start

Visit a **Standard Quest Bulletin Board** found in taverns and public buildings.

## Quest Details

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Quest Giver | Standard Quest Bulletin Board |
| Objective | Kill a specific monster or find an item in a dungeon |
| Reward | Gold (based on target Fame or item value) |
| Cooldown | Time-based cooldown after completion |

## Quest Types

### Monster Bounty (50% chance)
- Kill a named creature in a specific dungeon region.
- The creature's location, name, and region are specified in your quest log.
- Travel to the dungeon and kill the correct creature type in the correct region.

### Item Recovery (50% chance)
- Find a named item by killing creatures in the specified dungeon.
- Items drop with a **7% chance** per kill in the correct region.
- Items can also be found by opening loot containers in the dungeon.

## Completing the Quest

1. Kill the target or find the item.
2. Return to any Standard Quest Bulletin Board to collect your gold payment.

## Rewards

- Gold paid directly to your backpack.
- Fame earned.
- Karma earned or lost (based on karma lock status).

## Tips

- Targets are selected from lands you've already discovered, scaling to your difficulty level.
- Higher-fame characters receive harder targets with better rewards.
- The quest text tells you the exact dungeon region and world to visit.
- You can check quest status at any time through the quest gump.
- If you want to abandon a quest, there is a gold penalty.

# Search Quests (Dungeon Pedestals)

Search Bases are ornate chests on pedestals found throughout dungeons. They serve as interaction points for many different quest types.

## How It Works

Walk up to a **Search Base** (a chest sitting on a carved stone pedestal) and use it. What happens depends on what quest items you are carrying in your backpack.

## Supported Quest Items

The Search Base checks your backpack for the following items and processes them in order:

| Quest Item | What Happens |
|------------|-------------|
| **Search Page** | Checks if you're in the correct dungeon. May find the legendary item, get redirected, find a fake, or learn the legend was false. |
| **Relic Tablet** | Similar to Search Page -- finds the relic item, a fake, or nothing. |
| **Vortex Cube** | Finds Keys of Infinity and Void Crystals for the Codex quest. |
| **Alien Egg** | Finds reagents for the alien egg hatching quest (rod of amber, sun crystal, blood crystal, potion of growth). |
| **Dragon Egg** | Finds potions for dragon egg hatching (elixir of flame, potion of earth, mixture of sea, oil of winds). |
| **Dracolich Skull** | Finds components for the dracolich quest (heart of dead god, eye of mad king, orb of astral lich, mind of planar ghost). |
| **Research Bag** | Finds research materials. |
| **Museum Book** | Finds museum/antique items. |
| **Quest Tome** | Finds Major Quest relics (held by powerful creatures or in chests). |
| **Demon Prison** | Finds shards for the demon summoning quest. |
| **Courier Mail** | Finds the courier's requested item. |

## If No Quest Items

If you have no relevant quest items, the chest appears to be empty.

## Guarded Chests

- You cannot open a Search Base if there are hostile creatures within 5 tiles.
- You cannot open them while invulnerable (blessed state).

## Tips

- Many quests rely on Search Bases -- always explore dungeons thoroughly.
- The chest's pedestal has decorative carvings in various languages that hint at its contents.
- Chests can serve multiple quests simultaneously if you carry multiple quest items.

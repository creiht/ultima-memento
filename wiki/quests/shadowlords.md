# Shadowlords Quest

The Shadowlords quest line is inspired by Ultima V. You must collect the three Flames of Principle -- the Book of Truth, Bell of Courage, and Candle of Love -- and use them alongside the Shards and the Gem of Immortality to defeat (or empower) the Shadowlords.

## The Three Flames

Found on **Flames Base pedestals** in dungeons:

| Item | Principle | Found On |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Book of Truth | Truth | Flames Base pedestal |
| Bell of Courage | Courage | Flames Base pedestal |
| Candle of Love | Love | Flames Base pedestal |

Approach a pedestal and use it to claim the item. Each item is bound to you.

## The Three Shards

Dark artifacts associated with the Shadowlords:

| Shard | Shadowlord |
|-------|------------|
| Shard of Falsehood | Faulinei |
| Shard of Cowardice | Nosfentor |
| Shard of Hatred | Astaroth |

## Other Quest Items

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Gem of Immortality | Key item for the Shadowlords quest finale |

## How It Works

1. Explore dungeons to find **Flames Base pedestals** and collect the Book, Bell, and Candle.
2. When you walk near a pedestal, you receive a hint: "I could use that item on the pedestal to take it."
3. Find the three Shards in other dungeon locations.
4. Use the Flames items and Shards to confront the Shadowlords.
5. The **Balinor Teleporter** provides access to the Shadowlords' domain.

## Rewards

- Unique virtue/anti-virtue items.
- Progression in the main story quest line.
- Fame and karma adjustments.

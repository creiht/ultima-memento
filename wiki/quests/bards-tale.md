# Bard's Tale Quest

The Bard's Tale quest line takes you through Mangar's Tower, inspired by the classic Bard's Tale RPG. Completing this dungeon rewards powerful magical items.

## Rewards

Defeating Mangar and completing the tower can reward class-specific robes and a bardic cap:

| Reward | Stats |
|--------|-------|
| **Mangar's Robe** (Mage) | +25% Lower Mana Cost, +25% Lower Reagent Cost, +10 Psychology, +10 Magery, +10 Magic Resist, +10 Meditation, +10 Mana Regen, +10 Int |
| **Mangar's Robe** (Necromancer) | +25% Lower Mana Cost, +25% Lower Reagent Cost, +10 Spiritualism, +10 Necromancy, +10 Magic Resist, +10 Meditation, +10 Mana Regen, +10 Int |
| **Mangar's Robe** (Elementalist) | +25% Lower Mana Cost, +25% Lower Reagent Cost, +10 Elementalism, +10 Focus, +10 Magic Resist, +10 Meditation, +10 Mana Regen, +10 Int |
| **Bardic Feathered Cap** | +10 Musicianship, +10 Provocation, +10 Discordance, +10 Peacemaking, +10 Mana Regen, +10 Dex |

All rewards are Standard Artefact level and are blessed (cannot be looted).

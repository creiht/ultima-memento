# Summon Quests (Prisons of Wizardry)

Summon quests involve freeing powerful creatures from magical crystal prisons found in dungeons. These are high-risk, high-reward encounters.

## How to Start

Find a **crystal prison orb** (Demon Prison / Summon Prison) in the world. The orb is bound to you -- only you can carry one at a time. If you pick up a second, the first vanishes.

## Quest Details

The orb's gump tells you:

| Info | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Prisoner | The name of the imprisoned creature |
| Jailor | The wizard who imprisoned them |
| Dungeon | The location where they were sealed |
| Required Keys | 3 special keys needed to unlock the prison |
| Required Reagents | 2 types of reagents in specific quantities |

## How It Works

1. Double-click the orb to read what items you need and where to go.
2. Collect the **3 key items** from dungeons (found in Search Base chests and other locations).
3. Collect the required **reagents** (standard spell reagents in specific quantities).
4. Travel to the **specified dungeon** with all items.
5. Use the orb while in the dungeon to **free the prisoner**.
6. A powerful creature spawns and attacks -- be ready for combat!
7. The freed creature remains for **one hour** before leaving permanently.
8. Defeat it and collect the loot from its corpse.

## Shard Collection (Demon Prison variant)

Some prison orbs require finding **4 shards** instead:

| Shard |
|-------|
| Shard of Hellfire |
| Shard of the Abyss |
| Shard of Souls |
| Shard of the Void |

Shards are found in dungeon Search Base chests. Each shard is hinted at by a rumor on the orb.

## Tips

- The summoned creature is scaled in difficulty.
- Only one prison orb can be held per character at a time.
- The creature will not wait forever -- you have one hour to defeat it.
- These encounters drop valuable loot proportional to the creature's power.

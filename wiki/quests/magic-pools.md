# Magic Pools

Magic pools are mysterious water features found in dungeons. Drinking from them produces random effects -- some beneficial, some harmful.

## How It Works

1. Find a **magic pool** in a dungeon (3x3 water feature with a glowing center).
2. Walk up and use the pool to drink from it.
3. The pool has a limited number of uses (1-10) before its magic is exhausted.
4. Pools disappear after **30 minutes**.

## Possible Effects

| Pool Type | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Stat Gain** | Permanently gain 1-5 points in a random stat (Str/Dex/Int), if below stat cap |
| **Cure** | Cures poison |
| **Heal** | Heals 50-175 hit points |
| **Water Elemental** | Spawns a hostile Water Elemental |
| **Gold to Lead** | Turns ALL coins in your backpack to worthless lead coins |
| **Item Vanish** | A random equipped item disappears permanently |
| **Stat Loss** | Permanently lose 1 point of Str, Dex, or Int |
| **Treasure Chest** | Pull a high-level mystical chest from the pool (drains pool completely) |
| **Coins to Gold** | Converts copper, silver, and lead coins to gold |
| **Poison** | Inflicts poison (Lesser to Deadly, depending on pool strength) |

## Risk vs Reward

- Stat Gain and Treasure Chest are the most desirable outcomes.
- Gold to Lead and Item Vanish are devastating -- make sure you're prepared to lose items.
- The effects are random and cannot be predicted.

## Tips

- If you have no gold/copper/silver, the Gold to Lead effect does nothing.
- Poison pools are more common than beneficial ones -- carry cure potions.
- Treasure chests from pools contain 5,000-8,000 gold plus possible artifacts.

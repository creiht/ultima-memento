# Slaver's Net

The Slaver's Net is a special item used to capture certain creatures.

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Type | Special tool |
| Function | Captures targeted creatures |

## How It Works

Use the net on a valid creature target to attempt capture. Success depends on the creature type and circumstances.

## How to Obtain

Found as rare loot in special encounters.

## Notes

- Not all creatures can be captured
- The net is consumed on use
- Captured creatures may become pets or resources depending on the type

# Relic: Exotic Furs

Exotic furs are relic items representing pelts from rare and fantastical creatures.

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Weight | 40.0 stones |
| Gold Value | 80-500 gold |
| ID Skill | Mercantile |
| Scissorable | Yes (can be cut) |

## Fur Types

Furs come from 39+ creature types including:

**Common Animals**: Beaver, ermine, fox, marten, mink, muskrat, sable, bear, deer, rabbit, badger, buffalo, camel

**Exotic Creatures**: Cheetah, leopard, lion, panther, lynx, dire bear, mammoth, mastadon, sabretooth, rhinoceros

**Fantastical Creatures**: Yeti (white), polar bear (white), black wolf (black), unicorn, pegasus, griffin, dragon, basilisk, gargoyle, manticore, demon

Some furs have special hues — yeti and polar bear furs are white (1150), black wolf and panther furs are black (1899).

## Naming Convention

Each fur follows the pattern: **[Quality] [Creature] fur pelt**

Quality adjectives: rare, nice, pretty, superb, delightful, elegant, exquisite, fine, gorgeous, lovely, magnificent, marvelous, splendid, wonderful, extraordinary, strange, odd, unique, unusual

## How to Obtain

Found as dungeon loot, monster drops, and in treasure chests.

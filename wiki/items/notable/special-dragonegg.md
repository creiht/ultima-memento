# Dragon Egg

Dragon Eggs are rare special items that can be hatched into dragon pets.

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Type | Special quest item |
| Weight | Varies |

## How to Obtain

Dragon Eggs are found in dragon lairs and as rare drops from powerful dragon creatures.

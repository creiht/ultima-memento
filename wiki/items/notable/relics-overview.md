# Dungeon Relics — Notable Items

Dungeon relics are treasure items found by adventurers in dangerous areas. Each relic type represents a different category of valuable collectible. All relics must be identified before their gold value is revealed.

## How Relics Work

1. **Discovery**: Relics are found as loot in dungeons, treasure chests, and monster drops
2. **Identification**: Use the appropriate skill (Mercantile, Arms Lore, or Tasting) to identify
3. **Value**: Each relic is worth 80-500 gold, revealed upon identification
4. **Selling**: Sell identified relics to merchants for their gold value
5. **Decoration**: Many relics double as home decorations (paintings, statues, rugs, lights)

## Special Relics

### Crystal Balls
When identified and double-clicked, crystal balls show random mystical visions. Purely flavor, but fun collectibles with randomly generated names like "Theram's Magnificent Crystal Ball."

### Bear Skin Rugs
These are full multi-tile house addons available in black or brown, facing east or south. The most complex relic type — they function as complete floor decorations.

### Candelabras
Functional light sources that can be turned on and off. Provide Circle225 light when lit.

### Copper/Silver Coins
Unlike other relics, currency relics can be converted directly to gold:
- **Copper Coins**: Exchange at 10 copper = 1 gold (at banker)
- **Silver Coins**: Exchange at **5 silver = 1 gold** (5:1 ratio)

## Tips for Relic Hunters

- Maximize your **Mercantile** skill to identify the widest range of relics
- Also train **Arms Lore** (for armor/weapon relics) and **Tasting** (for drink/alchemy relics)
- Enable **Double-Click ID** in your preferences for quick identification
- Higher-value relics are found in more dangerous areas

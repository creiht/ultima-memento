# Fountain of Life

The Fountain of Life is a special reward item that enhances bandages placed inside it.

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Type | Home addon / Container |
| Function | Heals bandages placed inside over time |

## How It Works

Place bandages inside the Fountain of Life and over time they become enhanced, providing better healing when used.

## How to Obtain

The Fountain of Life is obtained as a special veteran reward or rare event prize.

## Placement

Must be placed inside a player-owned house. Functions as both a decorative fountain and a functional item.

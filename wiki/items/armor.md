# Armor

Armor provides physical and elemental resistance to damage. Armor comes in many types, from light leather to heavy plate, and can be crafted from various materials that affect its stats. See the [Armor Guide](armor-guide.md) for full stat tables.

## Armor Categories

### Leather Armor
Light armor crafted from leather. Low protection but minimal penalties. Includes standard, studded, and hide variants.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest (male/female variants), Gloves, Gorget, Legs, Cap, Shorts, Skirt, Bustier Arms, Garb, plus Japanese-style Do, Haidate, HiroSode, Mempo, Suneate, and Ninja variants (Hood, Jacket, Mitts, Pants).

### Bone Armor
Medium armor crafted from bones. Includes standard bone and savage variants.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest, Gloves, Helm, Legs, Skirt, Orc Helm, Savage Arms/Chest/Gloves/Helm/Legs.

### Ringmail Armor
Medium metal armor. Good balance of protection and weight.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest, Gloves, Legs, Skirt.

### Chain Armor
Medium-heavy metal armor.

**Pieces**: Chest, Coif, Legs, Skirt, Hatsuburi.

### Plate Armor
Heaviest armor with the best protection but highest weight.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest (male/female), Gloves, Gorget, Helm, Legs, Skirt, plus Japanese-style Do, Haidate, HiroSode, Mempo, Suneate, Kabuto (Standard, Decorative), Hatsuburi, Jingasa (Light, Heavy, Small).

### Scaled/Scaly Armor
Dragon-scale armor with elemental resistance bonuses.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest, Gloves, Gorget, Helm, Legs, Boots.

### Drakbone Armor
Crafted from drake bones with unique properties.

**Pieces**: Bracers, Greaves, Gauntlets, Helm, Tunic.

### Dragon Armor
High-end armor crafted from dragon materials.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest, Gloves, Helm, Legs.

### Royal Armor
Decorated armor with royal aesthetics.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest, Gloves, Gorget, Helm, Legs, Boots.

### Ranger Armor
Woodland-themed medium armor.

**Pieces**: Arms, Chest, Gloves, Gorget, Legs.

### Oniwaban Armor
Ninja/stealth-oriented armor set.

**Pieces**: Boots, Gloves, Hood, Leggings, Tunic.

### Shinobi Garb
Full-body ninja outfit.

### Wooden Armor
Light armor made from wood.

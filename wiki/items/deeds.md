# Deeds

Deeds are consumable contract items that grant special services or abilities when used. They are typically blessed (cannot be looted) and weigh 1.0 stone.

## Deed Types

| Deed | Weight | Effect | How to Obtain |
|------|--------|--------|---------------|
| Barkeep Contract | 1.0 | Places a barkeep NPC in your house. Must be house owner. Limited per house. | Purchase from merchants |
| Clothing Bless Deed | 1.0 | Blesses a piece of clothing, making it remain with you on death. Target a clothing item in your pack. | Rare loot, rewards |
| Commodity Deed | 1.0 | Wraps a stack of commodity resources (e.g., ingots, reagents) into a single deed for safe storage and trading. Must be used from bank box. | Purchase from bankers |
| Dragon Barding Deed | 1.0 | Applies dragon armor to a tamed swamp dragon. Can be crafted with different materials and exceptional quality. | Blacksmith crafting |
| Hair Restyling Deed | 1.0 | Opens a gump to choose a new hairstyle. Human and Elf racial styles available. Blessed. | Purchase from merchants |
| Name Change Deed | 1.0 | Allows you to change your character's name. | Purchase, rewards |
| Vendor Rental Contract | 1.0 | Allows house owners to rent vendor spots to other players. Default price 1500 gold. Must be placed in a public house. | Purchase from merchants |

## Usage Notes

- **Barkeep Contract**: Must be standing inside your house. The house must not exceed the maximum barkeep limit. Cannot place where another vendor already stands.
- **Clothing Bless Deed**: Only works on regular (non-blessed, non-insured) clothing. Cannot bless arcane items.
- **Commodity Deed**: Empty deeds have a grey hue; filled deeds change color. Both filling and redeeming must be done from the bank box.
- **Dragon Barding Deed**: Craftable via Blacksmithing. Can be exceptional quality. Material type affects the barding's appearance and properties. Remove barding from a swamp dragon by using a bladed item on it.
- **Vendor Rental Contract**: Must be locked down inside a public AOS-enabled house. Landlord can set rental duration and price.

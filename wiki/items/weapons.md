# Weapons

Weapons are the primary combat items in Ultima Memento. Each category is governed by a different skill. Stats below are **AOS mode** values (the active ruleset). Speed rating: higher number = faster swing.

See the [Weapons Guide](weapons-guide.md) for extended stats, special moves, and material bonuses.

## Weapon Properties

All weapons can have:
- **Damage Increase (DI)**: Percentage bonus to base damage
- **Elemental Damage Split**: Physical/Fire/Cold/Poison/Energy percentages
- **Special Moves**: Up to 5 weapon abilities (Primary through Fifth)
- **Slayer Properties**: Up to 2 slayers — bonus damage vs. specific creature types
- **Quality**: Low, Regular, Exceptional (Exceptional adds +15% Damage Increase)
- **Material Bonuses**: Different metals/woods provide elemental resistance bonuses
- **Durability**: Hit points degrade with use

## Damage Type Notes

- Most swords: **Slashing** damage
- Kryss, Lance, Spears, Pike: **Piercing** damage
- Maces, Staves, Hammers: **Blunt** damage
- Damage type can affect special moves and creature interactions

## Weapon Properties

All weapons can have:
- **Damage Increase (DI)**: Percentage bonus to base damage
- **Elemental Damage Split**: Physical/Fire/Cold/Poison/Energy percentages
- **Special Moves**: Up to 5 weapon abilities (Primary through Fifth)
- **Slayer Properties**: Bonus damage vs. specific creature types
- **Quality**: Low, Regular, Exceptional (+15% DI)
- **Material Bonuses**: Different metals/woods provide stat bonuses
- **Durability**: Hit points that degrade with use

## Enhancement

Weapons can be further improved using [Sharpening Stones, Bow Strings, and Weighting Stones](sharpening.md).

# Technology (Sci-Fi Items)

Technology items are sci-fi themed equipment found in alien/futuristic areas of the game world. All technology items are marked with the `Technology` flag and use special crafting materials like **Durasteel**.

- Fire (Physical 50% / Fire 50%)
- Poison (Physical 50% / Poison 50%)

## Durasteel Resource

Technology weapons use Durasteel as their default crafting resource instead of Iron. This gives them a distinct sci-fi appearance and properties.

## Notes

- Technology items appear in alien-themed areas, crashed spaceships, and sci-fi zones
- Chainsaw and Portable Smelter break after their charges are used and leave behind junk items
- Laser swords play a special activation sound when equipped
- The Computer Database terminal allows character appearance customization in alien zones

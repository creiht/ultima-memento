# Trade Items

The Trades category (233 files) encompasses all crafting resources, raw materials, and intermediate components used in the game's extensive crafting system.

## Mining Resources

### Ores
Raw ore is mined with a pickaxe and smelted into ingots:

| Ore Type | Skill Required | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Iron | Base | Standard metal |
| Dull Copper | Low | First special metal |
| Shadow Iron | Low-Mid | Dark colored |
| Copper | Mid | Warm toned |
| Bronze | Mid | Alloy metal |
| Gold | Mid-High | Precious metal |
| Agapite | High | Green tinted |
| Verite | High | Rare green metal |
| Valorite | Very High | Rarest standard metal |
| Additional types | Varies | Server-specific metals |

### Ingots
Smelted from ore, used in Blacksmithing and Tinkering.

### Granite
Special stones used in Masonry crafting.

## Resource Quality

Higher-tier resources provide bonus properties when used in crafting:
- **Metals**: Additional resistances, damage bonuses
- **Woods**: Resistance bonuses, durability
- **Leathers**: Resistance bonuses
- **Scales**: Elemental resistance bonuses

## How to Obtain

- **Mining**: Pickaxe on mountains/caves
- **Lumberjacking**: Hatchet on trees
- **Skinning**: Knife on creature corpses
- **Farming**: Farmable crops (see [Food](food.md))
- **Fishing**: Seafaring skill
- **Purchase**: NPC vendors sell basic resources
- **Salvage**: Unravel crafted items for materials

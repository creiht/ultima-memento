# Explorer Items

Explorer items are equipment used for camping, wilderness survival, and exploration. Most require the **Camping** skill to use effectively.

## Items

| Item | Weight | Skill Req | Uses | Description |
|------|--------|-----------|------|-------------|
| Bedroll | 5.0 | Camping | Single use | Lay out to recover health and stamina. Lasts 100 seconds. 10-minute cooldown. |
| Kindling | 1.0 | Camping | Consumable (stackable) | Light a campfire that heals nearby players for 60-100 seconds. 3-minute cooldown. |
| Camping Tent | 5.0 | Camping 40+ | 10 charges | Teleports you to a safe resting area. At 90+ Camping, usable in dungeons. |
| Small Tent | 2.0 | Camping 10+ | 10 charges | Creates a protective tent in the wilderness. Lasts 5 minutes. Blessed. |
| Spyglass | 1.0 | None | 20 uses | Grants +25 Tracking skill for 2 minutes. Outdoor use only. Craftable with metal resources. |
| Hitching Post | 20.0 | Camping 100 | Unlimited | Place in your home to stable pets. Costs 30 gold per pet per real-world week from bank. |

## Bedroll Details

- Must be used outside of towns and safe areas
- Cannot use if enemies are within 20 tiles
- Only the owner benefits from the healing effect
- Heals both HP and Stamina while you remain within 3 tiles
- Requires your character to not be starving (Hunger > 4, Thirst > 4)
- After 100 seconds the bedroll automatically rolls back up into your pack

## Campfire Details

- Created by using Kindling
- Cannot be used on boats or in space areas
- No enemies can be within 20 tiles
- Heals **all** nearby players (within 3 tiles), not just the creator
- Burns for 60 seconds, then begins extinguishing (30 sec), then goes out
- Requires Hunger > 4 and Thirst > 4 to benefit

## Camping Tent Details

- At less than 90 Camping: outdoor use only
- At 90+ Camping: usable in dungeons (creates a "Dungeon Room" safe space)
- At 66+ Camping: access to an upgraded tent interior
- Others can follow you in if a standing tent is placed on the ground (visible for ~30 seconds)
- Anyone can stay as long as they want; they return to where they entered when they leave

## Small Tent Details

- Wilderness only (main overworld regions)
- Creates a physical protective barrier around you
- Blessed (not lost on death)
- Cannot be set up on boats, during combat, or in non-wilderness areas

## Spyglass Details

- Crafted from metal resources (Iron default)
- Higher-tier metals provide bonus tracking skill and longer duration
- Outdoor use only (wilderness, outdoor, village regions)
- Cannot stack the buff — must wait for current effect to expire

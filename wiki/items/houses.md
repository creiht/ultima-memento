# Houses

Player housing in Ultima Memento is one of the server's most extensive systems, spanning placement, ownership, storage, customization, addons, lawn and shanty remodeling, tents, magic carpets, and a Monopoly-style town-house rental system.

Full documentation is maintained in the dedicated Housing section:

**[Housing Section](../housing/README.md)**

That section covers everything: house types and costs, custom foundations, ownership and access lists, lockdowns and secures, decay (disabled by default), vendors, addons and the Interior Decorator, lawn and shanty remodeling, doors and security, house components (sign, teleporter, moving crate, mailbox), tents, magic carpets, the Monopoly rental system, and all admin commands and Settings.cs flags.

This page exists for structural consistency with other Items category pages.

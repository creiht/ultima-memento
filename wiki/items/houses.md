# Houses

The Houses category contains 310 files covering player housing components, addons, and decorations. Player housing is one of the most extensive systems in Ultima Memento.

## Housing Basics

Players can place houses in the world using house placement tools. Houses serve as:
- Personal storage (lockdowns, secure containers)
- Vendor shops (player-run merchants)
- Crafting workshops
- Display areas for trophies and decorations
- Pet stabling (with Hitching Post)

## House Types

Houses range from small 7x7 structures to large castle-sized buildings. House designs include:
- Small houses (stone, wood, plaster, log cabin)
- Medium houses (two-story, workshops, villas)
- Large houses (towers, keeps, castles)
- Custom-designed houses

## Addons

Addons are multi-tile decorative or functional items placed inside houses:

### Functional Addons
| Addon | Function |
|-------|----------|
| Forge | Blacksmithing station |
| Anvil | Blacksmithing station |
| Spinning Wheel | Produces thread/yarn |
| Loom | Produces cloth |
| Flour Mill | Produces flour |
| Water Trough | Water source |
| Training Dummy | Weapon skill training |
| Archery Butte | Archery skill training |
| Pentagram / Abattoir | Necromancy focus |

### Decorative Addons
- Beds (various styles)
- Tables and chairs
- Bookshelves and display cases
- Rugs and carpets (including bearskin rugs)
- Wall hangings and banners
- Fountains and garden features
- Fireplaces and chimneys
- Suits of armor (decorative)
- Mounted fish/animal trophies
- Flower arrangements
- Stone and wood architectural elements

## House Security

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Lockdowns | Items locked to the house floor |
| Secure Containers | Locked storage accessible by authorized players |
| Access Lists | Co-owner, Friend, Ban lists |
| Private/Public Toggle | Control house accessibility |
| Vendor Contracts | Rent vendor spots |

## How to Obtain

- **House Deeds**: Purchase from architects or use house placement tool
- **Addon Deeds**: Crafted via Carpentry, Tinkering, or Blacksmithing; or purchased from vendors
- **Decorations**: Crafted, purchased, or looted throughout the world

# Weapons Guide

This guide contains full stat tables for all weapon types, including damage, speed, strength requirements, and special moves.

## Stat Legend

- **Min/Max Dmg**: Base damage range
- **Speed**: Attack speed (lower = faster)
- **STR Req**: Minimum Strength to equip
- **Skill**: Required combat skill

## Weapon Enhancement

Weapons can be improved using:
- [Sharpening Stones](sharpening.md) — for bladed weapons (Blacksmithing)
- [Bow Strings](sharpening.md) — for ranged weapons (Bowcraft)
- [Weighting Stones](sharpening.md) — for blunt weapons (Blacksmithing)

## Material Bonuses

Higher-tier crafting materials provide:
- Bonus damage
- Bonus durability
- Elemental damage conversion
- Additional properties (luck, hit effects, etc.)

## Weapon Abilities

Every weapon has up to 5 special abilities:

| Ability | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Armor Ignore | Bypasses target's armor |
| Bleed Attack | Causes damage over time |
| Concussion Blow | Reduces target's intelligence |
| Crushing Blow | Extra damage hit |
| Death Blow | Bonus vs. near-death targets |
| Defense Mastery | Lowers target's defense |
| Disarm | Forces target to drop weapon |
| Dismount | Knocks riders off mounts |
| Double Shot | Fires two projectiles |
| Double Strike | Hits twice in one swing |
| Elemental Strike | Deals full elemental damage |
| Force of Nature | Nature-based special hit |
| Mortal Strike | Prevents healing temporarily |
| Moving Shot | Fire while moving |
| Paralyzing Blow | Temporarily paralyzes target |
| Riding Swipe | Attack while mounted |
| Stunning Strike | Stuns the target |
| Zap Dex/Int/Mana Strike | Drains target stats |

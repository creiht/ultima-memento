# Potions

Potions are consumable items crafted via the **Alchemy** skill. They provide healing, stat boosts, offensive effects, and utility. Potions come in standard bottles; in sci-fi areas they appear as pill bottles.

## Standard Potions

| Potion | Effect | Tier |
|--------|--------|------|
| Lesser Heal Potion | Restores a small amount of HP | 1 |
| Heal Potion | Restores a moderate amount of HP | 2 |
| Greater Heal Potion | Restores a large amount of HP | 3 |

## Elixirs

Elixirs are enhanced potions with longer-lasting or more powerful effects than standard potions.

## Mixtures & Alchemic Items

Throwable alchemic concoctions:

| Mixture | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Alchemic Slime | Creates a slime creature |
| Liquid Fire | Fire damage pool |
| Liquid Goo | Sticky slowing effect |
| Liquid Ice | Cold damage pool |
| Liquid Pain | Direct damage |
| Liquid Rot | Poison damage pool |
| Mixture Slime / Fire / Ice / Diseased / Radiated | Creates elemental slime variants |

## Canopic Jars

Special containers used in advanced alchemy:
- **Empty Canopic Jar**: Used to collect special ingredients
- **Canopic Jar**: Filled jar with special properties

## Brew Cauldron / Potion Cauldron

Crafting stations for mass-producing potions. Place near your alchemy workspace for batch brewing.

## Evil Skull

A special alchemic item with dark magic properties.

## Crafting

Potions are crafted using the **Alchemy** skill at a heat source with the appropriate reagents. Higher skill unlocks more potent recipes. The Venom Sack item is a special poison source dropped by venomous creatures.

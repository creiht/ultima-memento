# Magical Items

The Magical category is the largest item category (843 files), encompassing reagents, scrolls, spell components, enchanted equipment, and magical consumables. This page provides an overview grouped by type.

## Reagents

Reagents are consumed when casting spells. Standard magic reagents include:

- Black Pearl, Blood Moss, Garlic, Ginseng, Mandrake Root, Nightshade, Spider's Silk, Sulfurous Ash

Additional reagent types exist for necromancy, druidism, and other magic schools.

## Spell Scrolls

Scrolls contain a single casting of a spell and can be used by anyone, regardless of skill level (though success may vary). Scrolls exist for all spell circles across multiple magic schools:

- **Magery Scrolls**: Circles 1-8
- **Necromancy Scrolls**: Dark magic spells
- **Chivalry Scrolls**: Knight/paladin spells
- **Other Schools**: Various expanded magic systems

## Enchanted Equipment

Many items in this category are base classes and systems for magical item generation:

- **Magical Armor**: Randomly generated armor with magical properties
- **Magical Weapons**: Randomly generated weapons with bonuses
- **Magical Clothing**: Clothes with stat/skill bonuses
- **Artifacts**: Unique named items with powerful fixed properties — see [artifacts/](artifacts/README.md) for the full catalogue (354 items across 12 categories)

## Wands & Staves

See [Trinkets](trinkets.md) for magic wands. Wizard Staves and Wands appear in both the Weapons and Magical categories.

## Magical Consumables

- **Runes**: Recall/Gate rune stones for teleportation
- **Moonstones**: Used for moongate travel
- **Spell Books**: Contains learned spells for a magic school
- **Scrolls of Power**: Increase skill caps (see [Books](books.md))

## Enchantment System

Items can be enchanted to cast spells when used. The enchantment is set via the `MagicSpell` property and provides charges that deplete with use.

## Slayer Properties

Magical weapons and instruments can have Slayer properties that deal bonus damage to specific creature types (e.g., Dragon Slayer, Undead Slayer, Demon Slayer, etc.).

## How to Obtain

- **Crafting**: Inscription for scrolls, Tinkering for some magical items
- **Loot**: Monsters drop magical equipment scaled to their difficulty
- **Treasure Chests**: Dungeon and treasure map chests contain magical loot
- **Merchants**: Some basic magical items available from vendors
- **Quests**: Specific artifacts come from quest chains and boss encounters

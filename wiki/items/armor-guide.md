# Armor Guide

This guide contains full resistance tables for all armor types and information about material bonuses.

## Resistance Legend

- **Phys**: Physical resistance
- **Fire**: Fire resistance
- **Cold**: Cold resistance
- **Pois**: Poison resistance
- **Enrg**: Energy resistance
- **STR**: Minimum Strength required to equip

## Crafting Tips

1. **Exceptional Quality**: Crafting armor at exceptional quality provides +15% bonus to all base resistances
2. **Material Choice**: Choose material based on what resistances you need most
3. **Runic Hammers/Kits**: Special crafting tools that add random magical properties
4. **Arms Lore**: Higher Arms Lore skill when crafting yields better results
5. **Repair**: Armor degrades with use; repair with crafting tools or repair deeds

## Choosing Armor

| Priority | Recommendation |
|----------|---------------|
| Physical tank | Plate armor with Valorite |
| Balanced mage | Leather armor (low STR, allows meditation) |
| Stealth build | Leather or Studded (allows Stealth skill) |
| Archer | Medium armor (Studded/Ranger) for mobility |
| Crafter | Any — prioritize utility |

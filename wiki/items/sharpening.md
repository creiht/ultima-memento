# Sharpening Stones, Bow Strings & Weighting Stones

These consumable items enhance weapons by adding damage increase, elemental damage, or consecration. Each type targets a specific weapon category.

## Sharpening Stones (Bladed Weapons)

Used on swords, knives, axes, and spears. Requires **Blacksmithing** skill.

| Item | Max Damage Bonus | Skill Required | Uses | How to Obtain |
|------|-----------------|----------------|------|---------------|
| Rough Sharpening Stone | +60% DI | 60 Blacksmithing | 5 | Crafting, loot |
| Heavy Sharpening Stone | +65% DI | 80 Blacksmithing | 5 | Crafting, loot |
| Dense Sharpening Stone | +70% DI | 100 Blacksmithing | 5 | Crafting, loot |
| Consecrated Sharpening Stone | Consecrates weapon (4 hrs) | 100 Blacksmithing + 80 Knightship | 5 | Rare loot |
| Elemental Sharpening Stone | Sets all elements to 20% each | 100 Blacksmithing + 100 Magery | 1 | Rare loot |

## How Enhancement Works

1. **Damage Increase**: Each use attempts to add a random bonus (based on your skill level). Higher skill = better chance of larger bonus. The bonus is capped at the item's Max Damage Bonus (counting exceptional quality's +15% DI).
2. **Consecration**: Applies the Consecrate Weapon effect for 4 hours, causing the weapon to deal damage of the type the target is most vulnerable to.
3. **Elemental**: Sets the weapon's damage split to 20% Physical, 20% Fire, 20% Cold, 20% Poison, 20% Energy.

## Notes

- All enhancement items must be in your backpack to use
- The weapon being enhanced must also be in your backpack
- Items are consumed after all uses are spent
- Damage bonus from the stone is random — you may fail and add nothing on a given use
- A weapon cannot exceed the maximum damage increase cap regardless of how many stones are used

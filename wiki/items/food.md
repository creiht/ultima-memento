# Food

Food items restore hunger and thirst, which are essential for benefiting from camping rest effects and general survival. Food is obtained through farming, cooking, fishing, and purchasing from vendors.

## Food Categories

### Baked Goods
Bread, rolls, muffins, and other baked items. Crafted via **Cooking** skill.

### Fruits
Apples, bananas, grapes, lemons, limes, peaches, pears, and other fruits. Found on trees, purchased from vendors, or farmed.

### Vegetables
Cabbage, carrots, corn, lettuce, onions, pumpkins, tomatoes, turnips, and watermelons. Grown via farming or purchased.

### Asian Food
Eastern-inspired cuisine items.

### Bowls
Soup bowls and stew preparations.

### Cooked Foods
Items that require cooking at a heat source (campfire, oven, etc.).

## Beverages

Beverages quench thirst and come in various containers:
- **Pitchers**: Water, milk, ale, wine, cider, liquor
- **Bottles**: Wine, ale, liquor
- **Glasses/Goblets**: Individual servings

## Hunger & Thirst System

- Characters have separate **Hunger** and **Thirst** values
- Eating food restores Hunger; drinking beverages restores Thirst
- Both must be above 4 to benefit from campfire and bedroll healing
- Starvation does not kill you but prevents rest-based healing

## Cooking

The **Cooking** skill allows you to combine raw ingredients into prepared meals at a heat source. Higher skill unlocks more complex recipes with better hunger restoration.

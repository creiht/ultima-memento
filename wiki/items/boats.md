# Boats

Boats allow players to travel the seas, engage in naval activities, trade cargo, and hunt pirates. Ships are placed using boat deeds and can be dry-docked back into deed form.

## Ship Types

| Ship | Size | Speed Bonus | Hold Distance | How to Obtain |
|------|------|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| Tiny Boat | Tiny | 0 | — | Crafting (Carpentry) |
| Small Ship | Small | 0 | 4 | Crafting, purchase |
| Small Dragon Ship | Small | 0 | 4 | Crafting, purchase |
| Medium Ship | Medium | +1 | 4 | Crafting, purchase |
| Medium Dragon Ship | Medium | +1 | 4 | Crafting, purchase |
| Large Ship | Large | +2 | 5 | Crafting, purchase |
| Large Dragon Ship | Large | +2 | 5 | Crafting, purchase |

## Galleons (NPC/Special)

Galleons are large multi-mast vessels. They have a hold distance of 8 and are significantly larger than regular ships.

| Galleon | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| Barbarian Galleon | Large vessel with barbarian theme |
| Royal Galleon | Large vessel with royal theme |
| Pirate Galleon | Large vessel, pirate-themed |
| Orcish Galleon | Large vessel, orcish-themed |

## Boat Equipment & Accessories

| Item | Weight | Description | How to Obtain |
|------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Grappling Hook | 3.0 | Target a crew member to board their ship. Range: 20 tiles. | Crafting, purchase |
| Docking Lantern | 2.0 | Helps launch or dock ships. Provides Circle300 light. Can be secured in houses. | Crafting, purchase |
| Boat Stain | 2.0 | Stains a docked ship to the standard color. Target the ship deed in your pack. | Crafting, purchase |
| Boat Model | 1.0 | Decorative house addon of a miniature boat. | Crafting |
| Cargo | 50.0 | Trade goods found on ships. Can be delivered to merchants for gold and karma. | Sea trading, pirate loot |
| Pirate Bounty | 1.0 | Bounty contract for a pirate. Turn in to town guards for 1000-3000 gold reward. | Pirate encounters |

## Cargo Trading

Cargo crates are found during sea encounters and trade runs. Each cargo has:
- **Value**: Gold reward when delivered
- **Karma**: Reputation gained/lost
- **Origin**: Where the cargo came from
- **Contents**: What materials are inside

## Navigation Commands

Ships are controlled via the Tiller Man. Common commands:
- **Forward/Back/Left/Right** — directional movement
- **Stop** — halt the ship
- **Anchor/Raise Anchor** — toggle anchoring
- **Turn Left/Right** — rotate the ship

## Tips

- Larger ships are faster but require more resources to build
- Dragon ships have decorative dragon figureheads but are otherwise identical to their regular counterparts
- Ships can be renamed via the Tiller Man
- Docking lanterns help with launching and docking operations
- Keep a grappling hook handy for boarding enemy vessels during pirate encounters

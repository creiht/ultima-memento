# Quivers

Quivers are wearable containers designed to hold ammunition for ranged weapons. They reduce the weight of stored arrows or bolts and are worn in the cloak slot.

## Base Properties

All quivers share these base stats:

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Weight | 2.0 stones |
| Capacity | 500 ammunition |
| Max Items | 1 type at a time |
| Max Weight | 50 stones |
| Equip Slot | Cloak |
| Accepted Ammo | Arrows, Bolts, Harpoon Ropes, Throwing Weapons |

## Quiver Types

| Item | Weight Reduction | Notes | How to Obtain |
|------|-----------------|-------|---------------|
| Quiver | 50% | Standard quiver | Crafting (Bowcraft), loot |
| Elven Quiver | 50% | Elven-styled variant | Crafting, loot |

## Features

- **Weight Reduction**: Quivers reduce the effective weight of stored ammunition by the listed percentage (50%).
- **Single Ammo Type**: A quiver can only hold one type of ammunition at a time. Once you place arrows, you cannot add bolts until the arrows are removed.
- **Quality**: Quivers can be crafted at different quality levels, and may receive random magical properties.
- **Damage Increase**: Quivers can potentially provide a damage increase bonus to ranged attacks.
- **Lower Ammo Cost**: Some quivers may reduce ammunition consumption.
- **Craftable**: Quivers can be crafted via the Bowcraft skill and benefit from material bonuses.

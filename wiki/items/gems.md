# Gems

Gems are precious stones found throughout the world. They are stackable, lightweight, and primarily used as crafting components for jewelry and as trade goods.

## Gem Types

|-----|--------|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Amber | 0.1 | Yes | Circle150 | Mining, loot, merchants |
| Amethyst | 0.1 | Yes | Circle150 | Mining, loot, merchants |
| Citrine | 0.1 | Yes | Circle150 | Mining, loot, merchants |

## Special Gem Items

| Item | Weight | Notes | How to Obtain |
|------|--------|-------|---------------|
| Crystals | ~0.007 | Can be exchanged for gold (5 gold per crystal) when double-clicked from your bank box | Loot, mining |
| Large Crystal | 20.0 | Decorative item, provides Circle225 light | Loot, rare drops |

## Uses

- **Jewelry Crafting**: Gems are used in Tinkering to craft rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings, and circlets. The gem type determines the appearance of the jewelry.
- **Trade Goods**: Gems can be sold to merchants for gold.
- **Light Source**: All gems emit a small light (Circle150) when on the ground.
- **Currency Conversion**: Crystals can be converted to gold at a rate of 5 gold per crystal by double-clicking them in your bank box.

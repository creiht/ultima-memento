# Artifact Weapons

Weapon artifacts are unique named weapons with fixed enchantments. Most are obtainable via the [Sage Artifact Quest](../../../quests/); a handful come from specific sources.

See [README](README.md) for acquisition details and artifact level explanations.

## Special Effects

Items marked * have a special double-click effect or notable tooltip:

- **Blade of the Cimmerian**: Conan's Lost Sword
- **Dagger of Venom**: Dripping With Venom
- **Excalibur**: King Arthur's Lost Sword
- **Hammer of Thor**: (Casts Lightning)
- **Merlin's Mystical Staff**: (Calls Dragons)
- **Rod Of Resurrection**: Unique double-click effect — use in-game to discover.
- **Staff of the Serpent**: (Summons Snakes)
- **Stormbringer**: Elric's Lost Sword
- **Sword of Shattered Hopes**: Glass Sword
- **Vampire Killer**: Simon Belmonts's Whip

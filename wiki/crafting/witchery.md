# Witchery

Witchery (Witch Brewing) creates dark brews and concoctions for necromantic purposes.

- **Skill**: Forensics (primary), Necromancy (secondary)
- **Tool**: Witch's Cauldron
- **Primary Resources**: Witch Reagents (from undead/dark creatures), Standard Reagents, Jars

## Brews

All brews require both Forensics and Necromancy skill, plus an empty jar.

# Tailoring

Tailoring creates cloth clothing, hats, robes, and accessories from fabric.

- **Skill**: Tailoring
- **Tool**: Sewing Kit
- **Primary Resource**: Fabric
- **Supports**: Repair, Breakdown, Enhancement

## Fabric Types

Regular, Furry, Wooly, Silk, Haunted, Arctic, Pyre, Venomous, Mysterious, Vile, Divine, Fiendish

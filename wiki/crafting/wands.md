# Wands

Wand Crafting creates magical wands that cast spells. Requires Inscription skill, a smithing hammer, and an anvil/forge.

- **Skill**: Inscription
- **Tool**: Wand Crafting Book
- **Primary Resources**: Metal Ingots (tiered by circle), Spell Scrolls, Arcane Gems
- **Facility Required**: Anvil and Forge, Smith Hammer in pack

## How It Works

Each wand corresponds to one of the 64 Magery spells (8 circles x 8 spells). The required metal tier, scroll count, and skill increase with each circle.

## Requirements by Circle

| Circle | Min Inscription | Ingot Type | Ingots | Scrolls | Arcane Gems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First | 60.0 | Dull Copper | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| Second | 70.0 | Shadow Iron | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Third | 80.0 | Copper | 4 | 5 | 1 |
| Fourth | 90.0 | Bronze | 4 | 8 | 1 |
| Fifth | 100.0 | Gold | 4 | 12 | 1 |
| Sixth | 105.0 | Agapite | 4 | 17 | 1 |
| Seventh | 110.0 | Verite | 4 | 23 | 1 |
| Eighth | 115.0 | Valorite | 4 | 30 | 1 |

Each wand requires the corresponding spell scroll(s) plus 4 of the appropriate metal ingot and 1 Arcane Gem.

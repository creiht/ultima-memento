# Mining

Mining extracts ore, granite, sand, and gemstones from the earth.

- **Skill**: Mining
- **Tool**: Pickaxe or Spade
- **Range**: Must be within 2 tiles of a mountain, cave wall, or sand tile

## How It Works

- Target a mountain or cave wall to mine ore and stone
- Target sand tiles (beaches) to mine sand for Glassblowing
- Resource banks are 8x8 tiles and respawn every 10-20 minutes
- Each bank holds 5-17 ore units
- Higher Mining skill unlocks rarer ore types
- At 100+ Mining with Stone Mining learned, you have a 10% chance per swing to get granite instead of ore

## Ore Types & Vein Distribution

| Ore Type | Min Skill | Vein Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Iron | 0.0 | 45% |
| Dull Copper | 65.0 | 16% |
| Shadow Iron | 70.0 | 11% |
| Copper | 75.0 | 8% |
| Bronze | 80.0 | 6% |
| Gold | 85.0 | 5% |
| Agapite | 90.0 | 4% |
| Verite | 95.0 | 3% |
| Valorite | 99.0 | 2% |

Rich Veins (found in special locations) can produce additional rare ore types: Nepturite, Obsidian, Steel, Brass, Mithril, Xormite, and Dwarven.

## Bonus Resources

At 90+ Mining skill, you have a small chance (0.1% each) to find gemstones while mining:
- Amber, Amethyst, Citrine, Diamond, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, Star Sapphire, Tourmaline

## Sand Mining

- Requires 100.0 Mining skill
- Mine sand from beach tiles
- Sand is used in Glassblowing
- Banks hold 6-12 sand units

## Processing Ore

Ore must be smelted at a forge to produce ingots, which are then used in Blacksmithy, Tinkering, and other metal-working professions.

## Granite

Granite is used in Masonry. Each granite corresponds to an ore type (Iron Granite, Dull Copper Granite, etc.).

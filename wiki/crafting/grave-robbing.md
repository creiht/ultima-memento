# Grave Robbing

Grave Robbing uses the Forensics skill to excavate graves for bones, reagents, and relics.

- **Skill**: Forensics
- **Tool**: Grave Shovel
- **Range**: Must be within 1 tile of a grave
- **Restrictions**: Cannot be mounted or polymorphed

## How It Works

- Use a grave shovel on a grave, tombstone, or similar tile
- Resource banks are small (1x1), holding 1-3 items
- Graves take a long time to respawn: 50-70 minutes

## What You Can Find

### Body Parts & Bones (always available)
| Item | Chance |
|---|---|
| Bones | 5% |
| Left/Right Arm | 5% each |
| Torso | 5% |
| Left/Right Leg | 5% each |
| Single Bone | 5% |
| Rib Cage | 5% |
| Bone Pile | 5% |

### Reagents & Items (skill-dependent)
| Item | Min Skill | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Grave Dust | 20.0 | 13% |
| Fertile Dirt | 20.0 | 12% |
| Cure Potion | 50.0 | 10% |
| Brimstone | 50.0 | 10% |
| Heal Scroll | 50.0 | 10% |

### Bonus Relics (60+ skill)
At higher levels, you have a chance to find relics (~2% each):
- Relic Vases, Art, Grave Items, Weapons, Armor, Jewelry, Instruments, Scrolls, Reagents, Orbs, Books

## Uses

- **Bones** (raw) are the **Brittle** tier starting material for [Bonecrafting](bonecrafting.md). Bring them to a bonecrafting tool to carve armor.
- **Grave Dust** is a Necromancy reagent
- **Fertile Dirt** is useful for gardening
- **Relics** are valuable collectibles

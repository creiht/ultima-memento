# Bulk Orders (Bulk Crafting)

The Bulk Craft system allows players to queue up multiple crafting actions to be performed automatically over time.

## How It Works

1. Open a crafting menu with the appropriate tool
2. Select an item to craft
3. Choose the "Bulk Craft" option to specify a quantity
4. The system will automatically craft that item repeatedly at timed intervals

## Features

- **Automatic Crafting**: Once started, crafting continues automatically without further player input
- **Timer-Based**: Each craft action occurs on a timer, simulating the time it takes to craft manually
- **Auto-Stop Conditions**: Bulk crafting stops automatically if:
  - You run out of materials
  - Your tool breaks
  - You disconnect from the server
  - Your character dies
  - You manually cancel
- **Cancellation**: You can cancel bulk crafting at any time through the crafting gump

## Tips

- Make sure you have enough materials in your pack before starting
- Ensure your tool has sufficient uses remaining
- Bulk crafting is great for producing large quantities of ammunition, potions, or ingredients
- You remain in place while bulk crafting, so find a safe spot

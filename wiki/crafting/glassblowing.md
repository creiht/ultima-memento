# Glassblowing

Glassblowing creates bottles, jars, flasks, and decorative glass items. Requires Alchemy 100+ and the Glassblowing skill (learned from a book).

- **Skill**: Alchemy (minimum 100.0 base)
- **Tool**: Blowpipe
- **Primary Resource**: Sand (mined from beaches)
- **Facility Required**: Forge
- **Prerequisite**: Must learn Glassblowing from a special book

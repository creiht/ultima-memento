# Global Shoppes

The Global Shoppe system provides specialized NPC trade shops that players can interact with for buying and selling goods.

## Available Shoppes

| Shoppe | Description |
|---|---|
| Alchemist Shoppe | Buy and sell potions, reagents, and alchemy supplies |
| Baker Shoppe | Buy and sell baked goods and cooking ingredients |
| Blacksmith Shoppe | Buy and sell metal weapons, armor, and smithing supplies |
| Bowyer Shoppe | Buy and sell bows, crossbows, ammunition, and fletching supplies |
| Carpentry Shoppe | Buy and sell wooden items, furniture, and carpentry supplies |
| Cartography Shoppe | Buy and sell maps, scrolls, and cartography supplies |
| Herbalist Shoppe | Buy and sell herbs, exotic reagents, and druidic supplies |
| Librarian Shoppe | Buy and sell books, scrolls, and inscription supplies |
| Mortician Shoppe | Buy and sell bones, grave goods, and necromancy supplies |
| Tailor Shoppe | Buy and sell cloth items, leather goods, and tailoring supplies |
| Tinker Shoppe | Buy and sell tools, parts, jewelry, and tinkering supplies |

## How It Works

- Each shoppe type is associated with specific categories of items
- Shoppes use an order-based system where customers can place orders
- The system calculates fair prices based on item type and quality
- Shoppes have reward systems for regular customers

## Shoppe Features

- **Item Caching**: The system caches available items for efficient browsing
- **Order System**: Players can place custom orders for specific items
- **Reward Types**: Various reward tiers for frequent shoppers
- **Commands**: Use the `[shoppe` command system to interact with shoppes

## Tips

- Shoppes are a good way to sell crafted goods without waiting for individual buyers
- Check multiple shoppes for the best prices
- Higher quality and exceptional items fetch better prices
- Rare material items are especially valuable

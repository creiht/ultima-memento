# Shelves

Shelves crafting creates storage furniture including armoires, cabinets, chests, crates, dressers, and decorative shelving.

- **Skill**: Carpentry
- **Tool**: Woodworking Tools
- **Primary Resource**: Wood (Boards)
- **Supports**: Breakdown

## Wood Types

Regular, Ash, Cherry, Ebony, Golden Oak, Hickory, Mahogany, Oak, Pine, Ghost, Rosewood, Walnut, Petrified, Driftwood, Elven

## Categories

### Armoires (17 types, 30-40 boards, 51.5-90.0 skill)
Fancy, standard, red, elegant, maple, cherry, bamboo, and colored varieties.

### Cabinets (15 types, 25-35 boards, 51.5-90.0 skill)
Tall, short, book, dish, storage, locker, and fancy varieties.

### Chests (9 types, 10-40 boards, 21.0-90.0 skill)
Wooden box, wooden chest, plain, ornate, gilded, foot locker, finished, coffin (requires Forensics 75), and casket.

### Crates (27+ types, 8-20 boards, 10.0-47.3 skill)
Small, medium, large, and themed crates (adventurer, alchemy, arms, baker, blacksmith, bowyer, carpenter, healer, jeweler, librarian, musician, necromancer, provisioner, sailor, tailor, tavern, tinker, treasure, wizardry).

### Dressers (24 types, 25-35 boards, 31.5 skill)
Standard, open, nightstand, and colored varieties.

### Shelves - Bamboo (21 types, 35 boards, 41.5 skill)
Themed shelves for different professions.

### Shelves - Redwood (21 types, 35 boards, 41.5 skill)
Same themes in redwood style.

### Shelves - Rustic (21 types, 35 boards, 41.5 skill)
Same themes in rustic style.

### Shelves - Stained (34 types, 25-35 boards, 41.5 skill)
Dyeable stained shelves for many professions.

### Shelves - Wood (35+ types, 25-35 boards, 41.5 skill)
Classic wooden shelves with many theme options.

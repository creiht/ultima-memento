# Masonry

Masonry (Stone Working) creates stone furniture, statues, containers, and tombstones. Requires Carpentry 100+ and the Masonry skill (learned from a book).

- **Skill**: Carpentry (minimum 100.0 base)
- **Tool**: Mallet and Chisel
- **Primary Resource**: Granite
- **Prerequisite**: Must learn Masonry from a special book
- **Supports**: Breakdown

## Granite Types

Standard, Dull Copper, Shadow Iron, Copper, Bronze, Gold, Agapite, Verite, Valorite, Nepturite, Obsidian, Steel, Brass, Mithril, Xormite, Dwarven

## Decorations & Furniture

Vases, amphoras, sculptures (Buddhist, Ming, Yuan, Qin), urns, chairs, benches, tables, columns, pedestals, pillars, steps, and blocks (42.5-95.0 skill, 2-20 granite).

## Statues

- **Small Statues** (55.0 skill, 4 granite): Angel, Dragon, Gargoyle, Man, Noble, Pegasus, Skull, Woman
- **Medium Statues** (65.0 skill, 8-10 granite): ~20 varieties including Adventurer, Amazon, Fighter, Gryphon, Medusa, Sphinx, Wizard
- **Large Statues** (75.0 skill, 16 granite): Dwarf, God, Horse Rider, Lion, Minotaur, Pegasus, Woman Warrior
- **Huge Statues** (85.0 skill, 24 granite): Angel, Daemon, Lion, Warrior, Wise Man, Wolf, Woman
- **Giant Statues** (95.0 skill, 32 granite): Gate Guardian, Guardian, Warrior

## Tombstones

20 varieties, all at 45.0 skill using 3 granite each.

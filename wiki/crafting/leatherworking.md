# Leatherworking

Leatherworking creates leather armor, footwear, bags, and accessories from leather hides.

- **Skill**: Tailoring
- **Tool**: Tanning Tools
- **Primary Resource**: Leather
- **Supports**: Repair, Breakdown, Enhancement

## Leather Types

Regular, Horned, Barbed, Necrotic, Volcanic, Frozen, Spined, Goliath, Draconic, Hellish, Dinosaur, Alien

# Lumberjacking

Lumberjacking harvests logs from trees, which are then processed into boards for use in Carpentry, Bow Fletching, and Shelves.

- **Skill**: Lumberjacking
- **Tool**: Hatchet (must be equipped)
- **Range**: Must be within 2 tiles of a tree

## How It Works

- Equip a hatchet and target a tree
- Resource banks are per-tree (1x1 tiles)
- Each tree holds 4-6 logs and respawns every 20-30 minutes
- Higher skill unlocks rarer wood types

## Wood Types & Vein Distribution

| Wood Type | Min Skill | Vein Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | 0.0 | 30% |
| Ash | 55.0 | 16% |
| Cherry | 60.0 | 10% |
| Ebony | 65.0 | 9% |
| Golden Oak | 70.0 | 8% |
| Hickory | 75.0 | 7% |
| Mahogany | 80.0 | 6% |
| Oak | 85.0 | 5% |
| Pine | 90.0 | 4% |
| Rosewood | 95.0 | 3% |
| Walnut | 100.0 | 2% |

Rich Lumberjacking (found in special locations) can produce additional rare wood types: Ghost, Petrified, Driftwood, and Elven.

## Bonus Resources

While lumberjacking, you may also find:
- **Bark Fragments** (90+ skill, ~10% chance) - used in Cartography and crafting blank scrolls
- **Mushrooms** (100+ skill, ~1% chance)

## Processing Logs

Logs must be converted to boards (using a saw mill or by using an axe on them) before they can be used in crafting.

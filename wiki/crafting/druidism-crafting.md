# Druidism (Herbalism Crafting)

Druidic Herbalism creates nature-themed brews and concoctions. This is distinct from the Druidism spell school.

- **Skill**: Druidism (primary), Veterinary (secondary)
- **Tool**: Druid's Cauldron
- **Primary Resources**: Exotic Reagents, Standard Reagents, Jars

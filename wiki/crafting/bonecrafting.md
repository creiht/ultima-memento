# Bonecrafting

Bonecrafting creates armor from skeletal remains of various creatures.

- **Skill**: Forensics
- **Tool**: Bonecrafting tool
- **Primary Resource**: Skeletal bones (Brittle and higher tiers)
- **Supports**: Repair, Breakdown, Enhancement

## Bone Types

Brittle, Drow, Orc, Reptile, Ogre, Troll, Gargoyle, Minotaur, Lycan, Shark, Colossal, Mystical, Vampire, Lich, Sphinx, Devil, Draco, Xeno

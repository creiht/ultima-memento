# Miscellaneous Spells

Miscellaneous spells are special magical abilities triggered by items rather than traditional spellbooks. They use [Magery](../skills/magery.md) (or [Necromancy](../skills/necromancy.md)) skill and are often bound to specific magical artifacts or items.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Magery](../skills/magery.md) or [Necromancy](../skills/necromancy.md) (whichever is higher)
- **Access:** Typically triggered through special items

## Spell List

| Spell | Skill Used | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Attack Spells | Magery/Necromancy | Casts a random offensive spell based on caster skill and mana; damage type and visual varies by creature type or random |
| Identify | Magery | Identifies properties of items |
| Summon Snakes | Magery | Summons venomous snakes to fight |
| Summon Dragon | Magery | Summons a dragon ally |
| Thor Lightning | Magery | Calls down a powerful bolt of divine lightning |
| Summon Skeleton | Necromancy | Raises a skeleton servant |
| Travel | Magery | Teleports to a destination |

## Attack Spells - Wizardry Types

The Attack Spells system selects random offensive spell effects based on the creature's affinity or location. There are 62 different visual/damage combinations grouped into these categories:

| Wizardry Type | Typical Spells |
|---------------|---------------|
| Air | Lightning, electricity, energy bolts |
| Cold | Ice bolts, hail storms, frost strikes, avalanches |
| Fire | Fireballs, flame strikes, meteors, ignite, steam |
| Main | Harm, mind blast, energy bolt, explosions |
| Nature | Acid, poison, vines, leaves, insects, weeds |
| Necro | Dark void, pain spike, strangle, wither, poison |
| Storm | Lightning storms, electrical bolts |
| Water | Water splashes, water globes, ice storms |

## Notes

- Attack Spells dynamically scale damage and mana cost based on caster's skill level (circles 1-8).
- The spell chosen depends on the caster's location (e.g., fire dungeons favor fire spells) and creature type.
- These spells are primarily used by NPCs/creatures but can be triggered via special items.

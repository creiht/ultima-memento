# Necromancy

Necromancy is the dark art of manipulating death, spirits, and the undead. It draws upon [Necromancy](../skills/necromancy.md) for casting and [Spiritualism](../skills/spiritualism.md) for damage calculations. Necromancy spells reduce Karma when cast.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Necromancy](../skills/necromancy.md)
- **Damage Skill:** [Spiritualism](../skills/spiritualism.md)
- **Spellbook:** Necromancer Spellbook
- **Reagents:** Required (or Arcane Gem charges as substitute)

## How to Learn

Obtain necromancy spell scrolls and inscribe them into a Necromancer Spellbook. Adventures in crypts and undead areas often yield these scrolls.

## Summon Familiar

The **Summon Familiar** spell (Kal Xen Bal, 17 mana, reagents: Bat Wing / Grave Dust / Daemon Blood) opens a selection gump after a 2-second cast. You choose from five familiars; each requires a minimum in both Necromancy **and** [Spiritualism](../skills/spiritualism.md). Only one familiar can be active at a time. Familiars gain bonus damage and hit points scaled from your combined Necromancy + Spiritualism total, and inherit your MagicResist skill.

See [Familiars — System 1](../systems/familiars.md#system-1--necromancy-summon-familiar) for the full familiar stats, special abilities, and mechanics.

## Notes

- Necromancy spells are **not** affected by Faster Casting items (pre-SE).
- Several spells are **transformation spells** (Horrific Beast, Lich Form, Vampiric Embrace, Wraith Form) -- only one transformation can be active at a time.
- Casting necromancy spells awards **negative Karma**.

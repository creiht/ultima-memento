# Ninjitsu

Ninjitsu encompasses the shadow arts of the ninja -- stealth, deception, shapeshifting, and lethal precision strikes. It uses [Ninjitsu](../skills/ninjitsu.md) skill.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Ninjitsu](../skills/ninjitsu.md)
- **Spellbook:** Book of Ninjitsu

## How to Learn

Acquire a Book of Ninjitsu. Abilities become available as your Ninjitsu skill increases.

## Spell List

| Ability | Mana | Min Skill | Effect |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|
| Focus Attack | - | 30.0 | Next attack deals bonus damage based on skill |
| Death Strike | - | 85.0 | Applies a delayed lethal strike that deals damage when target moves/acts |
| Animal Form | 0 | 10.0 | Transforms the caster into an animal with unique bonuses |
| Ki Attack | - | 80.0 | Charging attack that deals bonus damage based on distance traveled |
| Surprise Attack | - | 60.0 | Next attack from stealth reduces the target's defense |
| Backstab | - | 40.0 | Deals extra damage when attacking from hiding |
| Shadow Jump | - | 50.0 | Teleports to a location while remaining hidden |
| Mirror Image | - | 20.0 | Creates mirror copies to absorb attacks |

## Notes

- Ninjitsu abilities do **not** reveal the caster when cast.
- Ninjitsu is **not** affected by Faster Casting items.
- Animal Form has no mana cost and offers various creature forms with different benefits.

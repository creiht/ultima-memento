## General Casting Mechanics

- Most spells require sufficient **mana** to cast.
- Many schools require a **spellbook** or special item equipped.
- Spells can be cast via the spellbook UI or by typing `[CommandName` in the chat.
- **Spell Bars** provide quick-access toolbars -- see [Spell Bars](spell-bars.md) for setup details.
- Some schools (Knight, Death Knight, Holy Man) require **Tithing Points**, **Souls**, or **Piety** as an additional resource.
- **Karma** affects access to certain schools: Jedi and Knight require positive Karma; Syth and Death Knight require negative Karma.
- **Armor** can interfere with casting in some schools (notably Elementalism and Magery).

# Bard Songs

Bard Songs are magical performances that provide buffs to allies and debuffs to enemies. They require [Musicianship](../skills/musicianship.md) skill and a musical instrument. Song power scales with the combined values of Musicianship, Provocation, Discordance, and Peacemaking.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Musicianship](../skills/musicianship.md)
- **Additional Skills:** [Provocation](../skills/provocation.md), [Discordance](../skills/discordance.md), [Peacemaking](../skills/peacemaking.md) (increase power)
- **Instrument:** Must have a musical instrument selected (lute, harp, etc.)
- **Spellbook:** Song Book

## How to Learn

Acquire a Song Book and obtain song scrolls to inscribe. Select an instrument using the instrument selection prompt before casting.

## Notes

- Instruments have limited uses; they break after the last use.
- Songs are **not** affected by Faster Casting.
- Carols are group resistance buffs; Threnodies are debuffs targeting a single enemy.
- Etudes boost stats; Paeon and Ballad boost regeneration.

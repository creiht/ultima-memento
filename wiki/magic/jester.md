# Jester

The Jester school is a unique set of comedic trick abilities. Jesters use [Begging](../skills/begging.md) as their primary casting skill and [Psychology](../skills/psychology.md) for damage scaling. Abilities require "pranks" as a resource, stored in a Bag of Tricks.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Begging](../skills/begging.md)
- **Damage Skill:** [Psychology](../skills/psychology.md)
- **Class:** Must be identified as a Jester
- **Resource:** Pranks (stored in Bag of Tricks)

## How to Learn

Become a Jester and acquire a Bag of Tricks. Pranks are accumulated through performing and can be spent on abilities.

## Notes

- Buff/debuff power scales with Begging + Psychology combination.
- Damage, duration, and skill checks all scale with both Begging and Psychology.
- Poison level scales with Psychology (up to level 5).
- Cast recovery is fast (2 base).
- Failed casts produce comical sound effects.

# Death Knight

The Death Knight is a dark warrior who draws power from trapped souls and negative Karma. Their abilities combine combat prowess with demonic magic, using [Knightship](../skills/knightship.md) skill. Death Knights are the evil counterpart to Paladins.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Knightship](../skills/knightship.md)
- **Karma:** Must be **negative** (< 0)
- **Resource:** Trapped Souls (stored in a Soul Lantern)
- **Stamina & Mana:** Both required to cast
- **Spellbook:** Death Knight Spell Book

## How to Learn

Obtain Death Skulls containing Death Knight spell knowledge and inscribe them into a Death Knight Spell Book. Collect souls in your Soul Lantern by slaying enemies to fuel your abilities.

## Notes

- Casting Death Knight spells reduces Karma further (negative Karma awards).
- Power scales with **negative Karma** and **Knightship skill**.
- Soul Lantern must contain enough trapped souls for abilities that require them.
- Lower Reagent Cost can reduce soul cost.

# Magery

Magery is the traditional school of arcane magic. It is divided into 8 circles of increasing power, with 8 spells per circle (64 total). Spells require reagents to cast and are learned via scrolls inscribed into a spellbook.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Magery](../skills/magery.md)
- **Spellbook:** Standard Spellbook (equipped or in backpack)
- **Reagents:** Each spell consumes specific reagents (or Arcane Gem charges)

## How to Learn

Purchase or find spell scrolls and use them on your spellbook to inscribe the spell. Scrolls can also be cast directly at a lower skill requirement (circle - 2).

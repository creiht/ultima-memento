# Bushido

Bushido is the way of the samurai warrior. These combat techniques focus on weapon mastery, defensive stances, and powerful strikes. They use [Bushido](../skills/bushido.md) skill and mana.

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Bushido](../skills/bushido.md)
- **Spellbook:** Book of Bushido

## How to Learn

Acquire a Book of Bushido. Samurai abilities are automatically available once the required Bushido skill level is reached.

## Spell List

| Ability | Mana | Min Skill | Effect |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|
| Honorable Execution | 0 | 25.0 | On next hit, deals bonus damage; if it kills the target, gain hit point regeneration |
| Confidence | 10 | 25.0 | Regenerates health and provides immunity to hit-interrupting your spells |
| Evasion | 10 | 60.0 | Grants a chance to parry incoming attacks with a weapon |
| Counter Attack | 5 | 40.0 | Automatically counter-attacks the next enemy that strikes you |
| Lightning Strike | 5 | 50.0 | Next attack has a greatly increased chance to hit |
| Momentum Strike | 10 | 70.0 | If the attack kills the target, a second strike hits a nearby foe |

## Notes

- Bushido abilities are **not** affected by Faster Casting items.
- Only one Bushido stance can be active at a time (Evasion, Confidence, Counter Attack).
- Cast recovery base is 7 seconds.

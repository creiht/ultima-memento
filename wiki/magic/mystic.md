# Mystic (Monk)

The Mystic school represents the martial arts and psionic disciplines of the monk. Abilities use [Fist Fighting](../skills/fist-fighting.md) skill and Tithing Points. Monks must wear a special Mystic Monk Robe, no armor, no weapons (pugilist gloves allowed), and have 100+ in both [Focus](../skills/focus.md) and [Meditation](../skills/meditation.md).

## Requirements

- **Skill:** [Fist Fighting](../skills/fist-fighting.md)
- **Restrictions:** No weapons (pugilist gloves only), no armor, no shields (spell channeling exception)
- **Equipment:** Mystic Monk Robe (mandatory, created via Create Robe ability)
- **Base Skills:** [Focus](../skills/focus.md) 100+ and [Meditation](../skills/meditation.md) 100+
- **Resource:** Tithing Points

## How to Learn

Find Mystic Scrolls and inscribe them into a Mystic Spell Book. Create your Monk Robe first -- it is required for all other abilities.

## Notes

- The Monk Robe can be enchanted: single-click it and select "Enchant" to spend attribute points.
- Only one Monk Robe can exist at a time; creating a new one destroys the old one.
- Wind Runner is disabled in certain restricted areas (dungeons, caves, indoors).
- Psychic Wall does not work against unusual magics like necromancy.

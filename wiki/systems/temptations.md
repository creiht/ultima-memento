# Temptations

Temptations are **permanent, irreversible gameplay modifiers** that grant powerful benefits at the cost of significant drawbacks. Once you accept a temptation, it can never be removed.

## How to Access

- **Command**: `[Temptations`
- **Location requirement**: Temptations can only be changed while in **the Gypsy Forest**. Outside of this area, the gump is read-only.
- **Avatar characters**: Must first unlock the "Power Overwhelming" upgrade in the Avatar Shop before temptations become available.

## Available Temptations

| Temptation | Benefit | Drawback |
|------------|---------|----------|
| **Puzzle Master** | You learn how to solve puzzle boxes | Reduced skill cap bonuses for Fugitives (+200) and Titan of Ether quest (+200); magical attributes from monster racial bonuses now act as minimums (resist/stat bonuses still stack) |
| **I Can Take It** | You deal 10 % more damage | You take 20 % more damage |
| **Strongest Avenger** | You learn how to wear pants | Enemies cast spells faster |
| **It's Dangerous to Go Alone! Take This.** (Deathwish) | Faster stat and skill gain; you receive an old sword | You may **never** be resurrected (permanent death) |

## Important Notes

- **Permanent**: Once accepted, temptations cannot be reversed. Choose carefully.
- **Multiple selections**: You can select more than one temptation.
- **Deathwish**: This is the most extreme temptation — it enables permadeath. Your character cannot be resurrected by any means. Not available for Avatar characters (who already have permadeath). You receive an Old Sword talisman as a keepsake.
- **Viewing others**: You can view another player's temptations using the `[Temptations` command and targeting them, but you cannot modify their choices.
- Achievements are tracked separately for permadeath (Deathwish) characters.

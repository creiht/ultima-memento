# Loot Tables

This section documents every loot system in Ultima Memento — the rules that govern what items creatures drop, what treasure chests contain, and what special rewards champions and paragons provide.

[Back to Systems Index](../README.md)

---

## System Overview

Loot is generated by six independent subsystems that can all fire on the same creature death or container opening:

```
Creature death
├── A. Body-drop packs   (LootPack.cs)      → monster Pack tiers
├── B. Special drops     (DropRelic.cs)     → conditional relics, eggs, dust …
├── C. Paragon bonus     (Paragon.cs)       → extra pack + possible artifact
└── D. Champion rewards  (BaseChampion.cs)  → power scrolls, decorative items

Chest / container opening
└── E. Container loot    (ContainerFunctions.cs + LootPack.cs) → treasure pack tiers

Fishing
└── F. Fishing loot      (Harvest/Fishing/…) → nets, bottles, sea relics
```

All magic items generated by packs A–E pass through `BaseRunicTool.ApplyAttributes()` with tier-specific intensity ranges and two optional bonus layers (see [Magic Item Pools](magic-item-pools.md)).

## Quick Reference: Pack Tiers

### Monster Packs (creature body drops)

| Internal Name | Accessor | Gold Dice | Magic Items | Artifacts |
|---------------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| MonsterPoor | `LootPack.Poor` | 2d10+20 | ~1% chance (poor) | — |
| MonsterMeager | `LootPack.Meager` | 4d10+40 | ~20% (meager) | — |
| MonsterAverage | `LootPack.Average` | 8d10+100 | ~33% ×2 (average) | — |
| MonsterRich | `LootPack.Rich` | 15d10+225 | ~76% ×2 (rich) | 1% semi-arty |
| MonsterFilthyRich | `LootPack.FilthyRich` | 3d100+400 | ~80% ×3 (filthy rich) | 1% semi + 0.5% arty |
| MonsterUltraRich | `LootPack.UltraRich` | 6d100+600 | 100% ×6 (ultra) | 2% semi + 1% arty |
| MonsterMegaRich | `LootPack.SuperBoss` / `MegaRich` | 10d100+800 | 100% ×10 (ultra) | 4% semi + 2% arty |

### Treasure Packs (chest fills)

| Internal Name | Accessor | Magic Items | Gems | Scrolls | Potions |
|---------------|----------|-------------|------|---------|---------|
| TreasurePoor | `LootPack.TPoor` | 10% poor | 20% | 5% low | 5% low |
| TreasureMeager | `LootPack.TMeager` | 20% meager | 40% | 10% low | 10% low |
| TreasureAverage | `LootPack.TAverage` | 33% average | 60% | 20% med | 20% med |
| TreasureRich | `LootPack.TRich` | 76% rich | 70% | 30% high | 30% high |
| TreasureFilthyRich | `LootPack.TFilthyRich` | 80% filthy rich | 80% | 40% high | 40% high |
| TreasureUltraRich | `LootPack.TUltraRich` | 100% ultra | 90% | 50% high | 50% high |
| TreasureMegaRich | `LootPack.TMegaRich` | 100% ultra ×2 | 100% | 60% high | 60% high |

## Currency Conversion

Gold dice output is **never kept as raw gold**. `LootPackChange.MakeCoins()` converts the total gold amount based on the creature's current region:

| Zone | Currency |
|------|---------|
| Normal world | Mix of Copper, Silver, Gold coins, Gold Nuggets, Gemstones (proportional to amount) |
| Sci-fi regions (Spaceship) | DDXormite at ×⅓ ratio |
| Mines of Morinia (20% chance) | Crystals at ×⅕ ratio |
| Underworld | DDJewels at ×½ ratio |

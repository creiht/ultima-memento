# Gardening

Gardening lets you grow plants from seeds in **plant bowls**. Plants progress through multiple growth stages, produce seeds for planting, and some yield special resources.

## Getting Started

1. Obtain a **seed** (found as loot, purchased from vendors, or harvested from existing plants).
2. Use a **plant bowl** (or bowl of dirt) — plant the seed in it.
3. Place the plant in a valid location (your backpack or bank box).
4. Tend to the plant daily.

## Growth Cycle

- Plants check for growth approximately every **23 hours**.
- A plant must be at **Healthy** or **Vibrant** health to grow.
- Plants progress through 9 growth stages before reaching maturity (full-grown plant).
- **Fertile dirt** gives a 10 % chance of double growth (advancing two stages in one cycle).

## Plant Health

| Status | Health % |
|--------|----------|
| Dying | Below 33 % |
| Wilted | 33–65 % |
| Healthy | 66–99 % |
| Vibrant | 100 % |

## Tending Your Plants

### Water
Keep water at level **2** (soft soil). Too much or too little water damages the plant.

| Water Level | Soil Status |
|-------------|-------------|
| 0–1 | Hard (too dry) |
| 2 | Soft (ideal) |
| 3 | Squishy (too wet) |
| 4 | Sopping wet (too wet) |

### Potions
Pour potions on plants to treat or buff them (max 2 doses each):

| Potion | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Poison** | Cures infestation — but excess poison damages the plant |
| **Cure** | Cures fungus — but excess cure causes disease |
| **Heal** | Cures poison and disease, then heals remaining HP |
| **Strength** | Reduces chance of infestation and fungus |

### Maladies

| Problem | Cause | Treatment |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Infestation | Random chance, increased by flowering plants and bright hues | Poison potion |
| Fungus | Random chance, increased by excess water | Cure potion |
| Poison | Excess poison potions | Heal potion |
| Disease | Excess cure potions | Heal potion |

## Pollination and Seeds

- Once a plant reaches full growth, it can be **pollinated**.
- Use the pollination tool to cross-pollinate two full-grown crossable plants, creating hybrid seeds.
- Each plant can produce up to **8 seeds**.
- Plants that self-pollinate (not cross-pollinated) produce seeds of their own type and color.

## Plant Types

Plants include flowers, grasses, palms, ferns, cacti, bonsai trees, and more:

**Crossable (can produce new varieties):** Campion Flowers, Poppies, Snowdrops, Bulrushes, Lilies, Pampas Grass, Rushes, Elephant Ear Plant, Fern, Ponytail Palm, Small Palm, Century Plant, Water Plant, Snake Plant, Prickly Pear Cactus, Barrel Cactus, Tribarrel Cactus

**Bonsai (rarity tiers):** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Exceptional, Exotic (green and pink variants)

**Peculiar (non-crossable):** Cactus, Flax Flowers, Foxglove Flowers, Hops, Orfluer Flowers, Cypress, Hedge, Juniper Bush, Snowdrop Patch, Cattails, Poppy Patch, Spider Tree, Water Lily, Sugar Canes

| Ponytail Palm | Bright Red | Red Leaves |
| Century Plant | Bright Red | Red Leaves |
| Poppies | Bright Orange | Orange Petals |
| Bulrushes | Bright Orange | Orange Petals |
| Pampas Grass | Bright Orange | Orange Petals |

| Barrel Cactus | Bright Green | Green Thorns |

## Decorative Mode

Full-grown plants can be set to **decorative mode**, which stops all growth and maintenance but also stops seed/resource production.

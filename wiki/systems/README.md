# Game Systems

An index of the major game systems in Ultima Memento.

| System | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| [Avatar Leveling](avatar-leveling.md) | Permadeath progression system with coins, a reward shop, starter templates, and persistent unlocks across lives |
| [Achievements](achievements.md) | Track exploration, hunting, harvesting, crafting, skill progress, and feats of strength for reward points |
| [Champion Spawns](champion-spawns.md) | Large-scale group encounters with escalating waves of enemies culminating in a powerful champion boss |
| [Pet Leveling (Jako)](pet-leveling.md) | Level up your tamed pets by spending trait points on hit points, mana, stamina, and elemental resistances |
| [Gardening](gardening.md) | Grow plants from seeds in bowls of dirt, tend them with water and potions, and harvest seeds and resources |
| [Apiculture (Beekeeping)](apiculture.md) | Raise bee colonies in hives to produce honey and beeswax, then craft candles, sculptures, and more |
| [Taxidermy](taxidermy.md) | Mount creature heads and stuff animal corpses as decorative trophies |
| [Temptations](temptations.md) | Permanent gameplay modifiers that grant powerful benefits at the cost of significant drawbacks |
| [Puzzle Chests](puzzle-chests.md) | Solve a color-coded cylinder puzzle to unlock guarded treasure chests |

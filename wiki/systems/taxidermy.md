# Taxidermy

Taxidermy lets you preserve creature corpses as decorative **mounted trophy heads** and **stuffed animals** to display in your home.

## Tools

There are two taxidermy tools:

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Trophy Base** | Mount a creature's head as a wall trophy |
| **Stuffing Basket** | Stuff a full animal corpse as a standing trophy |

Both tools are obtained through crafting or vendor purchases. Double-click either tool for lore information, or single-click (context menu) to use.

## How to Create Trophies

1. Kill a supported creature.
2. Use the tool (context menu → Use) and target the **corpse**.
3. The corpse is consumed, and a trophy item is placed in your backpack.
4. Each corpse can only be claimed once — once a taxidermist visits it, it cannot be used again.

## Mounted Trophy Heads (Trophy Base)

Target a corpse with the Trophy Base to create a wall-mountable head. Supported creatures include:

- **Dragons & Wyrms**: Ancient Wyrm, Dragon King, Shadow Wyrm, Elder Dragon, Volcanic Dragon, Crystal Dragon, and many more
- **Giants**: Stone Giant, Ice Giant, Lava Giant, Mountain Giant, Storm Giant, Cloud Giant, Fire Giant, Frost Giant, and more
- **Demons & Gargoyles**: Daemon, Balron, Fire Gargoyle, Stone Gargoyle, Shadow Demon, Archfiend, and more
- **Bears**: Brown Bear, Grizzly Bear, Polar Bear, Black Bear, Cave Bear, Dire Bear, Sabretooth Bear
- **Big Cats**: Tiger, White Tiger, Sabretooth Tiger, Lion, Panther, Snow Lion
- **Reptiles & Dinosaurs**: Lizardman variants, Sakleth variants, Drake, Meglasaur, Stegosaurus, Tyranasaur
- **Humanoids**: Cyclops, Ogre, Ogre Lord, Ettin, Hill Giant, Minotaur, Goblin, Ratman, Orc variants, Troll variants, Bugbear
- **Mythical**: Unicorn, Dark Unicorn, Nightmare, Griffon, Hippogriff, Wyvern, Hydra, Cerberus, Chimera, Dragon Turtle
- **Fey**: Pixie, Sprite, Faerie
- **Fish**: Regular fish and big fish can also be mounted

Trophies record the **location** where the creature was slain and the **name of the killer**. Many trophy heads can be flipped (double-click in your backpack) to face a different direction.

## Stuffed Animals (Stuffing Basket)

Target a corpse with the Stuffing Basket to create a full standing trophy. Supported creatures include:

- **Bears**: Brown Bear, Black Bear, Grizzly Bear, Polar Bear, Dire Bear, Great Bear, Kodiak Bear, Cave Bear, Sabretooth Bear, Elder variants, Sabreclaw Cub
- **Big Cats**: Lion, Snow Lion
- **Lizardfolk**: Lizardman, Grathek, Reptalar, Sakleth, Sleestax and their variants
- **Snakes**: Death Adder, Jade Serpent, Giant Adder, Titanoboa, Lava Snake, Ice Serpent, Giant Serpent, and more
- **Tigers**: Regular Tiger and Sabretooth Tiger produce rug deeds; White Tiger produces a white tiger rug deed

Stuffed animals also record location and killer information.

## Tips

- Act quickly — corpses decay, so taxidermy should be done soon after the kill.
- Trophies make excellent house decorations and conversation pieces.
- Not all creatures can be turned into trophies — if you target an unsupported corpse, you'll receive a message that it cannot be mounted/stuffed.

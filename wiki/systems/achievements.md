# Achievements

Achievements (also called **Feats**) track your progress across many activities. Completing them awards **achievement points** and sometimes item rewards.

## Commands

- `[feats` or `[achievements` — Target yourself or another player to open the Achievements gump.

## Achievement Categories

### Exploration

Discover new lands, towns, dungeons, and points of interest. Exploration achievements are organized into subcategories:

- **Facets** — Discover each of the game's worlds (Sosaria, Umber Veil, Ambrosia, Lodoria, Serpent Island, Isles of Dread, Savaged Empire, Kuldar, Underworld, Luna, Skara Brae, Atlantis).
- **Towns** — Visit specific towns within each facet. Requires discovering the parent facet first. Coordinates are shown after completion.
- **Dungeons** — Enter specific dungeons. Requires discovering the parent facet first. Some dungeons also mention a relic quest item.
- **Points of Interest** — Discover notable landmarks.

### Hunting (Hunter)

Kill specific creature types. Tracked automatically when you land the killing blow. Examples include feats of strength such as slaying Exodus or Jormungandr.

### Resource Gathering (Harvest)

Harvest resources through gathering skills:

| Skill | Milestones |
|-------|-----------|
| Lumberjacking | 1 → 100 → 1,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 logs |
| Mining | 1 → 100 → 1,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 ore |

### Crafting (ItemCrafted)

Craft items through the various trade skills: Alchemy, Blacksmith, Bowcraft, Carpentry, Cooking, Inscription, Tailoring, and Tinkering.

### Character Development (SkillProgress)

Reach skill milestones as you train your character.

### Feats of Strength (FeatsTarget)

Special one-time accomplishments, such as:

| Achievement | Description | Points |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| Domo Arigato | Destroy Exodus, the demonic automaton | 5 |
| Like Thor, but better | Defeat Jormungandr, the Serpent of Midgard | 5 |

## How Achievements Work

- **Progress**: Each achievement has a completion total. Progress increments automatically as you perform the tracked action.
- **Prerequisites**: Some achievements require a prerequisite to be completed first (e.g., discovering a facet before its towns become available).
- **Hidden achievements**: Some achievements hide their title or description until completed.
- **Rewards**: Each achievement awards a set number of points (typically 5). Some also grant item rewards placed in your backpack (or bank if overloaded).
- **Notification**: When you earn an achievement, you receive an on-screen message and a sound effect.

## Viewing Achievements

The achievement gump displays all categories, your progress, and total points. You can also view another player's achievements by targeting them with the `[feats` command.

Achievement data is tracked per account. Avatar characters and hardcore (permadeath) characters have separate achievement progress.

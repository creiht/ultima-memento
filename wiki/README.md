# Ultima Memento — Player Wiki

Welcome to the Ultima Memento player wiki! Ultima Memento is a custom Ultima Online server built on the RunUO emulator, featuring 19 magic systems, 22 crafting professions, dozens of skills, over 24 quest types, and a vast multi-facet world to explore. Whether you are a veteran of Britannia or setting foot in the land for the first time, this wiki will help you understand the many systems and secrets the world has to offer.
